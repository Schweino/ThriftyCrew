Makes 14 servings · ~591 cal · 61g protein · 53g carbs · 20g fat per serving.

Ingredients

  • 93/7 ground beef. 2,712 g (~6 lb)
  • Pasta sauce (Great Value Traditional). 2,040 g (~72 oz, about three 24-oz jars)
  • Penne pasta (Barilla Protein). 784 g (2 boxes)
  • Cottage cheese. 678 g (~24 oz, about 1½ tubs)

Estimated Cost

Rough estimate only. Prices swing by region and by what's on sale.

  • 93/7 ground beef (~6 lb): ~$36
  • Pasta sauce (3 jars): ~$5
  • Penne pasta (2 boxes): ~$5
  • Cottage cheese (~24 oz): ~$4
  • Batch total: ~$50 at 14 servings → about $3.55 per serving

These are generally non-sale prices. You can go even cheaper by catching ground beef on sale and using store-brand for everything else.

That's roughly $3.55 a meal. Versus the $25 we'd drop on a single takeout dinner.

Shop Smart

  • Watch for ground-beef sales and stock the freezer. Lean 93/7 is one of the pricier proteins, so grab the big family packs when your store marks it down and freeze what you won't use that week. And if you spot a great deal on 80/20, go for it. Just know that swapping to 80/20 adds about 100 calories and 12g of fat per serving, and drops protein by ~4g.
  • Store-brand pasta sauce is the move. Great Value Traditional costs a fraction of the name brands. Punch it up with garlic, Italian seasoning, or a pinch of red pepper.
  • Pasta is flexible. Barilla Protein penne for the macros above, but store-brand penne is dirt cheap. Your macros will shift a little if you swap.
  • Buy cottage cheese in bulk and compare price per ounce. Costco/Sam's usually wins, but scan your local weekly ads too.

Make it

1. Weigh your empty pot first and write down the number. You'll need it to portion at the end. 2. Boil the penne until al dente; drain and set aside. 3. Brown the ground beef and drain the excess fat. Season to your liking. Salt, pepper, garlic, Italian seasoning, a little red pepper if you want heat. 4. Make the sauce: blend the pasta sauce together with the cottage cheese, all 3 jars of sauce with about 3 cups of cottage cheese, until smooth and creamy, then warm it through. Depending on your blender's size, you may need two batches (e.g., 2 jars + 2 cups, then the last jar + 1 cup). 5. Combine the beef, sauce, and penne back in the pot and stir to coat.

Portion it

Set the full pot on your large scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you noted in Step 1, and divide by 14. Scoop that amount into each glass container. Every meal lands right at ~591 cal / 61g protein.
