Makes 14 servings · ~794 cal · 50g protein · 99g carbs · 30g fat per serving (chicken + fries).

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast. 2,267 g (~5 lb)
  • BBQ sauce (sugar-free). 1,536 g (~54 oz, about three 18-oz bottles)
  • Crinkle-cut fries (Great Value). 5,950 g (~13 lb)
  • Buns. Your choice (not counted in the macros above). We use Bettergoods Keto buns, but get whatever's cost-effective for you.

Estimated Cost

Rough estimate only. Prices swing by region and by what's on sale.

  • Chicken breast (~5 lb): ~$15
  • BBQ sauce (3 bottles): ~$11
  • Crinkle-cut fries (~13 lb): ~$19
  • Batch total: ~$45 at 14 servings → about $3.20 per serving

These are generally non-sale prices. You can go even cheaper by catching chicken on sale and using store-brand for everything else.

That's roughly $3.20 a meal. Versus the $25 we'd drop on a single takeout dinner.

Shop Smart

  • Buy chicken on sale and freeze it. Same play as always. Bulk family packs when it's marked down.
  • Buy the big fries bag. Great Value crinkle-cut runs about $7.50 for the 5-lb bag vs $2.97 for 2 lb. The 5-lb is the better deal per pound, and fries keep great in the freezer.
  • Sugar-free vs regular BBQ. Your call. Sugar-free keeps calories down; regular store-brand is cheaper.
  • Compare price per ounce on everything.

Make it

1. Season the chicken breast to your liking. 2. Add the seasoned chicken to your slow cooker and cook on HIGH for 4 hours. 3. Shred the cooked chicken and drain off any excess liquid. 4. Mix the BBQ sauce into the shredded chicken until evenly coated. 5. Bake the crinkle-cut fries per the bag. 6. Pile the BBQ chicken onto a bun and serve with the fries.

Portion it

Weigh the finished BBQ chicken and divide by 14 for your per-sammie meat portion (it freezes and reheats great). Bake the fries fresh when you eat. About 5 servings (~15 oz) per plate. Together that's the ~794-cal, 50g-protein meal above.
