Makes 14 servings · ~764 cal · 53g protein · 52g carbs · 32g fat · ~$3.70 per serving (at everyday cost).

A proper chile relleno means roasting and peeling each pepper, stuffing it by hand, dipping it in whipped egg batter, and frying it one at a time. That is a beautiful way to spend a Sunday and a terrible way to make fourteen servings. This takes the same parts, charred peppers, spiced meat, and an absurd amount of melted cheese, and builds them into a skillet. Each serving carries 53 grams of protein and 764 calories for about $3.70 (at everyday cost).

Ingredients

  • 93/7 Ground Turkey (Jennie-O): 5.25 lb (2380 g)
  • Green Bell Peppers: 7 (840 g)
  • Yellow Onion: 1.7 (192 g)
  • Garlic: 2 tbsp (18 g)
  • All-Purpose Flour (Great Value): 3.5 tbsp (28 g)
  • Chili Powder: 1 tbsp (9 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 1.25 tbsp (21 g)
  • Ground Cumin: 0.75 tbsp (4 g)
  • Chicken Broth (Swanson): 1 carton (840 g)
  • Mexican Cheese Blend (Great Value): 34.5 oz (980 g)
  • Rice (Member's Mark): 4.25 cups dry (770 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.5 tbsp (4 g)

    Shop Smart

    • Green bell peppers cost less than any other color and this recipe uses seven of them. If poblanos are close in price that week, grab them instead for a more authentic, slightly hotter result.
    • Cheese is the whole budget here. Compare 8 oz bags against blocks priced per pound, because at three pounds of cheese the difference adds up fast.
    • Char the peppers yourself under the broiler rather than buying jarred roasted peppers. Jarred costs several times as much and tastes like vinegar.
    • Ground turkey in the largest pack you can freeze is the other lever. Buy on sale and split it across recipes.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Halve and seed the green bell peppers, lay them cut side down on a sheet pan, and broil 8 to 10 minutes until the skins blister and blacken in patches.
    3. Cover the hot peppers with foil for 10 minutes to steam, then slip off the loose skins and cut them into wide strips. That char is the flavor of the whole dish.
    4. Brown the ground turkey in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high, then add the diced onion and cook 5 minutes until soft.
    5. Stir in the garlic, chili powder, ground cumin, salt, and black pepper and cook one minute, then sprinkle the flour over everything and stir for two minutes.
    6. Pour in the chicken broth and simmer 5 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the meat rather than pooling around it.
    7. Fold in the charred pepper strips and about half the Mexican cheese blend, then spread the mixture level in the skillet.
    8. Cover with the remaining cheese and bake at 375F for 20 minutes, then broil 2 to 3 minutes until the top browns and blisters. Rest 10 minutes before portioning.

    Portion It

    Set the skillet on the scale, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Cut wedges to that weight and lift them with the cheese layer intact. Each serving comes to 764 calories and 53 grams of protein.
