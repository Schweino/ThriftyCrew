**TITLE:**

Fajita Chicken Rice Bowl

**SUBTITLE:**

A high-protein, low-fat batch with a Tex-Mex kick — about two weeks of dinners from one cook session.

**BODY:**

**Makes 14 servings · ~541 cal · 49g protein · 69g carbs · 5g fat · ~$2.25 per serving.**

## Ingredients

- **Chicken breast (Member's Mark)** — 2,495 g (~5.5 lb, raw)
- **Seasoned black beans (Great Value)** — ~910 g (2 cans)
- **Jasmine rice (Member's Mark)** — 800 g dry (4 cups uncooked)
- **Fajita seasoning (Taco Bell)** — 6 packets
- **Green bell peppers** — ~715 g (6 medium)

That's about **$2.25 a bowl** — versus **$10 or more** for a single fast-casual fajita bowl.

## Shop Smart

- **Buy chicken when it's on sale, then freeze it.** Stores drop chicken to a sale price at random — we recently grabbed 50 lbs at $1.99/lb instead of our usual $2.99/lb and loaded the deep freezer. At ~5.5 lbs per batch, that one buy makes about 9 batches, or roughly 63 nights of dinner for two.
- **The seasoning is your priciest line per ounce.** Six Taco Bell packets run about $6. A store-brand fajita or taco seasoning is a fraction of that, and mixing your own from chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic, and onion powder costs pennies. Just know the macros shift a little if you swap.
- **Buy rice in the big bag.** A large bag of jasmine is far cheaper per pound than a small box. Compare the price per ounce — that's the number that tells you the real deal.
- **Beans by the case, peppers in season.** Canned beans are cheapest bought a case at a time, and bell peppers drop hard when they're in season or in a 3-count bag. Frozen pepper strips work too.

## Make it

> **Brad, confirm your real method here before we publish.** These steps are a reasonable draft, but I don't want to put words in your kitchen. Tell me how you actually cook it and I'll match it exactly.

1. **Weigh your empty pot first.** Put the largest pot you'll use to mix everything onto your large scale and write down its weight — you'll need that number at the end to portion accurately.
2. Cook the rice (4 cups dry jasmine plus water per the package) and set aside.
3. Slice the bell peppers into strips.
4. Cube and cook the chicken breast in a large skillet until done.
5. Add the fajita seasoning with a splash of water, toss in the peppers, and simmer until the peppers soften and everything is coated.
6. Warm the black beans through.
7. Combine the rice, seasoned chicken and peppers, and beans back in the big pot and stir.

## Portion it

Set the full pot on your large scale and subtract the empty-pot weight you noted in Step 1 — that's your total food weight. Divide it by 14, then scoop that amount into each glass container. Every bowl lands right at ~541 cal / 49g protein.
