      Adapted from Eating on a Dime.

      Makes 14 servings · ~565 cal · 60g protein · 55g carbs · 9g fat · ~$2.58 per serving.

      Orange, lime, and garlic give this pork that classic Cuban mojo punch, and it comes together with almost no hands-on time. Served over seasoned black beans, each bowl carries a big 60 grams of protein for about $2.74. Fourteen servings means your fridge is stocked and your wallet barely notices.

      Ingredients

      • Pork Tenderloin: 3,400 g (7.5 lb)
      • Seasoned Black Beans (Great Value): 1,200 g (4.6 cups)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 640 g (3.2 cups)
      • Orange Juice: 480 g (1.9 cups)
      • Lime Juice: 60 g (4 tbsp)
      • Garlic: 15 g (5 clove)
      • Ground Cumin: 8 g (4 tsp)

      Shop Smart

      • Canned seasoned black beans save you the spices and the soak time. They come pre-flavored, so you get more taste for less work.
      • A small carton of orange juice is plenty. You only need a splash, so use the rest for breakfast instead of letting it go to waste.
      • Fresh garlic beats the jarred stuff on flavor and price. One bulb costs under a dollar and lasts weeks.

      Make It

      1. Place the pork tenderloin in the slow cooker.
      2. Pour the orange juice and lime juice over the pork, then add the garlic and cumin.
      3. Cover and cook on low for 7 to 8 hours until fork-tender.
      4. Shred the pork and stir it back through the citrus juices.
      5. Warm the seasoned black beans and spoon them into your bowls as the base.
      6. Top the beans with the mojo pork and divide into containers.
