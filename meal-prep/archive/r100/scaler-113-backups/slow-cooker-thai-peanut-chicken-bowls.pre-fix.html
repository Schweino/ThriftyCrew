      Adapted from Slender Kitchen.

      Makes 14 servings · ~603 cal · 63g protein · 52g carbs · 15g fat · ~$2.36 per serving.

      Peanut sauce makes almost anything taste like a treat, and this Thai-inspired bowl is no exception. It brings 63 grams of protein and a creamy coconut-peanut sauce for about $2.55 a serving. Fourteen servings from one pot means you can eat like this all week and still come out ahead.

      Ingredients

      • Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast (Member's Mark): 3,400 g (7.5 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 800 g (4.0 cups)
      • Red Bell Pepper: 600 g (1.3 lb)
      • Peanut Butter: 170 g (170 g)
      • Coconut Milk (Thai Kitchen): 180 g (0.7 cups)
      • Chicken Broth (Swanson): 120 g (0.5 cups)
      • Soy Sauce: 60 g (60 g)
      • Honey (Great Value): 21 g (1 tbsp)
      • Garlic: 12 g (4 clove)

      Shop Smart

      • That jar of peanut butter powers the whole sauce for under a dollar's worth, so the pantry staple you already own is doing the heavy lifting.
      • Canned coconut milk is far cheaper than the carton kind and keeps for a year, so buy a few cans at once.
      • Red peppers cost the most of any veg here, so watch for the three-pack sale and buy them when they're marked down.

      Make It

      1. Add the chicken, peanut butter, coconut milk, chicken broth, soy sauce, honey, and garlic to the slow cooker and stir.
      2. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 7 hours until the chicken is tender and the sauce is glossy.
      3. Stir in the sliced red bell pepper for the last 45 minutes so it softens but holds its shape.
      4. Whisk the sauce smooth, then shred the chicken right back into it.
      5. Serve over white rice and spoon extra sauce on top.
