      Adapted from Eating Bird Food.

      Makes 14 servings · ~513 cal · 51g protein · 62g carbs · 5g fat · ~$2.06 per serving.

      This is a full week of buffalo chicken dinners built right in your slow cooker, tangy shredded chicken over rice with a little broccoli and melty cheddar. It hits that wing-night craving with real protein instead of a plate of empty fried stuff, and every gram of protein comes from lean chicken breast. A tray of buffalo wings runs you $12 a person at a bar, and this bowl feeds you the same flavor for a fraction of that.

      Ingredients

      • Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast (Member's Mark): 2,495 g (5.5 lb)
      • Ranch Seasoning Mix (Hidden Valley): 47 g (5 tbsp)
      • Buffalo Wing Sauce (Frank's RedHot): 240 g (16 tbsp)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 1,000 g (5.0 cups)
      • Broccoli Florets (Great Value): 595 g (7.0 cups)
      • Fat Free Cheddar (Great Value): 196 g (1.8 cups)

      Shop Smart

      • Buy chicken breast in the big family pack. The per-pound price on a 5 to 6 lb pack is almost always lower than small trays, and this recipe uses all of it.
      • Grab a store-brand ranch seasoning. It tastes the same in a slow cooker as the name brand and costs half as much. Buffalo sauce keeps for months, so watch for it on sale and stock up.
      • Use frozen broccoli if fresh is pricey that week. Frozen is picked at peak, costs less, and works perfectly here since you are cooking it down anyway.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot on a kitchen scale and write the number down. You will need it to portion evenly at the end.
      2. Put the chicken breast in the slow cooker, sprinkle on the ranch seasoning, and pour over the buffalo sauce. Cook on low for 6 to 8 hours or high for 3 to 4, until the chicken pulls apart with a fork.
      3. While that finishes, cook your rice per the package. This is your base and it stretches the whole batch.
      4. Roast or steam the broccoli florets until just tender and still bright green. Do not overcook it or it turns to mush by day three.
      5. Shred the chicken while it is still warm, right in the slow cooker, using two forks. Warm chicken shreds far easier than cold.
      6. Toss the shredded chicken back through its own cooking liquid so every bite stays juicy and coated.
      7. Add the cooked rice, broccoli, and the shredded cheddar to your weighed mixing pot and fold everything together.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide that number by 14. Scoop that exact weight into each of your 14 containers so every dinner carries the same hearty serving of protein.
