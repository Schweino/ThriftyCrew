      Shop Smart

      • Honey and hoisin do the heavy lifting here, so skip the bottled teriyaki sauce that charges extra for the same sweet-savory mix.
      • A bag of frozen broccoli is the cheapest veg in the recipe and keeps for weeks, so lean on it.
      • One red bell pepper adds color and crunch, but green peppers are cheaper if you want to shave a little more off the total.

      Make It

      1. Brown 4.9 pounds of 93/7 ground beef with the chopped onion, then drain.
      2. Steam the broccoli florets and sliced red bell pepper until crisp-tender.
      3. Whisk the soy sauce, hoisin, honey, beef broth, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and cornstarch into a glaze.
      4. Pour the glaze over the beef and simmer until it thickens and coats the meat.
      5. Serve the teriyaki beef and veggies over rice in each container.
