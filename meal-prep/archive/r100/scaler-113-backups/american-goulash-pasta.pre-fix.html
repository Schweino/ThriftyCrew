      Makes 14 servings · ~598 cal · 55g protein · 63g carbs · 16g fat · ~$3.02 per serving.

      Old-school American goulash, the humble one-pot dinner that fed families for generations because it stretched a dollar. This version packs 55 grams of protein per serving for about $3.44 and makes 14 bowls. Cheap, hearty, and honestly hard to beat for feeding a crowd.

      Ingredients

      • 93/7 Ground Beef (Member's Mark): 2,450 g (5.4 lb)
      • Penne Pasta (Barilla): 1,050 g (2.3 lb)
      • Yellow Onion: 300 g (1.9 cups)
      • Carrots: 200 g (200 g)
      • Crushed Tomatoes: 1,300 g (2.9 lb)
      • Tomato Paste (Great Value): 120 g (7 tbsp)
      • Beef Broth (Great Value): 700 g (2.9 cups)
      • Parmesan Cheese (Great Value): 80 g (0.7 cups)
      • Garlic: 20 g (7 clove)
      • Italian Seasoning: 6 g (6 tsp)

      Shop Smart

      • Crushed tomatoes in the large can are one of the cheapest ways to build a big pot of sauce, and this recipe leans right into that.
      • A pound of whole carrots costs less than pre-shredded and shreds in seconds. Small savings that add up across a batch.
      • Dried italian seasoning from a single jar covers this and a dozen other recipes, so buy it once and forget about it.

      Make It

      1. Brown 5.4 pounds of 93/7 ground beef with the chopped onion and shredded carrots, then drain.
      2. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, beef broth, garlic, and italian seasoning.
      3. Boil the penne separately until just tender, then drain.
      4. Simmer the sauce a few minutes so it thickens, then fold in the cooked pasta.
      5. Stir in the parmesan and portion it into containers.
