      Ingredients

      • Pork Loin: 3,200 g (7.1 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 750 g (3.8 cups)
      • Broccoli Florets (Great Value): 1,200 g (14.1 cups)
      • Brown Sugar: 120 g (120 g)
      • Soy Sauce: 120 g (120 g)
      • Olive Oil: 30 g (2 tbsp)
      • Toasted Sesame Seeds: 18 g (18 g)
      • Yellow Onion: 160 g (1.0 cup)

      Shop Smart

      • Brown sugar and soy sauce make a glaze for almost nothing. A bag of brown sugar lasts for months and you use just a little each time.
      • Toasted sesame seeds are a small touch that makes the bowl look finished. One jar goes a long way, so buy it once.
      • Lean on pork loin at around $2 a pound to keep this bowl in the dollar-something range where it belongs.

      Make It

      1. Rub the pork loin with a little olive oil and set it in the slow cooker with the sliced onion.
      2. Stir together the brown sugar and soy sauce, then pour it over the pork.
      3. Cover and cook on low for 7 to 8 hours until fork-tender.
      4. Shred the pork and stir it back through the glaze.
      5. Steam the broccoli florets until just tender.
      6. Serve over cooked white rice, sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds, and pack it up.
