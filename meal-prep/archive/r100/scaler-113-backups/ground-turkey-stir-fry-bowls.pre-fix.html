      A fast, saucy stir fry that skips the takeout price tag entirely. You get 51 grams of protein per bowl for about $3.42 a serving. Fourteen servings from one skillet, and it comes together in one go.

      • 93/7 Ground Turkey (Jennie-O): 3,300 g (7.3 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 900 g (4.5 cups)
      • Zucchini: 620 g (5.0 cups)
      • Green Bell Peppers: 360 g (3 medium pepper)
      • Teriyaki Sauce (Kikkoman): 180 g (10 tbsp)
      • Honey (Great Value): 42 g (2 tbsp)
      • Hot Sauce (Frank's RedHot): 30 g (6 tsp)
      • Olive Oil: 27 g (2 tbsp)

      Shop Smart

      • Buy the 8 lb ground turkey pack to get the best per-pound price on the biggest line item.
      • A single bottle of teriyaki sauce covers several batches, so it is not the budget-buster it looks like.
      • Fresh zucchini runs under a dollar apiece and bulks out the pan without much cost.

      Make It

      1. Heat a little olive oil in a large skillet and brown the ground turkey.
      2. Add the sliced zucchini and green peppers and stir fry until they are just tender.
      3. Stir in the minced garlic, teriyaki sauce, honey, and a dash of hot sauce.
      4. Let it bubble a couple minutes so the sauce coats everything and thickens slightly.
      5. Serve it hot over cooked white rice.
