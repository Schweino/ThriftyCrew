      Adapted from The Endless Meal.

      Makes 14 servings · ~576 cal · 56g protein · 54g carbs · 13g fat · ~$1.65 per serving.

      Gochujang brings sweet heat and a deep savory kick, and it turns cheap pork loin into something that tastes like takeout. Each bowl packs 56 grams of protein over rice and broccoli for just $1.78. Fourteen servings at that price is the kind of math that keeps money in your pocket.

      Ingredients

      • Pork Loin: 3,300 g (7.3 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 640 g (3.2 cups)
      • Broccoli Florets (Great Value): 900 g (10.6 cups)
      • Honey (Great Value): 130 g (6 tbsp)
      • Chicken Broth (Swanson): 240 g (1.0 cup)
      • Gochujang: 200 g (200 g)
      • Soy Sauce: 120 g (120 g)

      Shop Smart

      • Pork loin is your cheap workhorse. At around $2 a pound it costs half what tenderloin does and shreds up just as nicely.
      • A tub of gochujang looks pricey up front, but you use a little at a time. One tub flavors several batches, so the real cost per meal is tiny.
      • Grab frozen broccoli florets instead of fresh. They are cheaper, they never spoil on you, and they steam straight from the bag.

      Make It

      1. Set the pork loin in the slow cooker.
      2. Whisk the gochujang, soy sauce, honey, and chicken broth, then pour it over the pork.
      3. Cover and cook on low for 7 to 8 hours until the pork shreds easily.
      4. Shred the pork and stir it back into the sauce.
      5. Steam the broccoli florets until just tender and set aside.
      6. Serve the pork and broccoli over cooked white rice and portion out.
