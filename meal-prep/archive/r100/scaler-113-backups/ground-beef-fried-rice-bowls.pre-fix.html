      Adapted from With the Woodruffs.

      Makes 14 servings · ~593 cal · 46g protein · 66g carbs · 15g fat · ~$2.93 per serving.

      Fried rice built on ground beef, which is cheaper than takeout and loaded with more protein. Each bowl carries 46 grams for about $3.30, and the pan makes 14 servings. This is the perfect home for leftover rice, and it beats the delivery version every time.

      Ingredients

      • 93/7 Ground Beef (Member's Mark): 2,450 g (5.4 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 1,020 g (5.1 cups)
      • Frozen Green Peas: 400 g (400 g)
      • Carrots: 300 g (300 g)
      • Yellow Onion: 250 g (1.6 cups)
      • Soy Sauce: 180 g (180 g)
      • Sesame Oil: 30 g (30 g)
      • Garlic: 20 g (7 clove)
      • Ginger: 12 g (12 g)

      Shop Smart

      • Use day-old cold rice if you can. It fries up firmer and cheaper than any restaurant version and keeps this recipe from going gummy.
      • A bag of frozen peas and carrots costs next to nothing, never spoils, and cooks straight from the freezer.
      • Soy sauce and sesame oil are the only special buys, and both last months, so you are set for many batches to come.

      Make It

      1. Brown 5.4 pounds of 93/7 ground beef with the chopped onion, then drain.
      2. Toss in the peas and carrots and cook until they warm through.
      3. Add the cooked rice, ideally day-old and cold, so it fries up instead of turning mushy.
      4. Drizzle in the soy sauce and sesame oil with the garlic and ginger, stirring until everything is coated.
      5. Push it around the hot skillet a couple minutes to get that fried-rice edge, then portion out.
