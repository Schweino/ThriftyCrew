      Adapted from The Magical Slow Cooker.

      Makes 14 servings · ~586 cal · 54g protein · 61g carbs · 12g fat · ~$1.62 per serving.

      Sweet, garlicky, and dead simple, this one tastes like takeout for a fraction of the cost. Lean pork loin slow cooks in a honey soy sauce until it falls apart, then goes over rice with broccoli. It runs about a dollar seventy a serving and gives you 54 grams of protein across 14 bowls.

      Ingredients

      • Pork Loin: 3,050 g (6.7 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 820 g (4.1 cups)
      • Broccoli Florets (Great Value): 1,200 g (14.1 cups)
      • Honey (Great Value): 150 g (7 tbsp)
      • Soy Sauce: 180 g (180 g)
      • Garlic: 60 g (20 clove)

      Shop Smart

      • Pork loin at around two dollars a pound is your workhorse here. Skip the pre-cut pork chops and buy the whole loin to slice yourself.
      • Frozen broccoli is cheaper than fresh and never goes to waste. Keep a few bags in the freezer and pull them as you need them.
      • A one pound bag of rice cooks up into a mountain of servings. Buy the big bag of plain white rice instead of the seasoned pouches.

      Make It

      1. Place the pork loin in the slow cooker.
      2. Whisk together the honey, soy sauce, and minced garlic, then pour it over the pork.
      3. Cover and cook on low for 7 to 8 hours until the meat shreds easily.
      4. Steam the broccoli florets in the last few minutes so they stay bright and crisp.
      5. Cook the rice while the pork rests.
      6. Shred the pork, pile it over rice, add the broccoli, and drizzle the pan sauce on top.
