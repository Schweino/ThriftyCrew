      Adapted from Crockpots and Flip Flops.

      Makes 14 servings · ~576 cal · 58g protein · 64g carbs · 9g fat · ~$2.09 per serving.

      Kung Pao is one of those dishes people assume you have to order out, and that assumption costs them real money every week. This version leans on the slow cooker to do the work, delivering 58 grams of protein per bowl at about $2.34 a serving. Fourteen servings out of one pot means the math finally works in your favor.

      Ingredients

      • Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast (Member's Mark): 3,200 g (7.1 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 980 g (4.9 cups)
      • Green Bell Peppers: 300 g (3 medium pepper)
      • Zucchini: 300 g (2.4 cups)
      • Soy Sauce: 120 g (120 g)
      • Honey (Great Value): 63 g (3 tbsp)
      • Hoisin Sauce: 60 g (60 g)
      • Olive Oil: 30 g (2 tbsp)
      • Garlic: 9 g (3 clove)
      • Cornstarch: 16 g (16 g)

      Shop Smart

      • Green peppers and zucchini are cheapest in summer, so buy them in season and let the price, not the recipe, pick your veg.
      • Honey and hoisin both keep nearly forever, so stock the small jars once instead of buying fresh every time.
      • When chicken breast runs high, swap in bone-in thighs on sale and just pull the meat off after cooking.

      Make It

      1. Drop the chicken, soy sauce, honey, hoisin, olive oil, and garlic into the slow cooker and stir to coat.
      2. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 7 hours until the chicken is fall-apart tender.
      3. Add the diced green bell peppers and zucchini for the last 45 minutes so they keep a little bite.
      4. Whisk the cornstarch with cold water, stir it in, and let the sauce cook down and thicken for 20 minutes.
      5. Shred the chicken into the sauce and spoon it over hot white rice.
