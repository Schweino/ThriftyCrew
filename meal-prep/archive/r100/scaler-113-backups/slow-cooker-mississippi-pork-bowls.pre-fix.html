      Adapted from Salt & Lavender.

      Makes 14 servings · ~550 cal · 52g protein · 53g carbs · 13g fat · ~$3.05 per serving.

      Mississippi pork is that tangy, buttery, ranch-and-pepperoncini magic, and it works just as well on pork as it does on the classic pot roast. Piled over potatoes with carrots, each bowl brings 52 grams of protein for about $3.33. Fourteen servings out of one pot keeps the whole week covered.

      Ingredients

      • Pork Tenderloin: 3,120 g (6.9 lb)
      • Potato: 3,606 g (7.9 lb)
      • Shredded Carrots: 900 g (2.0 lb)
      • Pepperoncini (Mezzetta): 150 g (15 peppers)
      • Butter: 90 g (90 g)
      • Ranch Seasoning Mix (Hidden Valley): 36 g (4 tbsp)

      Shop Smart

      • Potatoes are one of the cheapest bases going. An 8 lb bag runs under $8 and doubles as your starch, so skip buying rice for this one.
      • One packet of ranch seasoning does all the flavor work. No need for a cabinet full of separate spices.
      • A jar of pepperoncini lasts a long time in the fridge, so the third of a jar you use here costs next to nothing per bowl.

      Make It

      1. Set the pork tenderloin in the slow cooker and surround it with the potatoes and shredded carrots.
      2. Sprinkle the ranch seasoning mix over everything.
      3. Add the pepperoncini along with a splash of their juice, then lay the butter on top.
      4. Cover and cook on low for 7 to 8 hours until the pork and potatoes are tender.
      5. Shred the pork and stir it through all those buttery, tangy juices.
      6. Divide the pork and potatoes into your containers.
