Makes 14 servings · ~630 cal · 44g protein · 73g carbs · 18g fat · ~$5.48 per serving (at everyday cost).

Oregano and dill seasoned beef over rice with a thick swipe of hummus, cool yogurt, crisp cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and salty feta. Every bowl delivers 44 grams of protein for about $5.48 (at everyday cost). It eats like the fast casual Mediterranean chains, without the line or the upcharges.

Ingredients

  • Olive Oil: 3.5 tbsp (47 g)
  • 93/7 Ground Beef (Member's Mark): 3.5 lb (1588 g)
  • Garlic Powder: 1.25 tbsp (11 g)
  • Dried Oregano (Great Value): 1.25 tbsp (4 g)
  • Dried Dill (Great Value): 1.25 tbsp (4 g)
  • Chickpeas (generic): 5.8 cans (1488 g)
  • Hummus (Great Value classic): 1.75 cups (400 g)
  • Rice (Member's Mark): 3.75 cups dry (700 g)
  • Greek Yogurt (Great Value): 1.75 cups (400 g)
  • Red Onion: 1.4 (150 g)
  • Cucumber: 1 (364 g)
  • Cherry Tomatoes: 3.5 cups (522 g)
  • Feta Cheese (Great Value crumbled): 7 oz (200 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 1 tbsp (20 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.5 tbsp (4 g)

    Shop Smart

    • The 93/7 beef is more than half the bill. When the 4 lb pack goes on sale, that is the week to make this one.
    • Cherry tomatoes ran high here. If the clamshells are not on sale, diced roma tomatoes do the same job for about half the money.
    • Feta is the sneaky line. The crumbled tubs charge you for convenience; a block of feta crumbles in ten seconds and usually costs less per ounce.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Cook the rice: rinse it, then simmer covered with water until tender and set it aside.
    3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet, add the ground beef, and break it up as it browns. Cook until no pink remains.
    4. Season the beef with the garlic powder, dried oregano, dried dill, salt, and black pepper, and let it toast in the spices for 2 minutes.
    5. While the beef cooks, dice the cucumber, halve the cherry tomatoes, and slice the red onion thin.
    6. Build each container: rice on the bottom, seasoned beef, a scoop of hummus, a scoop of Greek yogurt, then cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, and crumbled feta.
    7. Portion evenly across 14 containers. Keep the yogurt and hummus to one side of the box if you plan to reheat, then stir them in after.

    Portion It

    Weigh the pot of rice and the pan of beef, subtract the empty pot number, and divide each by 14 so every container gets an even share. Each finished bowl comes out to about 630 calories and 44 grams of protein.
