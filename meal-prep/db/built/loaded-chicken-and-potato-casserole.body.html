Makes 14 servings · ~777 cal · 51g protein · 41g carbs · 43g fat · ~$3.74 per serving (at everyday cost).

This is the loaded baked potato that grew up and became dinner: crispy seasoned potatoes, juicy chicken, and a topping of cheddar, bacon, and green onion with cool sour cream on the side. A monster 51 grams of protein per serving for about $3.74 (at everyday cost). Big portions, big flavor, small receipt.

Ingredients

  • Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast (Member's Mark): 3.5 lb (1588 g)
  • Potato (generic): 14 (2982 g)
  • Olive Oil: 0.5 cups (126 g)
  • Hot Sauce (Frank's RedHot): 1.75 tbsp (26 g)
  • Paprika: 1.25 tbsp (8 g)
  • Garlic Powder: 0.75 tbsp (8 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 0.5 tbsp (10 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.25 tbsp (2 g)
  • Cheddar Cheese, Shredded (Great Value): 14 oz (396 g)
  • Hickory Smoked Bacon (Great Value): 1.25 lb (595 g)
  • Green Onions: 14.75 tbsp (88 g)
  • Light Sour Cream (generic): 1.25 cups (300 g)

    Shop Smart

    • Chicken breast in the family pack is doing the heavy lifting here. It is one of the cheapest proteins per gram in the whole store.
    • Buy whole russets in the bag, not loose. Seven pounds runs so little on our board that potatoes are nearly free calories.
    • Bacon is the luxury line item. If your store runs ends and pieces for less, grab that pack. It gets crumbled anyway and nobody will know.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Heat the oven to 425 F. Cube the potatoes, toss them in your big pot with the olive oil, hot sauce, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper, then spread on sheet pans.
    3. Roast the potatoes 35 to 40 minutes, flipping once, until browned and crisp at the edges.
    4. While they roast, cook the bacon until crisp, drain, and chop. Cube the chicken breast into bite-size pieces.
    5. Cook the chicken in a bit of the bacon fat over medium-high, about 6 to 8 minutes, until it hits 165 F inside.
    6. Lower the oven to 400 F. Combine potatoes and chicken in a 9x13 pan, top with the cheddar and bacon, and bake about 10 minutes until melted.
    7. Finish with green onions. Keep the sour cream separate and add a spoonful when you reheat each bowl.

    Portion It

    Weigh your pot with the finished chicken and potatoes before topping, subtract the empty pot number from step 1, and divide by 14 for your per-bowl weight. Split the cheese and bacon evenly across the containers. Each serving lands at 777 calories and 51 grams of protein.
