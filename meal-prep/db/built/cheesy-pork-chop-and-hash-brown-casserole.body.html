Makes 14 servings · ~672 cal · 47g protein · 43g carbs · 35g fat · ~$3.90 per serving (at everyday cost).

Seared pork chops on a bed of cheesy sour cream hash browns with buttery mushrooms and onions. This is diner comfort food built for the week ahead: 47 grams of protein and 672 calories per serving at about $3.90 (at everyday cost). Bring an appetite and a fork; the casserole handles the rest.

Ingredients

  • Pork Chops: 5.25 lb (2380 g)
  • Frozen Hash Browns (Great Value): 25.25 cups (2117 g)
  • Yellow Onion: 6 (662 g)
  • Butter (generic): 0.5 cups (131 g)
  • White Mushrooms: 9.5 cups (662 g)
  • Light Sour Cream (generic): 3.5 cups (805 g)
  • Chicken Broth (Swanson): 0.25 lb (140 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 1.25 tbsp (21 g)
  • Cheddar Cheese, Shredded (Great Value): 14 oz (397 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.5 tbsp (4 g)

    Shop Smart

    • Pork chops are the big line item, so watch for family packs on sale and stock the freezer; chops freeze and thaw with zero drama.
    • Frozen hash browns cost far less per pound than fresh potatoes once you count your shredding time, and there is no gray-potato panic.
    • White mushrooms in the 8 oz packs go on sale often. Compare per-ounce to the sliced ones; whole mushrooms are cheaper and slicing takes two minutes.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Preheat the oven to 350F. Season the pork chops with salt and pepper and sear them in a hot skillet, about 2 minutes per side, just for color. Set aside; they finish in the oven.
    3. In the same skillet, melt the butter and cook the diced onions and sliced mushrooms 6 to 8 minutes until browned and their liquid has cooked off.
    4. In your biggest bowl, mix the thawed hash browns, sour cream, chicken broth, the mushroom-onion mixture, half the cheddar, salt, and pepper.
    5. Spread the hash brown mixture into two greased 9x13 pans and lay the seared chops on top.
    6. Cover with foil and bake 45 minutes, then uncover, scatter the remaining cheddar over everything, and bake 10 to 15 more minutes until bubbly and the pork reaches at least 145F.
    7. Rest 10 minutes, then cube the chops and fold everything together in your weighed pot so every portion gets pork, potatoes, and cheese.

    Portion It

    Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number, and divide by 14. Scoop that weight into each of 14 containers. Every bowl delivers 672 calories and 47 grams of protein.
