Makes 14 servings · ~574 cal · 33g protein · 67g carbs · 16g fat · ~$4.03 per serving (at everyday cost).

Taco-seasoned turkey simmered in salsa with peppers and onions, piled on rice with black beans, melted cheese, and cilantro. Each bowl carries 33 grams of protein for about $4.03 (at everyday cost). Same idea as the $12 chain bowl, minus the $12.

Ingredients

  • Olive Oil: 3.75 tbsp (50 g)
  • Red Onion: 3.7 (404 g)
  • Green Bell Peppers: 3.7 (441 g)
  • Garlic: 6.5 tbsp (55 g)
  • 93/7 Ground Turkey (Jennie-O): 3.75 lb (1667 g)
  • Salsa (Pace): 4.5 cups (1194 g)
  • Taco Seasoning (Great Value): 7.5 tbsp (59 g)
  • Rice (Member's Mark): 4 cups dry (752 g)
  • Seasoned Black Beans (Great Value): 2.5 cans (632 g)
  • Mexican Cheese Blend (Great Value): 7.25 oz (208 g)
  • Fresh Cilantro: 15 tbsp (15 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 1.25 tbsp (21 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.5 tbsp (4 g)

    Shop Smart

    • Jarred salsa is the flavor engine, and it replaces tomatoes, chiles, and half your spice rack in one pour. Store brand jars on sale drop the cost further.
    • The 4 lb pack of 93/7 ground turkey is the price-per-pound winner, and this recipe uses 3.75 lb of it, so nothing lingers in the fridge.
    • Seasoned black beans are cheap by the can and add protein and fiber for next to nothing a bowl. Stock up when they hit a sale price.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Cook the rice: rinse, simmer covered with water until tender, and set aside.
    3. Heat the olive oil in your biggest skillet. Cook the diced red onion and green bell peppers until soft, about 6 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
    4. Add the ground turkey and break it up as it browns. Cook to 165F with no pink remaining, then stir in the taco seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
    5. Pour in the salsa and simmer 8 to 10 minutes until it thickens into a saucy taco filling.
    6. Stir in the drained black beans and heat through.
    7. Portion rice into 14 containers, top with the turkey mixture, then finish with cheese and chopped cilantro.

    Portion It

    Weigh the rice pot and the skillet, subtract the empty pot number, and divide each by 14 so every container gets an even share. Each bowl comes out to about 574 calories and 33 grams of protein.
