Makes 14 servings · ~565 cal · 43g protein · 52g carbs · 20g fat · ~$5.73 per serving (at everyday cost).

Takeout-style lo mein with a honey soy sriracha sauce, a mountain of vegetables, and lean turkey doing the protein work. Every serving lands 43 grams of protein for about $5.73 a bowl (at everyday cost). The delivery app cannot touch those macros or that price.

Ingredients

  • 93/7 Ground Turkey (Jennie-O): 5.5 lb (2542 g)
  • Carrots (generic): 5 (316 g)
  • Yellow Onion: 2.5 (280 g)
  • Red Bell Pepper (generic): 3.5 (420 g)
  • White Mushrooms: 9 cups (636 g)
  • Green Cabbage (generic): 4.75 cups (336 g)
  • Frozen Chopped Spinach (generic): 1.5 cups (126 g)
  • Olive Oil: 5.75 tbsp (76 g)
  • Lo Mein Noodles (Simply Asia): 24 oz dry (681 g)
  • Honey (Great Value): 4.25 tbsp (88 g)
  • Soy Sauce (generic): 6.5 tbsp (104 g)
  • Sriracha (Huy Fong Foods): 2.75 tbsp (42 g)
  • Hoisin Sauce (generic): 2 tbsp (34 g)
  • Ground Ginger (Great Value): 0.25 oz (5 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 1 tbsp (20 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.5 tbsp (4 g)

    Shop Smart

    • The lo mein noodles are the shocker in this batch, easily the priciest noodle option on the shelf. Plain spaghetti pulls the same duty in a stir fry for a fraction of the price, and it would knock a real chunk off every bowl.
    • Cabbage is the cheapest vegetable in the cart: a slice of one head fills nearly 5 cups of this batch.
    • This uses 5.5 lb of turkey, so buy the big 6 lb pack. The per-pound savings over small trays covers the mushrooms.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Whisk the sauce: honey, soy sauce, sriracha, hoisin, and ground ginger. Set it by the stove.
    3. Boil the lo mein noodles 1 minute short of the package time, drain, and rinse briefly so they do not clump.
    4. Heat half the olive oil in your biggest pot or wok. Brown the ground turkey, breaking it up, until it reaches 165F with no pink remaining. Season with salt and pepper and set aside.
    5. Add the rest of the oil and stir fry the carrots, onion, bell pepper, and mushrooms until they just soften, about 5 minutes. Add the cabbage and spinach and cook 2 more minutes.
    6. Return the turkey, add the noodles and sauce, and toss over high heat until everything is coated and glossy.
    7. Portion evenly into 14 containers.

    Portion It

    Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scoop that weight into each container and every bowl comes out to about 565 calories and 43 grams of protein.
