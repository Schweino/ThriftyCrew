Makes 14 servings · ~594 cal · 35g protein · 23g carbs · 42g fat · ~$3.86 per serving (at everyday cost).

Kofta are the Middle East's proof that ground meat deserves better than burger night: herb-packed turkey skewers with warm spices under a river of lemony tahini sauce. Each bowl brings 35 grams of protein for about $3.86 (at everyday cost).

Ingredients

  • 93/7 Ground Turkey (Jennie-O): 3.5 lb (1588 g)
  • Garlic: 1 cups (140 g)
  • Yellow Onion: 1.7 (192 g)
  • Fresh Mint: 7 tbsp (11 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 1 tbsp (16 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.25 tbsp (1 g)
  • Ground Cumin: 1 tbsp (7 g)
  • Dried Oregano (Great Value): 0.75 tbsp (2 g)
  • Fresh Cilantro: 9 tbsp (9 g)
  • Ground Cinnamon (Great Value): 1.25 tbsp (9 g)
  • Ground Allspice (Great Value): 0 oz (2 g)
  • Ground Cloves (Great Value): 0 oz (2 g)
  • Red Pepper Flakes (Great Value): 0.25 tbsp (1 g)
  • Eggs (Great Value): 4 large eggs (175 g)
  • Lemon Juice (ReaLemon): 5 (229 g)
  • Bread Crumbs (generic): 10.5 tbsp (74 g)
  • Tahini (generic): 3.5 cups (840 g)

    Shop Smart

    • Ground turkey carries the batch at about 3.5 pounds. It takes on the mint, cilantro, and warm spices so well that nobody misses the pricier lamb. Tahini is the splurge line, this recipe uses a full 3.5 cups. Buy the big jar, per ounce it costs far less than the small one, and it keeps for months for dressings and hummus. Fresh mint and cilantro cost next to nothing per bunch and transform lean turkey. That is the cheapest flavor upgrade in the store, use the stems too.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Mix the ground turkey with the minced garlic (save a third of it for the sauce), grated onion, chopped mint, cilantro, eggs, bread crumbs, and the spice blend of cumin, oregano, cinnamon, allspice, cloves, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes. Mix just until it comes together.
    3. Shape the mixture into oval kofta, about 3 per serving. Wet hands make this far less annoying.
    4. Bake at 400F for 18 to 20 minutes, or sear in a hot pan, until browned and 165F in the center.
    5. Make the tahini sauce: whisk the tahini with the lemon juice, the reserved garlic, a big pinch of salt, and enough cold water to reach a pourable, creamy consistency. It will seize up first, keep whisking, it comes back.
    6. Build bowls with the kofta and a generous spoon of tahini sauce over the top.
    7. Store extra sauce in a separate container if you like it fresh, it also holds up fine mixed in.

    Portion It

    Weigh your finished kofta and sauce in the pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Portion to that number and each bowl lands at about 594 calories with 35 grams of protein.
