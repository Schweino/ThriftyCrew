Makes 14 servings · ~855 cal · 61g protein · 46g carbs · 48g fat · ~$7.61 per serving (at everyday cost).

Caldereta is the Filipino special occasion stew, the one that shows up at birthdays and fiestas. Beef braises with potatoes, carrots, and peppers in a tomato base, and then two things go in that surprise everyone the first time: peanut butter and cheese. They are not a gimmick. They thicken the sauce and give it a savory richness that tomato alone cannot reach. Each bowl lands at 855 calories and 61 grams of protein for about $7.61 a serving (at everyday cost).

    Shop Smart

    • Chuck roast is the entire budget here. Nothing else on the list comes close, so this is a recipe to make the week chuck goes on sale rather than the week you feel like caldereta.
    • Potatoes and carrots are how a party stew feeds a crowd without more meat. Buy both in the big bags, since the per pound price is far better and neither one goes bad quickly.
    • Chicken livers are the traditional thickener and they cost almost nothing. Blend them smooth with a little broth and nobody will identify them in the finished sauce, they will just notice it tastes deeper than it should.
    • Peanut butter and canned tomato sauce do the rest of the thickening work for pocket change. Between those and the livers, you never need a store bought caldereta mix.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Cut the potatoes and carrots into big chunks and fry them in the vegetable oil until browned on the outside, then set them aside. This keeps them from disintegrating during the braise.
    3. Season the cubed beef with salt and pepper and brown it hard in the same pot in batches.
    4. Cook the onion and garlic until soft, then add the tomato sauce, tomato paste, soy sauce, and the broths and scrape the bottom of the pot clean.
    5. Return the beef, bring it to a simmer, cover, and cook low for about 2 hours until it is fork tender.
    6. Blend the chicken livers with a ladle of the hot broth until smooth and stir the mixture back into the pot along with the peanut butter. Let it simmer 10 minutes to thicken.
    7. Add the fried potatoes and carrots, the sliced red and green peppers, and the olives and cook another 20 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the sauce coats a spoon.
    8. Stir the shredded cheddar in off the heat until it melts into the sauce, then taste for salt.

    Portion It

    Set the full pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Stir the pot well right before you scoop, because the beef and potatoes sink and the sauce floats. Weighing each container is the only way to keep the last one from being a bowl of gravy. Every portion lands at 855 calories.
