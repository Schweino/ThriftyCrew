Makes 14 servings · ~604 cal · 36g protein · 45g carbs · 28g fat · ~$3.62 per serving (at everyday cost).

This is the potluck casserole that always disappears first. Spanish rice built from scratch instead of a box, turkey and spinach folded into a sour cream and green chile base, corn chips crushed across the top for crunch. Each serving lands at 604 calories with 36 grams of protein for about $3.62 (at everyday cost). The chips on top are not a garnish. They are the entire reason people go back for seconds.

Ingredients

  • Butter (generic): 4.75 tbsp (65 g)
  • Yellow Onion: 2.3 (257 g)
  • Rice (Member's Mark): 2.5 cups dry (450 g)
  • Diced Tomatoes & Green Chilies (Great Value (Ro-Tel style)): 2.3 cans (660 g)
  • Diced Green Chiles (Old El Paso): 2.3 cans (264 g)
  • Light Sour Cream (generic): 4.75 cups (1073 g)
  • Cream of Chicken Soup (generic condensed (USDA)): 2.3 cans (695 g)
  • Frozen Chopped Spinach (generic): 1.5 lb (663 g)
  • Ground Cumin: 1.5 tbsp (10 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 0.5 tbsp (7 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.5 tbsp (3 g)
  • Turkey Breast (generic (USDA)): 2.5 lb (1143 g)
  • Mexican Cheese Blend (Great Value): 18.5 oz (527 g)
  • Corn Chips (Fritos): 2.25 cups (65 g)

    Shop Smart

    • Skip the boxed Spanish rice mix. Plain rice plus a can of tomatoes with green chilies and a spoonful of cumin gives you the same thing for a fraction of the cost.
    • Light sour cream in the 16 oz tubs is the biggest non-meat line here at nearly five cups. Check whether a larger tub drops the price per ounce before you grab three small ones.
    • Frozen chopped spinach costs a fraction of fresh for the same cooked volume, and you must wring it dry or the casserole will weep.
    • One bag of corn chips covers the topping with plenty left over. Crush them by hand right before baking so they stay crisp instead of going stale in the pan.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Melt the butter in a large pot, cook the diced onion 5 minutes until soft, then stir in the dry rice and toast it for two minutes.
    3. Add one can of the tomatoes with green chilies plus enough water to cook the rice, season with the ground cumin and salt, then cover and simmer 18 minutes until tender.
    4. Thaw the frozen chopped spinach and wring it dry in a clean towel. Squeeze harder than feels necessary, then squeeze once more.
    5. In a large bowl, stir together the light sour cream, the condensed cream of chicken soup, the remaining tomatoes with green chilies, and the drained diced green chiles.
    6. Fold in the chopped turkey breast, the squeezed spinach, the cooked Spanish rice, and about two thirds of the Mexican cheese blend, then season with salt and black pepper.
    7. Spread everything into a greased 9x13 pan, top with the remaining cheese, and bake at 350F for 30 minutes until it bubbles at the edges.
    8. Crush the corn chips over the top and return the pan for 5 minutes only, just long enough to warm them without letting them soften. Rest 10 minutes before portioning.

    Portion It

    Weigh the pan, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Cut to that number so the chip crust rides along with every square. Each serving comes to 604 calories and 36 grams of protein.
