Makes 14 servings · ~596 cal · 53g protein · 62g carbs · 17g fat · ~$4.75 per serving (at everyday cost).

Chili and macaroni in one skillet, the way it should be. This crowd-pleaser stacks 53 grams of protein per bowl for about $4.75 (at everyday cost) and makes 14 servings. It is warm, filling, and cheap enough that you will wonder why you ever bought the boxed version.

Ingredients

  • 93/7 Ground Beef (Member's Mark): 5 lb (2230 g)
  • Penne Pasta (Barilla): 3 boxs (969 g)
  • Yellow Onion: 3 onions (250 g)
  • Kidney Beans (generic): 1 can (430 g)
  • Diced Tomatoes (Great Value): 2 cans (800 g)
  • Tomato Sauce (generic): 2 cans (450 g)
  • Beef Broth (Great Value): 1 carton (900 g)
  • Cheddar Cheese, Shredded (Great Value): 1 bag (160 g)
  • Chili Powder: 1 jar (14 g)
  • Ground Cumin: 1 jar (8 g)
  • Garlic: 1 bulb (15 g)
  • Olive Oil: 2 tsp (27 g)
  • Salt (Morton): to taste (15 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): to taste (4 g)

    Shop Smart

    • A can of kidney beans adds protein and fiber for about a dollar, making it one of the best-value stretchers in the recipe.
    • Buy chili powder and cumin in bulk jars. They cost pennies per meal and last months, unlike the pricey single-use seasoning packets.
    • Grab store-brand shredded cheddar. Melted into hot chili mac, nobody can tell the difference from the name brand.

    Make It

    1. Brown 4.9 pounds of 93/7 ground beef with the chopped onion, then drain.
    2. Stir in the kidney beans, diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, beef broth, chili powder, cumin, and garlic.
    3. Simmer until the chili thickens and the flavors come together.
    4. Boil the penne separately until just tender, then drain and fold it in.
    5. Melt the shredded cheddar over the top before portioning into containers.
