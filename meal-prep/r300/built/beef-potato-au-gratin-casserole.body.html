      Shop Smart

      • Potatoes are the volume and the value. Buy the biggest bag you will get through, since the per pound price drops steeply with size and they keep for weeks in a cool dark spot.
      • Shredded cheddar goes on sale constantly. Buy the bags when they drop and freeze what you will not use this week, because it melts perfectly fine after thawing.
      • Slicing the potatoes thin and even matters more here than in any other recipe in this batch. Uneven slices mean some are mush while others are still hard.
      • Ground beef is the only line that moves the number much. Everything else on this list is a dollar or two.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Brown the ground beef in a skillet with the paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until no pink remains, then drain the fat.
      3. Peel the potatoes and slice them thin and even, about an eighth of an inch. A mandoline is fastest, a steady hand and a sharp knife works.
      4. Slice the onion thin as well.
      5. Layer half the potatoes in the slow cooker, then half the onion, half the beef, and a third of the cheddar. Repeat the layers.
      6. Pour the chicken broth over the top and finish with the last of the cheddar.
      7. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours, until a knife goes through the potatoes with no resistance.
      8. Turn the cooker off and let it sit uncovered 15 minutes so the layers set enough to lift out cleanly.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Cut the set casserole into portions of roughly that weight and lift them into 14 containers so the layers stay intact. Each serving lands at 569 calories with 34 grams of protein.
