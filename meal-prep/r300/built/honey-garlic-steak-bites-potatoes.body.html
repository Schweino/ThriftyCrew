      Steak bites are one of those things people order out and never think to make at home, which is a shame, because cubed sirloin in a honey garlic glaze is about as simple as cooking gets.       Shop Smart

      • Sirloin is the whole budget here. Watch the meat case for markdowns near the sell by date, since these cubes get cooked long enough that a day of age means nothing.
      • Honey is the second biggest line. The larger jars cost meaningfully less per ounce and honey does not spoil, so buying up is a pure win.
      • Cut the steak into cubes yourself from a whole sirloin instead of buying pre cut stew beef or kabob meat. Pre cut always carries a labor markup.
      • Cornstarch and soy sauce are cheap pantry items with long shelf lives. Buying the small bottle of soy sauce costs more per ounce every single time.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cut the sirloin into even bite sized cubes and season them with salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes.
      3. Sear the cubes in the vegetable oil in batches over high heat, just long enough to brown the outside. They should still be rare in the middle.
      4. Move the seared beef to the slow cooker with any browned bits from the pan.
      5. Whisk the honey, soy sauce, and minced garlic together and pour it over the beef.
      6. Cover and cook on low for 3 hours, until the cubes are tender but still holding their shape.
      7. Whisk the cornstarch with a little cold water, stir it in, and cook on high 15 minutes until the glaze coats the back of a spoon.
      8. Stir in the butter off the heat so the glaze goes glossy, then taste for salt.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Spoon that weight of steak bites and glaze into each of 14 containers, scraping the glaze from the bottom as you go so it does not all end up in the last container. Each serving lands at 677 calories with 54 grams of protein.
