Makes 14 servings · ~636 cal · 54g protein · 28g carbs · 33g fat · ~$6.70 per serving (at everyday cost).

This is the pot roast every other pot roast is measured against. Chuck roast, onion, carrots, celery, potatoes, and broth, all in one vessel, cooked long enough that the meat gives up and the vegetables take on the gravy. Nothing clever, nothing trendy, just the dinner that has worked for a hundred years. Each bowl carries 54 grams of protein and 636 calories for about $6.70 (at everyday cost).

Ingredients

  • Beef Chuck Roast: 7.75 lb (3500 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.5 tbsp (4 g)
  • Olive Oil: 3.5 tbsp (47 g)
  • Yellow Onion: 1.7 (192 g)
  • Garlic: 5.25 tbsp (44 g)
  • Carrots (generic): 9 (534 g)
  • Celery: 5.3 (289 g)
  • Beef Broth (Great Value): 7.25 cups (1750 g)
  • All-Purpose Flour (Great Value): 11 tbsp (88 g)
  • Italian Seasoning: 0.5 tbsp (2 g)
  • Dried Thyme (Great Value): 1 tbsp (3 g)
  • Potato (generic): 6 (1312 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 1 tbsp (20 g)

    Shop Smart

    • Chuck roast is the whole budget. Set a price in your head that counts as a good deal in your store, and buy two when you see it. The second one freezes for months.
    • Carrots and celery are cheapest as whole bunches and bags, not as pre cut sticks. You are chopping them into big chunks anyway.
    • Skip the fancy potatoes. Plain russets break down slightly at the edges and thicken the gravy, which is a feature here, not a flaw.
    • Flour is what turns the cooking liquid into gravy. You already own it, and it costs pennies, so there is no reason to buy a gravy packet for this one.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Pat the chuck roast dry, season it heavily with salt and black pepper, and sear it in the olive oil in a hot pan until it is deeply browned on all sides.
    3. Move the roast to the slow cooker. In the same pan, cook the chopped onion and garlic until soft, scraping the browned bits loose.
    4. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for a minute, then whisk in the beef broth until it is smooth and starting to thicken.
    5. Pour that over the roast, then add the carrots, celery, potatoes, thyme, and Italian seasoning around it.
    6. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours, until the roast pulls apart with light pressure from a fork.
    7. Lift the roast out, shred it, and stir it back into the vegetables and gravy.
    8. Taste the gravy and correct the salt. Long cooks dull seasoning, so it almost always needs a little at the end.

    Portion It

    Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scoop that weight into each of 14 containers, mixing between scoops so every one gets beef, vegetables, and gravy in the same ratio. Each bowl lands at 636 calories with 54 grams of protein.
