      Makes 14 servings · ~623 cal · 42g protein · 26g carbs · 39g fat · ~$1.96 per serving.

      Order a turkey Reuben at a deli and you get turkey, sauerkraut, Swiss, and Russian dressing on grilled rye. Order fourteen of them and you get a bill you do not want to look at. This bakes the whole sandwich into a pan instead: buttered rye cubes on the bottom, turkey and kraut and Swiss layered through, dressing whisked in. Each serving lands at 623 calories with 42 grams of protein for about $1.96.

      Shop Smart

      • A single loaf of rye covers this whole pan. Marked-down day-old rye is actually better here, because stale bread holds its structure under the dressing instead of turning to paste.
      • Canned sauerkraut costs less than the refrigerated bags and works fine once it is squeezed dry. That squeeze is not optional; wet kraut will flood the pan.
      • Swiss cheese is the biggest line item. Buy it by whichever pack has the lowest price per ounce and shred it yourself rather than buying slices.
      • Skip bottled Russian dressing. Mayonnaise, ketchup, and chopped dill pickles make the same thing from items already in your fridge.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cube the rye bread, toss it with the melted butter and the caraway seeds, and toast it on a sheet pan at 350F for 10 minutes until the edges dry out.
      3. Drain the sauerkraut and squeeze it dry in a clean towel. Wring it hard, then wring it again, because kraut holds more liquid than it looks like it does.
      4. Whisk the mayonnaise with the Dijon mustard, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, chopped dill pickles, salt, and black pepper to make the dressing.
      5. Spread half the toasted rye cubes across a greased 9x13 pan, then layer on the sliced turkey breast, the squeezed sauerkraut, and half the shredded Swiss.
      6. Spoon the dressing evenly over the layers, then top with the remaining rye cubes and press the whole thing down firmly so it compacts.
      7. Cover with foil and bake at 350F for 30 minutes, then uncover, add the rest of the Swiss, and bake 15 minutes more until browned and bubbling.
      8. Rest 15 minutes before portioning so the layers hold together on the spatula.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pan, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Cut squares to that number and lift them flat so the rye layers stay stacked. Each serving comes to 623 calories and 42 grams of protein.
