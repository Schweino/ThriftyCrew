      Shop Smart

      • Buy cheddar in blocks and shred it yourself. Cheese is the biggest line in this recipe. Blocks cost less per ounce and the pre shredded bags are dusted with starch that keeps them from melting smooth.
      • Store brand pasta shells are the right call. Shells hold more sauce than elbows do, and the house brand boxes cost noticeably less than the name brands for identical wheat.
      • Milk carries most of the sauce, cream just enriches it. If the cream price is ugly, shift the ratio toward milk. The roux is what actually thickens it.
      • Dijon and Worcestershire cost pennies per batch. Both come out of bottles you already own and both add the savory depth that separates good mac from beige mac.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the oven to 375F and grease two large baking dishes. Boil the pasta shells to just under tender, since they will keep cooking in the oven, then drain them.
      3. Slice the smoked sausage into coins and dice the onion.
      4. Brown the sausage coins in a large pot until the edges darken, then add the onion and cook until it softens. Move both to a bowl.
      5. Melt the butter in that same pot, whisk in the flour, and cook the roux for two minutes so it loses the raw flour taste.
      6. Pour in the milk and the heavy cream slowly while whisking constantly, and keep whisking until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon.
      7. Kill the heat and stir in the dijon mustard, the Worcestershire, salt, black pepper and most of the shredded cheddar. Adding cheese off the heat is what keeps the sauce from turning grainy.
      8. Fold in the drained pasta and the sausage and onion, spread it into the baking dishes, top with the rest of the cheese, and bake until bubbling and golden.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on your scale and subtract the empty pot weight from Step 1. Divide that total by 14 and scoop the exact amount into each container. Every bowl lands near 786 calories and 30 grams of protein.
