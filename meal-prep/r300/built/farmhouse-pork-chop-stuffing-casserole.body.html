      Makes 14 servings · ~669 cal · 40g protein · 36g carbs · 40g fat · ~$1.77 per serving.

      This is church potluck food, and that is a compliment. Pork chops sitting on a bed of buttery bread stuffing, all of it bound with cream of chicken soup and baked until the edges go crisp and the middle stays soft. It tastes like Thanksgiving on a Wednesday. The stuffing is made from scratch with bread cubes, celery, onion and butter, which costs less than the boxed mix and tastes considerably better.

      Shop Smart

      • Make the stuffing from bread, not a box. Two loaves of plain sandwich bread cost less than the mix and give you better texture. Cube it and dry it in a low oven while you prep everything else.
      • Save your bread heels all month. Toss them in a freezer bag as you go and this casserole costs you almost nothing on the stuffing side.
      • Bone-in pork chops are cheaper and stay juicier. They cost less per pound than boneless and the bone protects the meat through a long bake.
      • Cream of chicken soup is the binder, and the store brand is identical. Buy the generic cans by the four pack. Nobody has ever identified the brand in a finished casserole.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cube the sandwich bread and dry the cubes on a sheet pan at 300F for about 20 minutes so they can soak up liquid without turning to mush.
      3. Melt the butter in a skillet and cook the diced celery and onion until soft and translucent, then pour the whole buttery mix over the bread cubes.
      4. Whisk the eggs with the milk and the chicken broth, season with salt and black pepper, and pour it over the bread. Toss until every cube is damp but not soaked.
      5. Spread the stuffing in a large greased baking dish and press it flat.
      6. Season the pork chops with salt and black pepper, sear them in a hot skillet for color, and nest them into the stuffing.
      7. Spoon the cream of chicken soup over the chops, cover with foil, and bake at 350F for 45 minutes, then uncover for 15 more to brown the top.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished casserole in its dish, subtract the dish weight, divide by 14, and cut portions to that exact weight so every container gets the same amount of chop and stuffing. Every serving lands near 669 calories with 40 grams of protein.
