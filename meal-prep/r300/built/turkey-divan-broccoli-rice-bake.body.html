      Shop Smart

      • Turkey breast is the biggest line here. A single 3 pound frozen roast covers this whole recipe, and frozen roasts run far under any fresh cut in the meat case.
      • Condensed cream of chicken soup is one of the cheapest cans in the store and it is the sauce base. Building the same thing from scratch takes a roux, broth, cream, and twenty minutes for pennies in savings.
      • Frozen broccoli florets cost almost nothing for this batch and go straight from bag to pan. Fresh crowns cost more per usable cup once you cut off the stalks.
      • Bread crumbs and butter make the crumb topping for pennies. That crunchy top is what separates a casserole people go back for from a pan of hot cream.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice and let it cool slightly. It goes into the pan already cooked, since it will not soften properly in a thick cream sauce.
      3. Cut the turkey breast into bite-size pieces, season with salt and black pepper, and cook them through in a skillet to 165F, or use turkey you already roasted.
      4. Whisk the cream of chicken soup, heavy cream, mayonnaise, curry powder, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper together into a smooth sauce.
      5. Steam or thaw the broccoli florets just until bright green and still firm. Overcooked broccoli in a casserole goes gray and sulfurous, and there is no coming back from that.
      6. Fold the rice, turkey, broccoli, sliced olives, most of the green onions, and about two thirds of the cheddar into the sauce, then spread it in your baking pans.
      7. Toss the bread crumbs with the melted butter and the rest of the cheddar, and scatter that over the top.
      8. Bake at 350F for 30 minutes until the edges bubble and the crumbs turn deep golden, then rest 10 minutes before you touch it.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is your per-container gram target. Cut down through the crumb topping so each container gets its share of the crunchy part, then finish with the remaining green onions. Each serving lands at 705 calories and 35 grams of protein.
