      Shop Smart

      • Jarred alfredo is one of the great budget shortcuts. Homemade would need butter, cream, and a wedge of real parmesan, which costs more than triple and takes a whisk and your attention.
      • Mushrooms are the biggest produce line here. Buy whole ones and slice them yourself, since presliced costs more per ounce and dries out faster in the package.
      • Rotini is one of the cheapest boxes in the aisle. Buy three, because a pasta bake needs volume and dry pasta stores forever in a cabinet.
      • Mozzarella goes on top where you can see it, so this is the one place a full 16 ounce buy earns its keep. Watch for the buy two get one deals and stack your freezer.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the rotini about 2 minutes short of the box time and drain it. It will finish cooking in the sauce in the oven, and pasta cooked twice to full is mush.
      3. Slice the mushrooms and cook them in a dry hot pan until they give up their water and it evaporates, about 8 minutes. Dry pan first, then season. That is how you get browned mushrooms instead of gray ones.
      4. Add the ground turkey to the pan and brown it, breaking it up, with salt and black pepper, until it is cooked through to 165F.
      5. Stir in the alfredo sauce and a splash of water, and let it warm through until it loosens into a pourable sauce.
      6. Fold the drained pasta into the sauce so every piece is coated, then spread it into your baking pans.
      7. Cover with the mozzarella and bake at 375F for 25 minutes, until it bubbles around the edges and the cheese has browned in patches.
      8. Let it rest 10 minutes, then transfer it into your weighed pot for portioning.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is your per-container gram target. Scoop from different spots in the pan so the browned edge pieces get spread around, because those are worth fighting over. Each serving lands at 574 calories and 43 grams of protein.
