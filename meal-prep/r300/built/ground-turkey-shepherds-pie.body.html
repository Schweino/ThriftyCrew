      Makes 14 servings · ~569 cal · 35g protein · 67g carbs · 18g fat · ~$1.87 per serving.

      Shepherd's pie is what happens when a pound of ground meat and a sack of potatoes decide to become dinner together. Seasoned turkey and peas on the bottom, a thick blanket of real mashed potatoes on top, parmesan browned across the whole surface. Each serving carries 35 grams of protein and 569 calories for about $1.87. The potatoes are doing most of the heavy lifting on that price, and they are not shy about it.

      Shop Smart

      • Buy potatoes in the big sack, not loose by the each. This batch runs through most of a 9 lb bag, so the bulk price is not going to waste sitting in your pantry.
      • 93/7 ground turkey is the engine of this dish. The per-pound price drops as the pack size climbs, and turkey freezes clean, so buy the bigger pack and split it.
      • Frozen peas beat canned on both price and texture. The store-brand 12 oz bags are the play, and you need several, so check whether a larger bag is cheaper per ounce.
      • Condensed cream of chicken soup is just the binder here. Store brand does the identical job, and leftover cans sit on a shelf for a year without complaint.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Peel and chunk the potatoes, cover them with cold salted water, and boil until a fork slides through with no resistance, about 20 minutes.
      3. While the potatoes cook, heat the olive oil in your largest skillet over medium-high and brown the ground turkey, breaking it apart as it goes.
      4. Add the diced onion and the garlic to the turkey, cook 5 minutes until soft, then season with salt, black pepper, and dried thyme.
      5. Stir in the condensed cream of chicken soup and the frozen peas, let it bubble 3 minutes, then spread the filling into a greased 9x13 pan.
      6. Drain the potatoes and mash them with salt, black pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. Keep them thick. Whipped potatoes will slide right off the filling.
      7. Spoon the mashed potatoes over the filling and spread them to the edges so the pan is sealed, then drag a fork across the top to make ridges that will brown.
      8. Scatter the parmesan over the potatoes and bake at 400F for 25 to 30 minutes, until the top is browned and the edges are bubbling. Rest 15 minutes before you portion.

      Portion It

      Set the whole pan on the scale, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Cut and weigh that number into each container so nobody gets the short square. Every serving lands at 569 calories and 35 grams of protein.
