      Shop Smart

      • Smoked turkey sausage is over half the cost here. Watch for the ropes to go on the two for one deal, since they freeze for months in their sealed packaging.
      • Potatoes by the 6 pound bag are the cheapest bulk you can buy and this soup eats a lot of them. Buy the bag, keep them cool and dark, and they will still be good weeks later.
      • One bunch of kale is all you need. Strip the leaves from the stems, and add it at the very end so it stays green instead of turning to seaweed.
      • Heavy cream is the finish and it is worth every penny. Milk will break in a soup this hot and acidic, so this is not the place to save a couple dollars.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Slice the smoked turkey sausage into coins and brown them in the olive oil in a skillet. Browning takes ten minutes and it is the single biggest flavor step in this recipe, so do not skip it just because a slow cooker is involved.
      3. Move the sausage into the slow cooker, then cook the diced onion and minced garlic in the same skillet for 5 minutes, scraping up the browned bits, and add those too.
      4. Cut the potatoes into half-inch thick slices and add them to the cooker with the chicken broth, the italian seasoning, cayenne, salt, and black pepper.
      5. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours, or on high for 3, until the potatoes are completely tender and starting to soften at the edges.
      6. Stir in the chopped kale and cook 15 minutes more, just long enough for it to wilt and turn deep green.
      7. Turn the cooker off, then stir in the heavy cream. Adding it at the end and off the boil is what keeps it from separating into curds.
      8. Let it stand 10 minutes to thicken, then move it into your weighed pot.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is your per-container gram target. Stir the pot between scoops so the potatoes and sausage stay evenly distributed and nobody ends up with a container of cream broth. Each bowl lands at 613 calories and 25 grams of protein.
