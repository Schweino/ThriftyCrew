      Makes 14 servings · ~619 cal · 38g protein · 52g carbs · 26g fat · ~$1.53 per serving.

      Kuku paka comes off the Swahili coast, where Indian spice trading and East African cooking met and stayed together. Chicken gets browned first, then finishes in a coconut and tomato sauce built on cumin, coriander and turmeric. It is milder than an Indian curry and brighter than a Thai one, and it is one of those dishes that improves overnight without any help from you. Each bowl runs 619 calories and 38 grams of protein for about $1.53.

      Shop Smart

      • Coconut milk is the priciest thing in this pot. Buy the store brand cans in the international aisle rather than the name brand ones, check the label for full fat instead of light, and stock up when they drop, since cans keep for a year.
      • Crushed tomatoes in the big 28 ounce can cost far less per ounce than the small cans, and this recipe uses more than one can worth, so the big size is the obvious buy.
      • Boneless skinless thighs stay tender in a coconut sauce where breast turns to chalk. The five pound club pack is the best per-pound price in the meat case.
      • The spice load here is cumin, coriander, turmeric and cayenne, all of which cost a fraction as much in the bagged section of the international aisle as they do in the branded jars on the spice rack.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Season the chicken thighs with salt and black pepper. Heat the vegetable oil in your largest pot over medium-high and brown them in batches until they have color on both sides, then set them aside. That browning is where half the flavor comes from.
      3. Cook the diced onion in the same pot for 5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and grated ginger and cook one more minute.
      4. Add the ground coriander, ground cumin, ground turmeric and cayenne pepper and stir them in the hot oil for 30 to 45 seconds until they smell toasted rather than dusty.
      5. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, scrape the bottom clean, and let it cook down for 8 to 10 minutes until the tomato darkens and the oil starts to separate at the edges.
      6. Stir in the coconut milk, return the chicken and its juices, and simmer uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes until the sauce thickens and the chicken reads 165F. Do not boil it hard or the coconut milk will split.
      7. While it simmers, cook the rice and let it steam covered off the heat for 10 minutes.
      8. Chop the chicken into bite-size pieces, stir it back in with the lemon juice and chopped cilantro, and fold the rice through the pot.

      Portion It

      Put the pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight, and divide by 14. Same number in every container so the math holds all week. Each bowl carries 619 calories, and coconut sauces reheat gently, so use half power in the microwave and stir once.
