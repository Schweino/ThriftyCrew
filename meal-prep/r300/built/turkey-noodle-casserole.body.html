      Shop Smart

      • Egg noodles are usually cheaper per ounce in the 12 oz bags than in the boxes, but check both. Wide noodles hold sauce better than fine ones in a bake.
      • Shredded cheddar is the biggest line item here at a pound and a half. Compare the 8 oz bags against a block you shred yourself, because the block often wins on price per ounce.
      • One frozen turkey breast roast covers this batch with meat left over. Roast it, dice it, and freeze the extra for the next casserole.
      • Frozen peas and canned corn are the cheapest vegetables you can put in a casserole, and they hold their texture through baking better than fresh does.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the egg noodles in salted water two minutes short of the package time and drain them. They will keep cooking in the sauce, and overcooked noodles turn to paste in a bake.
      3. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat, add the garlic for one minute, then whisk in the flour and cook it for two minutes.
      4. Pour in the milk slowly while whisking, then cook 4 to 5 minutes until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon.
      5. Season with the dried thyme, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper, then melt in about three quarters of the shredded cheddar off the heat so it does not go grainy.
      6. Fold in the diced turkey breast, the frozen peas, the drained corn, and the cooked noodles until everything is coated.
      7. Spread it into a greased 9x13 pan, scatter the rest of the cheddar over the top, and bake at 350F for 25 to 30 minutes until bubbling and browned in spots.
      8. Rest 10 minutes before portioning so the sauce sets around the noodles instead of running out of them.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pan, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Noodle bakes cut cleanly once rested, so slice to the scale rather than guessing at squares. Each serving delivers 578 calories and 37 grams of protein.
