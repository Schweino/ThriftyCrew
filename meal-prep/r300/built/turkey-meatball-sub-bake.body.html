      Makes 14 servings · ~587 cal · 55g protein · 68g carbs · 26g fat · ~$3.16 per serving.

      A meatball sub is a wonderful thing that turns into a soggy tragedy about four hours after you build it. So we build it in a pan instead: turkey and smoked sausage meatballs baked in marinara, tucked into split rolls under melted mozzarella, assembled fresh when you eat it. Each serving carries 587 calories and 55 grams of protein for about $3.16.

      Shop Smart

      • The buns are the biggest surprise on this list. Any split roll works here, so buy whatever sturdy roll your store discounts. Day-old bread from the bakery rack is even better, since it holds sauce without collapsing.
      • Smoked turkey sausage adds a smoky depth that ground turkey alone cannot reach, and it is not an expensive addition. That is the ingredient doing the flavor heavy lifting.
      • Jarred marinara is the right call. Sauce simmered with meatballs picks up plenty of character on its own, so an expensive jar is wasted money here.
      • Buy parmesan and mozzarella by the tub and the bag rather than the small shaker cans. This recipe uses both, and the cheese aisle punishes small purchases harder than almost any other department.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Chop the smoked turkey sausage fine, or run it through a food processor, and mix it with the ground turkey, the eggs, the bread crumbs, the parmesan, minced garlic, minced onion, chopped mushrooms, salt, and black pepper.
      3. Roll the mixture into meatballs and set them on lined sheet pans. Two per serving is the target size, so aim a little bigger than a golf ball.
      4. Bake at 400F for 20 minutes until browned and cooked through to 165F.
      5. Warm the marinara in a pot, then slide the baked meatballs in and simmer them 15 minutes so they drink up the sauce instead of just wearing it.
      6. Cook the rice and move it into your weighed pot.
      7. Split the buns and lay them out. Do not assemble them all now, since bread that sits in sauce for four days is a texture nobody wants.
      8. Shred or portion out the mozzarella and keep it in its own small container.

      Portion It

      Set the pot of rice on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is the base in each container. Add an even share of meatballs and sauce, then pack the bun and the mozzarella separately, so you can build and broil the sub fresh when you eat it. Each serving lands at 587 calories and 55 grams of protein.
