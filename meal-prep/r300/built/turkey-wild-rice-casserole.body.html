      Makes 14 servings · ~575 cal · 49g protein · 53g carbs · 19g fat · ~$3.71 per serving.

      Wild rice is not really rice, it is a marsh grass that Minnesota has been harvesting by canoe for centuries, and it tastes like nothing else in the grain aisle. Here it goes into a creamy turkey bake with mushrooms, celery, and Swiss cheese. Each serving carries 49 grams of protein and 575 calories for about $3.71. That is the most expensive dish in this batch, and the wild rice is the reason. It is also the reason it tastes like a supper club instead of a casserole.

      Shop Smart

      • Wild rice is the whole cost story. A blend labeled wild rice blend costs a fraction of pure wild rice and still gives you the nutty flavor and the chew, so check both bags before you commit.
      • White mushrooms in 8 oz packs cost far less than pre-sliced baby bellas and cook down identically in a cream sauce. Slice them yourself.
      • Buy the whole celery bunch rather than celery hearts. You use most of it here, and the outer ribs are the flavorful ones anyway.
      • Two frozen turkey breast roasts cover this batch. Roast both at once, dice, and freeze half for the next bake so you only heat the oven one time.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Rinse the wild rice and simmer it in salted water for 40 to 45 minutes, until the grains split open and are tender but still chewy, then drain well.
      3. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat and cook the diced onion, sliced celery, and sliced mushrooms for 10 minutes, until the mushroom liquid cooks away.
      4. Add the garlic and cook one minute, then stir in the chicken broth, milk, and heavy cream and bring it to a low simmer.
      5. Season with the garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, salt, black pepper, and a small pinch of nutmeg, then melt in the shredded Swiss until the sauce is smooth.
      6. Fold in the cooked wild rice and the diced turkey breast, checking that the mixture is loose. Wild rice keeps drinking liquid in the oven, so a little extra broth here is insurance.
      7. Spread it into a greased 9x13 pan, top with the parmesan, and bake at 350F for 30 to 35 minutes until bubbling and browned at the edges.
      8. Rest 10 minutes before portioning so the sauce tightens up around the rice.

      Portion It

      Put the pan on the scale, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Weigh each scoop into its container, because wild rice settles unevenly and eyeballing will short somebody. Every serving lands at 575 calories and 49 grams of protein, a strong protein number for a creamy bake.
