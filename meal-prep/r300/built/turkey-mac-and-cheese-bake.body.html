      Shop Smart

      • Cheese is the entire cost of this dish. Compare the 8 oz shredded bags against blocks priced per pound, because a block plus five minutes with a box grater is often the cheaper route.
      • Pasta shells and elbows are the cheapest shapes on the shelf. Buy whichever store-brand box is lower per ounce, since a bake does not care about the shape.
      • Milk is a loss leader at most stores. If you are buying a gallon anyway, this recipe uses over six cups of it and the marginal cost is tiny.
      • Stuffing mix makes a better crunchy topping than bread crumbs and costs about the same. Crush it with your hands, no processor needed.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the pasta shells in heavily salted water two minutes short of the box time, then drain. They finish in the oven and mushy mac is unforgivable.
      3. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat, whisk in the flour, and cook two minutes until it smells toasted rather than raw.
      4. Add the milk in stages, whisking constantly, and cook 5 to 7 minutes until the sauce is thick enough that a spoon drawn through it leaves a track.
      5. Season with salt, black pepper, Italian seasoning, and a small pinch of nutmeg, which is the trick that makes cheese sauce taste like cheese sauce.
      6. Pull the pot off the heat and melt in the shredded cheddar and the Mexican cheese blend a handful at a time. Direct heat will break a cheese sauce every time.
      7. Fold in the diced turkey breast and the drained shells, then spread everything into a greased 9x13 pan.
      8. Crush the stuffing mix over the top and bake at 350F for 25 to 30 minutes until the crust is golden and the sides bubble, then rest 10 minutes before portioning.

      Portion It

      Weigh the whole pan, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Baked mac holds a clean edge once rested, so cut to the scale instead of eyeballing squares. Every serving delivers 663 calories and 44 grams of protein.
