      Makes 14 servings · ~569 cal · 44g protein · 83g carbs · 5g fat · ~$2.33 per serving.

      Loubia is the green bean stew that shows up on tables from Beirut to Amman, tomato and allspice and cinnamon simmered down until the beans go soft and the sauce goes deep. Turkey breast stands in for the usual stew beef, and it soaks up the spice just as well for a fraction of the money. Over rice it lands at 569 calories and 44 grams of protein for about $2.33, and it reheats better on day four than it did on day one.

      Shop Smart

      • Frozen turkey roasts are the cheapest whole-muscle turkey in the store by a wide margin. Buy them when they are marked down after the holidays and keep one in the freezer for exactly this.
      • Frozen green beans beat fresh here on both price and effort. In a stew that simmers this long, nobody can tell the difference, and you skip twenty minutes of trimming.
      • Allspice, cinnamon, coriander, and bay leaves are the entire personality of this dish and they cost almost nothing from jars you already keep. That is the best trade in cooking.
      • Buy the tomato paste in the small can and use the whole thing. This recipe calls for a full can, so nothing goes gray in the back of the fridge.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cut the turkey breast into bite-size chunks, pat them dry, and toss them with the flour, salt, and black pepper.
      3. Heat the olive oil in a heavy pot over medium-high and brown the turkey in batches so it sears instead of steams, about 4 minutes a batch. Set it aside.
      4. Drop the heat to medium, cook the diced onion for 5 minutes, then add the garlic, allspice, cinnamon, coriander, and paprika and stir for one minute until the kitchen smells like the dish already.
      5. Stir in the tomato paste, let it darken for a minute, then add the diced tomatoes, the bay leaves, and enough water to loosen it into a stew.
      6. Return the turkey, cover, and simmer on low for 45 minutes. In a slow cooker, this is the point where you set it on low for 6 hours and go live your life.
      7. Add the green beans in the last 20 minutes so they stay tender instead of turning to mush, and check that the turkey has reached 165F.
      8. Cook the rice, move it into your weighed pot, and keep the stew in its own pot for portioning.

      Portion It

      Set the pot of rice on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is the rice bed for each container. Then ladle the stew evenly across all 14, digging to the bottom each time so the turkey chunks get spread around instead of settling. Each portion lands at 569 calories and 44 grams of protein.
