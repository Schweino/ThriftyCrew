      Shop Smart

      • Corn muffin mix is one of the cheapest items in the entire store and it keeps for a year in the box. Buy a stack when it goes on sale, since you need four boxes for this pan.
      • Two frozen turkey breast roasts cover this batch. Roast both on one oven cycle, dice, and freeze what you do not use immediately.
      • Cream of mushroom soup is the binder and store brand is indistinguishable once it is baked under cornbread. Three cans is all you need.
      • Frozen peas beat canned on price and texture here, and they will not turn to mush during a 40 minute bake the way canned peas do.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Stir the condensed cream of mushroom soup together with the onion powder, poultry seasoning, salt, and black pepper in a large bowl until smooth.
      3. Fold in the diced turkey breast, the frozen peas, and the shredded cheddar. The filling should be thick, since the cornbread pulls moisture out of it as it bakes.
      4. Spread the filling into a greased 9x13 pan and press it into an even layer all the way to the corners.
      5. Whisk the corn muffin mix with the milk and eggs just until combined. Lumps are fine; overmixed cornbread bakes tough.
      6. Pour the batter over the filling and spread it gently to the edges with the back of a spoon so it seals the pan.
      7. Bake at 400F for 30 to 35 minutes, until the cornbread is golden and a toothpick pushed into the topping comes out clean.
      8. Rest 15 minutes before portioning. Cornbread finishes setting as it cools and will crumble if you cut it hot.

      Portion It

      Set the pan on the scale, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Cut squares to that weight so every container gets both filling and topping. Each serving comes to 571 calories and 44 grams of protein.
