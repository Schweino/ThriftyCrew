      Makes 14 servings · ~655 cal · 37g protein · 84g carbs · 18g fat · ~$2.60 per serving.

      Green chili is a completely different animal from red. Tomatillos and green chiles instead of tomato and chili powder, bright and tangy instead of deep and smoky. The shortcut that makes this a weeknight pot instead of a weekend project is jarred salsa verde. Somebody already roasted and blended the tomatillos for you. Add ground turkey, creamy white beans and rice and you have a full dinner in about half an hour of actual work.

      Shop Smart

      • Salsa verde is the whole budget here, so shop it. Check the Hispanic aisle before the salsa aisle. The same green salsa in a taller jar with Spanish on the label is routinely half the price of the branded one up front.
      • Cannellini beans do double duty. Mash a scoop against the side of the pot and the chili thickens without a roux, without cream, and without another dollar spent.
      • Buy garlic by the head, not the jar. Jarred minced garlic costs several times more per usable clove and tastes tinny in a bright dish like this one.
      • Sour cream goes on at reheat. Stirred into the pot it separates by day three. Kept in a small container and spooned on top, it stays perfect all week.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice separately per the package and set it aside covered.
      3. Brown the ground turkey in the olive oil in your big pot over medium-high heat, breaking it up until it is cooked through.
      4. Add the diced onion and cook until translucent, then stir in the minced garlic for the last minute.
      5. Pour in the jarred salsa verde and the chicken broth, scrape the bottom of the pot, and bring it to a simmer.
      6. Add the drained cannellini beans, mash a scoop of them against the side of the pot to thicken the chili, and simmer 25 minutes.
      7. Season with salt and black pepper, stir in the chopped cilantro off the heat, and keep the sour cream separate for topping.

      Portion It

      Spread the cooked rice into your 14 containers, then set the chili pot on your scale, subtract the empty pot weight from Step 1, divide by 14, and spoon that exact weight over the rice. Every serving lands near 655 calories with 37 grams of protein once the sour cream goes on at reheat.
