      Makes 14 servings · ~562 cal · 37g protein · 85g carbs · 9g fat · ~$1.93 per serving.

      Sopa de fideo is the first thing a lot of Mexican kids learn to love and one of the last things they stop craving. Thin noodles toasted in oil until they turn the color of a penny, then simmered in a blended tomato broth. Cooked down thick instead of soupy it becomes sopa seca, and it holds in the fridge far better than any brothy version would. Each serving carries 37 grams of protein and 562 calories for about $1.93.

      Shop Smart

      • Fideo and broken spaghetti are the same thing once they hit the pan. Buy whichever is cheaper per ounce, and snap the spaghetti into short pieces with your hands.
      • A small can of chipotles in adobo costs about a dollar and lasts for months. Freeze the leftovers in single-pepper portions on a sheet of parchment.
      • Canned diced tomatoes go on sale by the case constantly and keep for years. Store brand blends into the sauce indistinguishably.
      • One frozen turkey breast roast covers the whole batch. Roast it, chop it, and it costs a fraction of anything sliced at a deli counter.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Break the spaghetti into pieces about an inch long. This takes a few minutes and it is what makes the dish fideo instead of just pasta.
      3. Heat the olive oil in your largest pot over medium and toast the broken pasta, stirring constantly, for 6 to 8 minutes until it turns golden brown. Do not walk away; it goes from toasted to burnt in under a minute.
      4. Scoop the toasted pasta out and set it aside, then blend the canned diced tomatoes with the onion, garlic, and chipotles in adobo until smooth.
      5. Pour the blended sauce into the hot pot and fry it for 5 minutes, stirring, until it darkens and thickens. Frying the sauce is the step most people skip and it is the one that matters.
      6. Add the chicken broth, the dried oregano, the brown sugar, salt, and black pepper, then bring it to a boil.
      7. Return the toasted pasta to the pot, drop to a simmer, and cook uncovered 12 to 15 minutes until the noodles are tender and most of the liquid is gone.
      8. Fold in the chopped turkey breast, warm it through for 3 minutes, and let the pot stand 10 minutes so the noodles absorb the last of the sauce.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pot, subtract the empty pot weight you noted, and divide by 14. Fideo keeps tightening as it cools, so scoop it while it is still loose and let the scale keep the containers even. Each serving comes to 562 calories and 37 grams of protein.
