      Makes 14 servings · ~578 cal · 25g protein · 71g carbs · 20g fat · ~$1.59 per serving.

      This one starts on the stovetop and finishes under the broiler, which means no long bake and no heating the whole house in August. Rice simmered in broth, smoked turkey sausage browned in with zucchini, peppers, corn and onion, a cheddar sauce poured over the top and blistered. Each serving runs 578 calories with 25 grams of protein for about $1.59. Fresh basil at the end is what keeps it from tasting like every other cheesy rice dish.

      Shop Smart

      • Zucchini is one of the cheapest vegetables in the store in summer and one of the more expensive ones in January. Buy it in season and swap in frozen mixed vegetables when it is not.
      • Smoked turkey sausage is the only real meat cost here and this recipe needs relatively little of it. That restraint is why the per-serving number stays low.
      • Canned corn is the cheapest way to add sweetness and bulk. One can does the job and the store brand is identical to the name brand.
      • Fresh basil is the one splurge and it is a small one. If the packs look tired, dried basil in the sauce works and costs even less.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Bring the chicken broth to a boil in a large oven-safe pot, stir in the rice, cover, and simmer on low for 18 minutes, then set it aside covered.
      3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet and brown the sliced smoked turkey sausage until the cut edges char, then set it aside.
      4. In the same skillet, cook the diced onion, red bell pepper, and zucchini for 8 minutes over medium-high, hot enough that the zucchini colors instead of weeping.
      5. Add the garlic and the drained corn, cook two minutes more, then season with salt and black pepper and return the sausage to the pan.
      6. Whisk the flour into the milk in a jar until smooth, pour it into the skillet, and cook 4 to 5 minutes until it thickens into a sauce.
      7. Off the heat, melt in about two thirds of the shredded cheddar, then fold the whole skillet into the cooked rice until evenly combined.
      8. Spread it level, scatter the rest of the cheddar over the top, and broil 3 to 5 minutes until blistered. Watch it the entire time, then finish with torn fresh basil.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pot, subtract the empty pot weight you noted, and divide by 14. Stir before every scoop, since the sausage and vegetables settle differently than the rice does. Each serving comes to 578 calories and 25 grams of protein.
