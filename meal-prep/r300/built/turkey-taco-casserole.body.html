      Shop Smart

      • Corn tortillas cost far less than flour and hold their shape in a layered bake instead of turning to paste. Buy the bigger packs.
      • Canned black beans are the cheapest protein per dollar in the entire recipe and this pan uses nearly five cans. Dried beans cut that cost further if you plan a day ahead.
      • Mix your own taco seasoning from chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and paprika. The packets cost several times as much for the same spices plus cornstarch.
      • The fresh toppings, cilantro, red onion, jalapeno, and peppers, are the cheapest way to make a casserole taste bright. Add them cold at the end rather than baking them in.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet and brown the ground turkey, breaking it into crumbles, then season it heavily with the taco seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
      3. Stir in the drained black beans and the salsa and simmer 10 minutes until the mixture thickens and stops running on the spoon.
      4. Spread a thin layer of the filling in the bottom of a greased 9x13 pan so the first tortillas do not stick, then cover it with a layer of corn tortillas, overlapping like shingles.
      5. Build three full layers: filling, tortillas, cheese, and repeat, finishing with cheese on top. Press each layer down as you go so the casserole holds together when you cut it.
      6. Cover with foil and bake at 375F for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake 12 to 15 minutes more until the top is browned and bubbling.
      7. Rest 20 minutes. Layered casseroles need that time or the first slice will slide apart on the spatula.
      8. Top with the drained diced tomatoes, sliced olives, green onions, red onion, diced bell peppers, jalapenos, cilantro, and light sour cream just before portioning.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pan, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Cut to that number and lift each square flat so the tortilla layers stay stacked. Every serving comes to 650 calories and 46 grams of protein.
