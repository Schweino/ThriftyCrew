      Makes 14 servings · ~575 cal · 29g protein · 68g carbs · 22g fat · ~$2.35 per serving.

      Moussaka is the Greek casserole everybody admires and almost nobody makes, because it has three parts and a reputation. Here is the truth: roasted eggplant, a cinnamon-warmed turkey tomato sauce, and a bechamel you whisk in eight minutes. Bake it, cut it into squares, set each square over rice, and you have 575 calories and 29 grams of protein for about $2.35 a container.

      Shop Smart

      • Eggplant is the biggest line on this list, so buy it in season when the price per each drops. Late summer is when it practically gives itself away in the Midwest.
      • The bechamel is butter, flour, milk, an egg, and a little parmesan. That is a small pile of dairy doing the job of a sauce most people think is fancy.
      • A gallon of milk costs about the same as a quart-size specialty carton, and you only need a few cups here. Buy the gallon, use the rest for breakfast, and stop paying the small-container tax.
      • Parmesan by the tub keeps for months in the fridge and one ounce goes into this whole batch. It is a jar that earns its shelf space across dozens of recipes.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Slice the eggplant into rounds, salt them, and let them sit 20 minutes to draw out water. Pat them dry, brush them with the olive oil, and roast at 425F for about 25 minutes until they are soft and browned.
      3. While the eggplant roasts, cook the diced onion in a large pot until soft, add the garlic for a minute, then brown the ground turkey until it reaches 165F.
      4. Add the tomato sauce, beef broth, cinnamon, oregano, red wine vinegar, bay leaves, salt, and pepper. Simmer 20 minutes until the sauce is thick and almost dry, because a wet meat layer means a soggy casserole.
      5. Make the bechamel: melt the butter, whisk in the flour and cook two minutes, then pour in the milk slowly while whisking until it thickens. Off the heat, whisk in the beaten eggs, the parmesan, nutmeg, salt, and pepper.
      6. Layer the roasted eggplant and the meat sauce in your baking pans, pour the bechamel over the top, and smooth it flat.
      7. Bake at 350F for 45 minutes until the top is set and golden, then let it rest at least 20 minutes so the squares hold together when you cut them.
      8. Cook the rice, move it into your weighed pot, and cut the moussaka into 14 even squares.

      Portion It

      Set the pot of rice on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is the rice bed in each container. Lay one moussaka square on top of each bed and you are done, no guessing required. Each portion lands at 575 calories and 29 grams of protein.
