      Makes 14 servings · ~575 cal · 34g protein · 56g carbs · 22g fat · ~$2.60 per serving.

      Spanakorizo is Greek spinach rice, lemony and green and usually meatless. We brown turkey into the base and turn a side dish into a full dinner, then finish it with mint, dill, and crumbled feta. One skillet, one pot to wash, and 575 calories with 34 grams of protein for about $2.60 a bowl.

      Shop Smart

      • Frozen chopped spinach is the second biggest line on this list, so it is the one worth chasing on sale. Fresh spinach would cost several times more for the same cooked volume, since it collapses to almost nothing.
      • Feta by the tub is cheaper per ounce than the crumbled cups near the salad bar, and it keeps for weeks in its brine. Crumble it yourself and pocket the difference.
      • Fresh mint is the priciest herb on this list, which makes it the one place you can trim. A pot of mint on a windowsill grows faster than you can cook with it and costs nothing after the first pot.
      • Buy the big bottle of olive oil. This recipe pours three quarters of a cup, and the large bottle usually runs close to half the per-ounce price of the small one.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Thaw the frozen spinach and squeeze it dry, hard, with your hands or in a clean towel. Wet spinach makes soggy rice, and this is the step people skip and then regret.
      3. Heat the olive oil in a large deep skillet and cook the diced onion until soft, about 8 minutes, then add the garlic for one minute.
      4. Add the ground turkey and brown it, breaking it up as it goes, until it is cooked through to 165F with no pink left.
      5. Stir in the tomato paste and let it darken for a minute, then add the squeezed spinach, the dried dill, salt, and black pepper.
      6. Add the rinsed rice and enough water to cover it by about half an inch, bring it to a boil, then drop to low, cover, and cook 18 to 20 minutes until the liquid is absorbed.
      7. Kill the heat, pour in the lemon juice, scatter the chopped fresh mint over the top, and let it sit covered 10 minutes so the rice finishes steaming.
      8. Fluff it with a fork, move it into your weighed pot, and hold the feta back for the containers.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is your per-container gram target. Scoop it out and crumble the feta over the top of each one, since feta stirred into hot rice disappears and feta on top does not. Each bowl lands at 575 calories and 34 grams of protein.
