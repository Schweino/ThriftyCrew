Makes 14 servings · ~695 cal · 48g protein · 57g carbs · 28g fat · ~$3.99 per serving (at everyday cost).

Feijoada is the national dish of Brazil, and it started the way most great dishes start, with cooks making something extraordinary out of the cuts nobody else wanted. Black beans and several kinds of pork go into one pot and cook until the beans break down into a dark, glossy gravy. Served over white rice on a Saturday, it is the meal an entire country slows down for.

Ingredients

  • Seasoned Black Beans (Great Value): 3 cans (794 g)
  • Hickory Smoked Bacon (Great Value): 0.75 lb (298 g)
  • Pork Shoulder (generic (USDA)): 2.75 lb (1191 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 2 tbsp (35 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 1 tbsp (7 g)
  • Beef Chuck Roast: 1.75 lb (770 g)
  • Diced Ham (John Morrell (Smithfield)): 1 lb (446 g)
  • Yellow Onion: 5.5 (604 g)
  • Garlic: 6 tbsp (52 g)
  • Chicken Broth (Swanson): 1.3 cartons (1260 g)
  • Ground Coriander (Great Value): 0.25 tbsp (2 g)
  • Bay Leaves (Great Value): 0.07 oz (2 g)
  • White Vinegar (generic): 1.75 tbsp (26 g)
  • Rice (Member's Mark): 3.75 cups dry (700 g)

    Shop Smart

    • Beans are the point, not the filler. Black beans give this dish its body, its color and a good chunk of its protein, and they cost less per serving than almost anything else you can put in a pot.
    • Buy the pork shoulder, use the trimmings. Feijoada was built on scraps. Every bit of fat and every bone you throw in makes the gravy richer, so buy bone-in when you can.
    • Bacon and diced ham are seasoning meats. Small amounts of each add smoke and salt through the whole pot. This is where a half pound does the job of two.
    • Slow cooker on low is the right setting. Eight or nine hours is what turns beans into gravy. Rushing it on high gives you beans in broth, which is not the same dish.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Brown the chopped bacon in a skillet, then use that fat to sear the cubed pork shoulder and the cubed beef chuck roast on all sides. Do it in batches so the meat browns instead of steams.
    3. Cook the diced onion and minced garlic in the same skillet until soft, scraping up everything stuck to the bottom.
    4. Move the bacon, seared meats, onion and garlic to the slow cooker along with the diced ham, the drained black beans, the chicken broth, the white vinegar, the bay leaves, ground coriander, salt and black pepper.
    5. Cook on low for 8 to 9 hours, until the pork shreds under a fork and the beans have gone soft enough to thicken the liquid.
    6. Mash a scoop of the beans against the side of the crock and stir it back in. That is what turns the broth into gravy.
    7. Pull out the bay leaves, shred the larger pieces of meat, and cook the rice per the package to serve underneath.

    Portion It

    Spread the cooked rice across your 14 containers, then set your mixing pot on the scale, subtract the empty pot weight from Step 1, divide by 14, and spoon that exact weight of beans and meat over the rice.
