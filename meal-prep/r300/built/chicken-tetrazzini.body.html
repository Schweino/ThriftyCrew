      Makes 14 servings · ~562 cal · 28g protein · 45g carbs · 31g fat · ~$1.70 per serving.

      Tetrazzini was named for an opera singer and built for leftovers, which is a combination you do not see every day. Spaghetti, chicken, and mushrooms get folded into a cream sauce, topped with mozzarella, and baked until the edges go brown and chewy. Those crispy edges are the reason people fight over the corner pieces. Each serving lands at 562 calories and 28 grams of protein for about $1.70 a serving.

      Shop Smart

      • Mushrooms are the biggest produce line and they are essential, not optional. Buy whole white mushrooms and slice them yourself, because pre-sliced costs more per pound and dries out on the shelf.
      • Heavy cream is the second real expense. A pint goes a long way and keeps in the fridge for weeks, so there is no waste even if you only use part of it.
      • Spaghetti is one of the cheapest foods in the entire store. Buy the boxes when they hit their sale price and keep several in the pantry, since dried pasta lasts essentially forever.
      • This dish was designed for leftover chicken. If you have a rotisserie carcass or leftovers from another recipe, use them here and the cost drops further with no work at all.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the oven to 375F. Boil the spaghetti two minutes short of the package time and drain it. It will finish cooking in the oven, and fully cooked pasta going into a bake comes out mushy.
      3. Cook the chicken breast in the olive oil, then cut or shred it into bite sized pieces.
      4. Brown the sliced mushrooms in a dry hot pan first, without crowding, until they release their water and it cooks off. Adding fat too early makes them steam, and steamed mushrooms make a watery casserole.
      5. Add the butter, onion, and garlic to the mushrooms and cook until soft, then sprinkle in the flour and cook it for 2 minutes.
      6. Whisk in the chicken broth slowly, then the heavy cream, and simmer until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon. Finish it with the lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
      7. Fold in the spaghetti and chicken, then spread everything into a large baking dish and scatter the mozzarella over the top.
      8. Bake 25 to 30 minutes until bubbling and browned on top, then rest 10 minutes before cutting.

      Portion It

      Score the bake into 14 even squares and lift one into each container, then set the dish on the scale and subtract the empty weight to confirm your portions are honest. Every serving lands at 562 calories. Reheat uncovered so the top browns again instead of going soft, and make sure somebody besides you gets a corner piece.
