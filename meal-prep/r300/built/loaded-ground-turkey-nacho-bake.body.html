      Nachos are usually a plate you build to order and eat standing up. Baked as a sheet pan instead, they portion by weight and reheat without turning into a soggy mess, which is the whole trick.       Shop Smart

      • Buy the largest bag of tortilla chips and use the sturdy ones from the middle. Thin chips collapse under a wet topping, so restaurant-style rounds hold up far better than the delicate kind.
      • Canned black beans are the cheapest protein in this recipe. Rinse them well and they add bulk without adding cost.
      • Canned diced tomatoes are far cheaper than fresh out of season and this recipe uses a lot of them. Drain them hard so the chip layer keeps some backbone.
      • Cheese is the cost driver. Compare the 8 oz shredded bags against a block you grate yourself, because at nearly a pound and a half the gap adds up.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet and brown the ground turkey, seasoning it with the garlic powder, salt, and black pepper as it cooks.
      3. Add the diced onion and green bell pepper and cook 5 minutes, then stir in the drained black beans and the drained diced tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes until the mixture thickens.
      4. The filling needs to be thick, not saucy. If liquid is still pooling in the pan, keep cooking, because a wet filling ruins the chips instantly.
      5. Spread half the tortilla chips across two greased sheet pans or a large baking dish, then spoon half the turkey mixture over them and scatter half the cheese.
      6. Repeat with the remaining chips, filling, and cheese, then add the sliced jalapenos across the top.
      7. Bake at 400F for 12 to 15 minutes, only long enough to melt the cheese and warm everything through. Any longer and the chips give up.
      8. Finish out of the oven with the chopped cilantro, dollops of light sour cream, and the salsa spooned over the top.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pans, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Portion right away while the chips still have structure, then add the cold toppings to each container. Each serving comes to 561 calories and 37 grams of protein.
