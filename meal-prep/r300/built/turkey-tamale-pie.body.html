      Shop Smart

      • Grits and cornmeal are close cousins, and a canister of either is one of the cheapest things in the store. Coarser grind gives the topping a better texture than fine cornmeal does.
      • Canned enchilada sauce goes on sale constantly and keeps for a year. Two cans covers this pan, and store brand is fine under a cornbread lid.
      • Canned black beans and diced green chiles are pantry staples worth stocking deep. Both are cheap, both keep forever, and both end up in half the Tex-Mex recipes here.
      • One frozen turkey breast roast covers the filling. Cook it, chop it, and you are done with the most expensive part of the recipe.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Warm the chopped turkey breast in a large skillet with the enchilada sauce, the drained green chiles, the drained black beans, and the chili powder, cumin, paprika, and garlic powder.
      3. Whisk the dry gravy mix into a splash of water and stir it into the filling, then simmer 5 minutes until it thickens enough that a spoon leaves a clean line through it.
      4. Spread the filling into a greased 9x13 pan and press it level, then scatter about half the Mexican cheese blend over it.
      5. In a bowl, whisk the grits, flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt together, then stir in the milk, melted butter, and eggs just until the batter comes together.
      6. Fold the drained corn and the rest of the cheese into the batter. Lumps are welcome here; overmixed cornbread bakes tight and dry.
      7. Spoon the batter over the filling and spread it to the edges so it seals the pan completely, then bake at 400F for 30 to 35 minutes until the top is golden and set.
      8. Rest 15 minutes, then scatter the sliced green onions over the top before portioning.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pan, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Cut to that number so every container gets cornbread and filling in the right ratio. Each serving comes to 561 calories and 31 grams of protein.
