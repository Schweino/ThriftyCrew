      Shop Smart

      • Buy the four pound pack of ground pork. The cost line already assumes it. Pork is usually the cheapest ground meat in the case, and what you do not use freezes flat in a bag.
      • Bread crumbs and flour are pennies here. Both are pantry items that carry a dozen batches. If you have heels of bread going stale, dry them in the oven and pulse them yourself for free.
      • Beef broth is the whole gravy. Store brand does the job. Buy the big carton rather than the little cans and the per batch cost drops to almost nothing.
      • Grab onions by the bag, not by the each. Two pounds of yellow onions costs barely more than a couple of loose ones, and onions keep on the counter for weeks.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the oven to 400F and line two sheet pans. Put a pot of salted water on for the potatoes.
      3. In a big bowl combine the ground pork, bread crumbs, eggs, green onions, garlic, italian seasoning, onion powder, paprika, dried thyme, salt and black pepper. Mix with your hands only until it comes together, because overworking it makes the meatballs tough.
      4. Roll even meatballs, space them out on the sheet pans, and bake until browned and cooked through.
      5. Peel and chunk the potatoes, boil until a fork slides in easy, then drain and mash with a little of the butter, salt and pepper.
      6. Melt the rest of the butter in a wide pot and cook the sliced yellow onion low and slow until it turns deep golden and sweet. That patience is the whole flavor of the gravy.
      7. Sprinkle the flour over the onions and stir for a minute, then pour in the beef broth along with the worcestershire and soy sauce. Whisk until it thickens into a glossy gravy.
      8. Slide the baked meatballs into the gravy and let them simmer a few minutes so they drink some of it up.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on your scale and subtract the empty pot weight from Step 1. Divide that total by 14 and scoop the exact amount into each container, mashed potatoes first, then meatballs and gravy over the top. Every bowl lands near 663 calories and 32 grams of protein.
