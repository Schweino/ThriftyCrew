      Makes 14 servings · ~563 cal · 44g protein · 60g carbs · 15g fat · ~$4.13 per serving.

      Ropa vieja means old clothes, which is what shredded beef in a pepper and tomato sauce apparently looked like to whoever named it. It is Cuba's national dish, it is built for a long slow cook, and the olives at the end are not optional. They are the salt and the sharpness that make the whole pot work. Each bowl carries 44 grams of protein and 563 calories for about $4.13.

      Shop Smart

      • Tomato paste and canned diced tomatoes together build the sauce. Paste comes in a small can, so freeze the remainder in spoonfuls instead of letting it die in the fridge.
      • Green bell peppers are the traditional choice and the cheap one. That is a rare alignment, so take it.
      • Olives are the flavor that makes this ropa vieja instead of generic shredded beef. Buy them by the can rather than from the olive bar, where you are paying a premium for the brine.
      • Cider vinegar is a small amount from a bottle that lasts forever. It brightens the sauce at the end and there is no good substitute for that job.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Season the steak with salt, black pepper, cumin, oregano, and turmeric and lay it in the slow cooker.
      3. Slice the onion and green bell peppers into strips and scatter them over the beef with the minced garlic.
      4. Stir the tomato paste into the beef broth until smooth, then pour it over along with the diced tomatoes and their juice.
      5. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours, until the beef pulls into strands with almost no effort.
      6. Shred the beef directly in the cooker with two forks and stir it back through the peppers and sauce.
      7. Stir in the sliced olives and the apple cider vinegar and let it sit 10 minutes on the heat so the flavors settle.
      8. Cook the rice separately and taste the sauce for salt, remembering the olives brought plenty.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Spoon that weight of shredded beef, peppers, and sauce into each container, then add the same weight of rice to every one. Each bowl lands at 563 calories with 44 grams of protein.
