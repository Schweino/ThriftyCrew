      Makes 14 servings · ~576 cal · 34g protein · 45g carbs · 26g fat · ~$1.15 per serving.

      Peruvian roast chicken is famous for two things: a cumin and vinegar marinade that soaks in overnight, and the green sauce that shows up beside it. That sauce, aji verde, is where the whole plate turns from good to memorable. Cilantro, chile, garlic and a little mayonnaise blitzed until it looks like house paint and tastes like a secret. Thighs marinate, one pan roasts, rice underneath, sauce over the top. Each bowl runs 576 calories and 34 grams of protein for about $1.15.

      Shop Smart

      • Boneless skinless thighs are the right cut and the cheap cut at the same time. They hold up to an overnight acid marinade without going chalky, and the five pound club pack beats the small tray packs per pound every time.
      • Cilantro is the backbone of the green sauce, so buy it as bunches from the produce case rather than the little clamshell of leaves. One or two bunches covers the whole batch for the price of a soda.
      • Bottled lemon and lime juice are doing real work here and cost a fraction of fresh citrus. For a marinade and a blended sauce, nobody can tell the difference.
      • Jalapeno is the whole chile budget. A single pepper or two carries the heat, so buy loose by the pound and pick the firm, heavy ones.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Whisk the olive oil, white vinegar, lemon juice, ground cumin, paprika, garlic powder, dried oregano, salt and black pepper into a marinade. Add the chicken thighs, turn them until every piece is coated, and refrigerate overnight if you can spare the time, or at least four hours if you cannot.
      3. Heat the oven to 425F. Spread the sliced yellow onion across a large sheet pan and lay the marinated thighs on top so the onion catches everything that drips.
      4. Roast 30 to 35 minutes, until the chicken reads 165F and the edges have gone dark and a little sticky. Do not crowd the pan; use two if you need to.
      5. While it roasts, cook the rice and let it steam covered off the heat for 10 minutes.
      6. Make the aji verde: blend the cilantro, jalapeno, garlic, mayonnaise and lime juice with a pinch of salt until it is completely smooth and bright green. Taste it and add more chile if you want it to bite back.
      7. Chop the roasted chicken into bite-size pieces and stir it into your mixing pot with the roasted onion, the pan juices and the cooked rice.
      8. Fold about half the green sauce through the whole batch and hold the rest back to spoon on at reheat time so it stays fresh and loud.

      Portion It

      Put the full pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight, and divide by 14. Same number in every container, no eyeballing. Each one carries 576 calories, and the reserved green sauce goes on top right before you eat so it never sees the microwave.
