      Makes 14 servings · ~561 cal · 38g protein · 45g carbs · 26g fat · ~$1.98 per serving.

      Manicotti is the Sunday dinner shape: big pasta tubes stuffed with ricotta and meat, buried in marinara, blanketed in mozzarella. It takes more assembly than a skillet dish, but every minute of it happens once and then feeds you for two weeks. Each serving carries 561 calories and 38 grams of protein for about $1.98.

      Shop Smart

      • The jumbo shells are the biggest single line on this list. Watch for the store brand, which usually runs half the Barilla price for a shape that gets stuffed and covered in sauce anyway.
      • Ricotta is the heart of the filling and the 15 ounce tubs are the value size. Three of them cover this batch and the leftover ounces go straight into scrambled eggs or toast.
      • Fresh basil costs about the price of a candy bar for one small pack. That is the right amount to spend. Dried basil is not the same thing here, since the fresh leaf is what cuts the richness of all that cheese.
      • Buy mozzarella in 8 ounce bags on sale and freeze the extras. Shredded cheese freezes better than almost anything else in the dairy case and it goes straight from freezer to pan.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the jumbo shells to just under done, about 2 minutes short of the box, then drain and spread them out so they do not stick together while you work.
      3. Heat the olive oil, cook the diced onion 5 minutes, add the garlic for a minute, then brown the ground turkey until it is cooked through to 165F. Season with the italian seasoning, salt, and pepper and let it cool a few minutes.
      4. Mix the ricotta with the chopped fresh basil and about a third of the mozzarella, then fold in the cooled turkey. Cool matters, because hot meat turns ricotta runny.
      5. Spread a layer of marinara across the bottom of your baking pans so nothing sticks.
      6. Stuff each shell with the filling using a spoon or a zip bag with the corner cut off, then set them in the pans in rows.
      7. Cover with the rest of the marinara and the remaining mozzarella, then bake covered at 375F for 30 minutes, uncover, and bake 10 more until the top browns.
      8. Rest 15 minutes, cook the rice, and move the rice into your weighed pot.

      Portion It

      Set the pot of rice on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is the base in each container. Then divide the stuffed shells evenly by count across all 14 and spoon the extra sauce from the pan over the top. Each serving lands at 561 calories and 38 grams of protein.
