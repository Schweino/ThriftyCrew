      Shop Smart

      • The small petite sirloins that get marked down because nobody knows what to do with them are exactly right for this. Slow cooking rescues cuts that a grill would ruin.
      • Brown gravy mix comes in multi packet boxes that cost less per packet than singles, and the packets keep in the pantry for a year.
      • Onions are the second ingredient by weight and the source of most of the flavor. Buy the bag, not singles.
      • Cornstarch finishes the gravy for pennies. A box lasts years and it beats buying a second gravy packet just to thicken things.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Season the steaks with salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning and lay them in the slow cooker in an even layer.
      3. Slice the onions thick and pile them over the steaks along with the minced garlic.
      4. Whisk the brown gravy mix with about two cups of water and pour it over everything.
      5. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours, until the steaks are tender and the onions have collapsed into the gravy.
      6. Whisk the cornstarch with a little cold water, stir it into the hot liquid, and cook on high 15 to 20 minutes until the gravy thickens.
      7. Break the steaks into large pieces in the gravy and let them sit in it for 10 minutes.
      8. Cook the rice separately and keep it plain so the gravy carries the plate.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Spoon that weight of steak, onions, and gravy into each container, then add the same weight of rice to every one. Each bowl lands at 561 calories with 40 grams of protein.
