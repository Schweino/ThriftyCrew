      Makes 14 servings · ~574 cal · 34g protein · 88g carbs · 10g fat · ~$1.25 per serving.

      Huevos rancheros is ranch-hand breakfast: fried eggs on a warm tortilla with salsa, the kind of meal built to keep somebody working until dark. Plated to order it is perfect and completely useless for meal prep. Built into a rice bowl instead, it portions cleanly and reheats without ruining anything. Each serving carries 34 grams of protein and 574 calories for about $1.25, the lowest price in this entire group.

      Shop Smart

      • Eggs are the cheapest complete protein you can buy and this recipe uses one per bowl. Two dozen covers the batch, and eggs keep for weeks past the carton date.
      • Corn tortillas cost less than flour and crisp better. Buy the larger packs and cut them into strips or wedges for the crunch on top.
      • Canned black beans are the second cheapest protein in the recipe. Rinse them well, warm them with a little cumin, and they taste like you cooked them from dried.
      • Jarred salsa on sale beats making your own out of season, when tomatoes are expensive and mealy. Buy the store brand and check the ingredient list for tomatoes first, not water.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice and hold it warm. It is the base of every bowl, so make sure it is seasoned with salt while it cooks rather than after.
      3. Cut the corn tortillas into strips and crisp them in a little vegetable oil until golden, then drain them on paper towels and salt them right away.
      4. Warm the chopped turkey breast in a skillet with a splash of the salsa and a pinch of salt and black pepper, just until it is heated through.
      5. Warm the drained black beans in a small pot with a little water so they stay loose rather than drying out in the fridge.
      6. Fry the eggs in batches to your preference. For meal prep, cook them a shade past runny, because a broken yolk in a container on Thursday helps nobody.
      7. Build the bowls: rice on the bottom, then beans, then turkey, then salsa spooned across the top.
      8. Set an egg on each bowl, scatter the Mexican cheese blend and the chopped cilantro over it, and add the crisped tortilla strips last so they stay crunchy.

      Portion It

      Weigh the rice, beans, and turkey together, subtract your empty pot weight, and divide by 14, then top each bowl with one egg. Building to a weight keeps the base even and the eggs make the count obvious. Each serving comes to 574 calories and 34 grams of protein.
