      Makes 14 servings · ~586 cal · 45g protein · 63g carbs · 17g fat · ~$4.06 per serving.

      Carne guisada is the Tex-Mex stew that shows up in a bowl at lunch and in a tortilla at breakfast. Sirloin, tomatoes, jalapeno, and a spoonful of flour that turns the whole thing into its own gravy as it cooks. It thickens up in the fridge, which makes it one of the better containers to open on day four. Each bowl carries 45 grams of protein and 586 calories for about $4.06.

      Shop Smart

      • Canned diced tomatoes go in by the stack here. Compare the store brand to the name brand on the price per ounce tag, because in a stew they are the same tomatoes.
      • Buy a whole sirloin and cube it yourself. Pre cut stew meat costs more per pound and gives you less say in the trim.
      • Jalapenos are sold loose and cost very little. Buy a few extra and freeze them whole, since they chop fine straight out of the freezer for a stew.
      • Two tablespoons of flour is the entire thickener. There is no reason to buy a roux, a gravy packet, or a cornstarch product for this one.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cut the sirloin into small cubes, pat them dry, and season with salt, black pepper, cumin, and oregano.
      3. Brown the beef in the olive oil in batches over high heat until it takes on real color.
      4. Add the chopped onion, minced garlic, and diced jalapeno and cook until soft, scraping the pot bottom clean.
      5. Sprinkle the flour over everything and stir for a minute so it toasts rather than tasting raw.
      6. Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juice and the beef broth, along with the splash of soy sauce from the pantry list.
      7. Cover and simmer low for 75 to 90 minutes, until the beef is tender and the liquid has reduced into a gravy that clings.
      8. Taste for salt and heat, then cook the rice separately.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Spoon that weight of stew into each container, then add the same weight of rice to every one. Each bowl lands at 586 calories with 45 grams of protein.
