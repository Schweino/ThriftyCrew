      Makes 14 servings · ~587 cal · 34g protein · 59g carbs · 24g fat · ~$2.25 per serving.

      Izmir kofte is the Turkish answer to the question every tired cook asks on a Sunday: what if the meatballs and the potatoes just cooked themselves in the same pan? Seasoned turkey kofte and thick potato slices bake together under a tomato and pepper sauce until the potatoes drink up everything around them. Each portion lands at 587 calories and 34 grams of protein for about $2.25, and it comes out of the oven looking like somebody worked a lot harder than you did.

      Shop Smart

      • The ground turkey is over half this batch cost by itself. Buy the 4 pound pack instead of two smaller ones, freeze what you do not use that day, and you have a head start on the next batch.
      • Canned crushed and diced tomatoes are the sauce here, and they are the ingredient most worth buying deep. Store brand, biggest can, whatever is on the shelf tag that week. Nothing about this sauce cares whose name is on the label.
      • Potatoes are cheapest by the bag, not by the each. A 7 pound bag runs close to what a handful of loose ones cost, and potatoes keep for weeks in a cool dark spot.
      • The bread crumbs, tomato paste, and both oils are pantry items that outlive this batch by a mile. You pay for the container once and it shows up in a dozen recipes after this one.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Grate or finely mince the onion, then mix it with the ground turkey, bread crumbs, eggs, cumin, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Work it just until it holds together and no further.
      3. Roll the mixture into small oval kofte, about the size of your thumb, and set them on a sheet pan.
      4. Peel the potatoes and cut them into thick slices, then toss them with the vegetable oil and a pinch of salt. Spread them on a second sheet pan.
      5. Roast the kofte and the potatoes at 400F for about 20 minutes, until the meat is browned and cooked through to 165F and the potato edges are starting to color.
      6. While those roast, build the sauce: warm the olive oil in a pot, soften the sliced garlic and bell peppers for 5 minutes, stir in the tomato paste for a minute, then add the crushed and diced tomatoes with salt and pepper. Simmer 15 minutes.
      7. Layer the potatoes and kofte in your baking pans, pour the sauce over everything, and bake at 375F for 30 to 35 minutes until the sauce is bubbling and thick.
      8. Let it sit 10 minutes before you touch it, then transfer the whole bake into your weighed pot and stir gently so every scoop gets sauce.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is your per-container gram target. Scoop it into 14 containers, making sure each one gets kofte, potato, and sauce instead of a lucky draw. Each portion lands at 587 calories and 34 grams of protein.
