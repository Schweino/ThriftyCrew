      Makes 14 servings · ~565 cal · 34g protein · 70g carbs · 15g fat · ~$3.11 per serving.

      Grilled carne asada does not batch. You cannot sear fourteen servings to order on a Sunday afternoon and have any of it left good by Wednesday. The slow cooker version solves that: the same citrus, garlic, and ancho marinade, but braised until it shreds instead of grilled until it chars. Each bowl carries 34 grams of protein and 565 calories for about $3.11.

      Shop Smart

      • Bottled lime and lemon juice work fine in a long braise. The acid is doing structural work here, not sitting on top as a fresh squeeze.
      • Green bell peppers cost less than red. Buy at least one red for color if you want the bowls to look good, and make the rest green.
      • One dried ancho chile carries the whole pot. Buy the bag from the Latin foods aisle, use one, and keep the rest for the other chile recipes in this section.
      • Cilantro goes in the marinade and on top. One bunch handles both if you use the stems in the pot and save the leaves for finishing.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Stem and seed the ancho chile, cover it with hot water, and soak 20 minutes until soft.
      3. Blend the soaked chile with the lime juice, lemon juice, soy sauce, red wine vinegar, olive oil, garlic, jalapeno, half the cilantro, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
      4. Put the steak in the slow cooker, pour the marinade over it, and add the bay leaves.
      5. Cover and cook on low for 7 hours, until the beef shreds under light pressure.
      6. Slice the onion and the bell peppers into strips and stir them in for the last 45 minutes so they soften without disappearing.
      7. Shred the beef in the cooker and let it sit in the juices for 10 minutes so it drinks some back up.
      8. Cook the rice separately, finish with the remaining cilantro, and keep the sour cream cold for serving.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Spoon that weight of shredded beef and peppers over the same weight of rice in each of 14 containers, with the sour cream packed separately. Each bowl lands at 565 calories with 34 grams of protein.
