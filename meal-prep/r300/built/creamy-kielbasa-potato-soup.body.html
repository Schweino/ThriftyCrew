      Makes 14 servings · ~626 cal · 26g protein · 48g carbs · 37g fat · ~$2.22 per serving.

      A thick, smoky potato soup with browned sausage in every spoonful, built on a real roux instead of a can of condensed anything. Potatoes, carrots, celery and corn keep it cheap all year, and the smoked sausage gives it a flavor that reads far more expensive than it is.       Shop Smart

      • The sausage is most of this recipe's cost. Everything else combined runs about a third of it. This is the dish to make the week smoked sausage goes on sale.
      • Heavy cream is the second line worth watching. Two pints carries the batch. If you want it cheaper, evaporated milk gives you similar body for a fraction of the price.
      • Canned corn is the cheapest thing on this list and it adds real sweetness. Frozen works too. Either way it earns its place in the pot.
      • Flour and butter make the thickener. A roux costs pennies and gives you far better texture than a slurry or a can of soup ever will.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Slice the smoked sausage into coins, dice the onion, carrots, celery and potatoes, and mince the garlic.
      3. Heat the olive oil in a large pot and brown the sausage coins until the edges get dark. Take them out and leave the fat behind, because that fat is flavor.
      4. Melt the butter in the same pot and cook the onion, carrots and celery until they soften, then add the garlic for a minute.
      5. Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir constantly for a couple of minutes so it cooks out instead of tasting like paste.
      6. Pour in the beef broth slowly while whisking, then add the diced potatoes, paprika, italian seasoning, salt and black pepper.
      7. Simmer until the potatoes are soft enough to mash against the side of the pot, then stir in the corn.
      8. Return the sausage, turn the heat low, and stir in the heavy cream. Warm it through without letting it boil so it stays smooth.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on your scale and subtract the empty pot weight from Step 1. Divide that total by 14 and ladle the exact amount into each container, stirring the pot between scoops so the sausage and potatoes stay even. Every bowl lands near 626 calories and 26 grams of protein.
