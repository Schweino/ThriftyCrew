      Shop Smart

      • Cannellini beans are the reason the protein number is so high without the cost following it up. Beans are the most underrated line item in any grocery budget.
      • The turkey is nearly half this batch. Buy the 5 pound pack, brown all of it, and freeze half already cooked if this batch does not need it.
      • Small pasta shells cost next to nothing here. Cook them separately and store them apart from the soup, because pasta sitting in broth all week drinks the whole pot dry.
      • Beef broth is a real line because a soup this size needs a lot of it. Bouillon base makes the same amount for a fraction, and in a pot with this much tomato and parmesan, nobody will ever know.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Brown the ground turkey in the olive oil in a skillet, breaking it into crumbles, until it is cooked through to 165F. A slow cooker will never brown meat, so this ten minutes on the stove is what keeps the soup from tasting flat.
      3. Add the minced garlic to the pan for one minute, then scrape everything into the slow cooker.
      4. Add the diced tomatoes, the beef broth, the dried basil, oregano, salt, and black pepper, and stir it together.
      5. Cover and cook on low for 6 hours, or on high for 3.
      6. Add the diced zucchini and the drained cannellini beans in the last 45 minutes, so the zucchini stays intact and the beans do not turn to paste.
      7. Cook the pasta shells separately in salted water, drain them, and toss them with a drizzle of oil so they do not clump in the fridge.
      8. Taste the soup for salt, then move the pasta into your weighed pot.

      Portion It

      Set the pot of drained pasta on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is the pasta in each container. Ladle the soup evenly over all 14, stirring the pot between scoops so the beans and turkey do not settle, then grate the parmesan over the top. Each bowl lands at 563 calories and 47 grams of protein.
