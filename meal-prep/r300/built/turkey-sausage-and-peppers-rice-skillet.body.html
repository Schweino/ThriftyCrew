      Makes 14 servings · ~620 cal · 26g protein · 72g carbs · 25g fat · ~$2.30 per serving.

      Sausage and peppers is street fair food, the smell that pulls you across a parking lot in July. Usually it lands on a hoagie roll. Here it goes over rice cooked in the same pan, which means all the sausage fat and pepper juice end up in the grain instead of soaking through bread. Each serving runs 620 calories with 26 grams of protein for about $2.30. A spoonful of pesto stirred in at the end does more for it than any amount of extra seasoning.

      Shop Smart

      • Red bell peppers cost noticeably more than green ones. If the price gap is wide that week, swap half of them for green and the dish loses nothing but a little sweetness.
      • Smoked turkey sausage ropes freeze for months. This recipe uses four and a half pounds, so buy them when they cycle on sale and stack the freezer.
      • Basil pesto in a jar looks expensive per ounce but you use very little and the jar keeps for weeks in the fridge under a film of oil. It is worth the line item.
      • Fresh basil is optional if the packs look sad. The pesto is already carrying the basil flavor, so dried oregano alone will do the job.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Slice the smoked turkey sausage into coins and brown them in the olive oil in your largest deep skillet over medium-high until both cut faces are dark, then set them aside.
      3. Add the sliced red onion and the red bell pepper strips to the same pan and cook 8 minutes, letting them pick up color from the browned bits on the bottom.
      4. Stir in the garlic, dried oregano, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper and cook one minute until it smells like a pizzeria.
      5. Add the dry rice and stir for two minutes so every grain gets coated in the seasoned fat. This step is what keeps the rice separate rather than sticky.
      6. Pour in the chicken broth, return the sausage to the pan, bring it to a boil, then cover and simmer on low for 18 to 20 minutes without lifting the lid.
      7. Take the pan off the heat and let it stand covered for 10 minutes so the last of the liquid absorbs and the rice finishes steaming.
      8. Fluff with a fork, stir in the basil pesto until it is evenly distributed, and finish with torn fresh basil.

      Portion It

      Weigh the skillet, subtract the empty pan weight you noted, and divide by 14. Stir from the bottom before each scoop so the sausage coins get spread across all the containers. Each serving comes to 620 calories and 26 grams of protein.
