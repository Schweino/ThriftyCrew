      Makes 14 servings · ~572 cal · 37g protein · 52g carbs · 24g fat · ~$2.78 per serving.

      Lasagna is wonderful and lasagna is a project. This is the version that skips the project: jumbo shells instead of flat noodles, everything stirred and baked in one pan, ricotta and mozzarella melted right through it. Nobody at your table is going to file a complaint about the shape. Each portion runs 572 calories and 37 grams of protein for about $2.78.

      Shop Smart

      • Jumbo shells are the surprise big line on this list. Any large pasta shape works here since it all gets stirred together, so grab whatever big shape is cheapest that week.
      • Ricotta by the 15 ounce tub is the budget size. Buy three, use them all, and skip the smaller containers that charge more per ounce for less cheese.
      • Jarred marinara beats making sauce from scratch on both price and time once you count the canned tomatoes, the herbs, and the hour. Buy the store brand and taste it before you judge it.
      • Reduced fat mozzarella in the 8 ounce bag melts fine in a covered bake. Shred-your-own from a block is cheaper still if your store prices blocks below bags, which about half of them do.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the jumbo shells to just under done, about 2 minutes short of the box time, then drain them. They finish in the oven and overcooked pasta in a bake turns to paste.
      3. Heat the olive oil in a large pot, cook the diced onion 5 minutes, add the garlic for a minute, then brown the ground turkey until it is cooked through to 165F.
      4. Add the marinara sauce, the chicken broth, the italian seasoning, red pepper flakes, salt, and black pepper, and simmer 10 minutes so it thickens up.
      5. Stir the ricotta together with the eggs and the parmesan in a separate bowl. The egg is what keeps the ricotta from weeping water into your casserole.
      6. Fold the drained shells into the meat sauce, then dollop the ricotta mixture through it and stir just twice so you get ribbons of white instead of a uniform pink.
      7. Spread it in your baking pans, cover with the mozzarella, and bake at 375F for 30 minutes until it bubbles at the edges and the cheese browns in spots.
      8. Let it rest 15 minutes, cook the rice, and move the rice into your weighed pot.

      Portion It

      Set the pot of rice on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is the base layer in each container. Then cut the casserole into 14 even squares and lay one on each bed so the portions come out honest instead of eyeballed. Every serving lands at 572 calories and 37 grams of protein.
