      Makes 14 servings · ~566 cal · 34g protein · 64g carbs · 18g fat · ~$1.60 per serving.

      Chicken Divan started life at a fancy New York hotel and got adopted by every church potluck in America, which is usually a sign a dish is genuinely good. Chicken and broccoli under a cheddar and sour cream sauce, topped with buttered bread crumbs and baked until it bubbles at the edges. You mix it cold, put it in the oven, and walk away. Each serving lands at 566 calories and 34 grams of protein for about $1.60 a serving.

      Shop Smart

      • Frozen broccoli florets cost less than fresh, are already trimmed, and hold their texture better in a casserole than fresh does. Thaw and drain them well, because the water they release is the number one cause of a soupy divan.
      • Buy block cheddar and shred it yourself when it beats the bag on price. The block also melts smoother, since bagged shreds are coated with starch to keep them separate.
      • Cream of mushroom soup on sale is worth stocking up on. It keeps for years and it is the backbone of half the casseroles in the American repertoire.
      • Light sour cream costs the same as regular and works exactly the same way in a baked dish, so this is one of the few places where the lighter version is a free upgrade.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the oven to 350F. Thaw the broccoli and press it dry in a towel. Do not skip the drying step, because every drop of water you leave in ends up thinning the sauce.
      3. Cook the chicken breast, then cut or shred it into bite sized pieces.
      4. In a large bowl, stir together the cream of mushroom soup, sour cream, milk, most of the shredded cheddar, garlic powder, onion powder, dijon mustard, salt, and pepper.
      5. Fold the chicken and broccoli into the sauce until everything is coated evenly.
      6. Spread it into a large baking dish and scatter the rest of the cheddar over the top.
      7. Toss the bread crumbs with the melted butter and sprinkle them over the cheese. This is what gives you a crunchy top instead of a soft one.
      8. Bake 30 to 35 minutes until it bubbles at the edges and the crumbs are golden. Cook the rice while it bakes and rest the dish 10 minutes before cutting.

      Portion It

      Weigh the rice into each container first, then score the bake into 14 even squares and lift one into each. Set the dish on the scale and subtract the empty weight if you want to check your squares are honest. Every portion lands at 566 calories. Reheat uncovered so the crumb topping crisps back up.
