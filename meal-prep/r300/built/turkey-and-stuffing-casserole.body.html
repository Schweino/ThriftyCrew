      Shop Smart

      • Stuffing mix goes on deep discount every November and keeps in the box for a year or more. Buy a stack then and you are cooking this dish at a fraction of the price all spring.
      • A frozen turkey breast roast is the cheapest way into this recipe outside the holidays. Roast one on a Sunday, dice it, and you have the protein for two batches.
      • Greek yogurt is standing in for sour cream in the gravy and costs less per ounce in the 32 oz tub. It also drops the fat without hurting the texture.
      • Frozen peas over canned, always. The store-brand 12 oz bags are the cheapest vegetable in this recipe and they hold their shape through the bake.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Melt the butter with the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat, sprinkle in the flour, and whisk for two minutes until it smells nutty instead of raw.
      3. Pour in the chicken broth and the milk slowly, whisking the whole time, and cook until the gravy thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.
      4. Season the gravy with the onion powder, garlic powder, poultry seasoning, salt, black pepper, and a few dashes of hot sauce, then take it off the heat.
      5. Stir in the Greek yogurt and the shredded cheddar off the heat so the yogurt does not break, then fold in the diced turkey breast and the frozen peas.
      6. Spread the filling into a greased 9x13 pan and level it out.
      7. Prepare the stuffing mix per the box directions but hold back a little of the liquid, then spoon it over the filling in an even layer and press it down lightly.
      8. Bake at 375F for 30 to 35 minutes, uncovered, until the stuffing is browned on top and the gravy bubbles at the edges. Rest 10 minutes before portioning.

      Portion It

      Set the pan on the scale, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Weigh each square into its container instead of guessing, since the stuffing layer makes the portions look bigger than they are. Every serving comes to 575 calories and 39 grams of protein.
