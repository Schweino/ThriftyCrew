      Makes 14 servings · ~584 cal · 35g protein · 68g carbs · 19g fat · ~$2.62 per serving.

      Cottage pie is the original stretch a pound of beef dinner. Ground beef and a chopped vegetable base go under a thick blanket of mashed potato, the whole thing bakes until the top browns, and it cuts into clean squares which makes portioning almost too easy. Shepherd's pie is the lamb version; this is the beef one. Each square carries 35 grams of protein and 584 calories for about $2.62.

      Shop Smart

      • Potatoes are the largest volume ingredient. Buy the ten pound bag, because the per pound price falls sharply as the bag gets bigger and these keep for weeks.
      • Russets mash lighter and cost less than yellow or red potatoes. For a topping that gets baked, russet is both the cheaper and the better choice.
      • Ground beef is the only expensive line. Buy the club size pack when it is on promotion and freeze it in batch sized portions the day you bring it home.
      • A small amount of parmesan on top is what gives the crust its color and salt. Buy the tub rather than a wedge if you are only using it as a finisher.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Peel and cube the potatoes and boil them in salted water until a knife slides through with no resistance.
      3. While they cook, brown the ground beef in the olive oil in a wide pot over medium high heat, breaking it up as it goes, until no pink remains.
      4. Add the chopped onion, carrots, celery, and garlic and cook until softened, about 8 minutes.
      5. Stir in the flour and tomato paste and cook 2 minutes, then pour in the beef broth and Worcestershire sauce with the thyme and bay leaves.
      6. Simmer 20 to 25 minutes until the filling is thick enough to hold a line when you drag a spoon through it. Pull the bay leaves.
      7. Drain and mash the potatoes with the milk and butter, season them well, and spread them over the filling in a large baking dish or two sheet pans.
      8. Scatter the parmesan on top and bake at 400F for 25 to 30 minutes, until the top is browned and the edges bubble.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pan, subtract the empty-pan number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Easier still, score the baked pie into 14 equal squares with a knife and lift them out one at a time. Each serving lands at 584 calories with 35 grams of protein.
