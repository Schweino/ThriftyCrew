      Makes 14 servings · ~565 cal · 29g protein · 72g carbs · 15g fat · ~$2.08 per serving.

      There is a kind of cooking where you brown one thing, dump everything else in a pot, put the lid on, and walk away for four hours. That is this. Turkey, rice, enchilada sauce, beans, corn and peppers all cook together in the slow cooker, and cheese goes on at the very end. Each serving lands at 565 calories with 29 grams of protein for about $2.08. It is the easiest fourteen-serving batch in this entire collection.

      Shop Smart

      • Canned enchilada sauce is the flavor engine here and this recipe uses five cans. It goes on sale constantly and keeps for a year, so buy it deep when the price drops.
      • Rice from the big bag is the cheapest thing in the pot. Use plain long grain, not instant, because instant rice turns to mush over a long slow cook.
      • Buy the peppers loose and use both red and green. Green costs less and the mix gives you the color without paying red bell pepper prices across the board.
      • Brown the turkey before it goes in the crock. It costs you one skillet and ten minutes, and it is the difference between a dish with depth and a dish that tastes boiled.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet and brown the ground turkey hard, breaking it into crumbles. This is the only stovetop step and it is worth every minute.
      3. Add the diced onion to the turkey and cook 5 minutes, then stir in the chili powder, ground cumin, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper and cook one minute more.
      4. Scrape the turkey mixture into the slow cooker and add the dry rice, the enchilada sauce, the drained black beans, the diced tomatoes with their juice, and the drained corn.
      5. Stir in the diced red and green bell peppers and mix everything thoroughly so the rice is fully submerged. Rice sitting above the liquid line will stay hard.
      6. Cover and cook on low for 4 to 5 hours or on high for 2 to 3, until the rice is tender and has absorbed nearly all the liquid.
      7. Resist lifting the lid while it cooks. Every peek costs you fifteen to twenty minutes of cooking time in a slow cooker.
      8. Scatter the Mexican cheese blend over the top, replace the lid, and let it melt for 10 minutes off the heat before portioning.

      Portion It

      Weigh the crock insert, subtract the empty insert weight, and divide by 14. Stir from the bottom before each scoop, because the rice settles heavy and the peppers float. Each serving comes to 565 calories and 29 grams of protein.
