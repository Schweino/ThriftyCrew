      Shop Smart

      • Rice is the cheapest base we track. It fills out this whole skillet for a rounding error. Buy the big bag and it costs even less per batch.
      • Frozen broccoli florets beat fresh on price and waste. No stems to trim, no crisper drawer casualties, and they cook straight from the bag.
      • Red peppers are the expensive produce line. Green peppers cost a fraction and taste fine in a cheesy skillet. Swap them when the price gap is wide.
      • Shred your own cheddar from a block. The pre shredded bags cost more per ounce and the anti caking starch keeps the cheese from melting into the rice the way you want.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice per the package directions and set it aside. Slightly undercooked is better than mushy since it will get stirred and reheated.
      3. Slice the smoked sausage into coins, dice the onion, chop the red bell pepper and mince the garlic.
      4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet and brown the sausage coins until the edges darken, then set them aside.
      5. Melt the butter in the same pan and cook the onion and pepper until they soften, then add the garlic, garlic powder, onion powder, salt and black pepper.
      6. Add the broccoli florets and cook until they turn bright green and just tender. Overcooked broccoli goes gray and mealy in the fridge.
      7. Fold the cooked rice and the browned sausage back into the skillet and stir everything together over low heat.
      8. Kill the heat, scatter the shredded cheddar over the top, and cover for a couple of minutes so it melts before you stir it through.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on your scale and subtract the empty pot weight from Step 1. Divide that total by 14 and scoop the exact amount into each container, stirring between scoops so the sausage does not settle. Every bowl lands near 727 calories and 29 grams of protein.
