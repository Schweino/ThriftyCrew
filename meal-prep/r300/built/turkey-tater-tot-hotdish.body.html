Makes 14 servings · ~563 cal · 28g protein · 33g carbs · 36g fat · ~$3.03 per serving (at everyday cost).

In Minnesota and the Dakotas this is not a casserole, it is a hotdish, and correcting people on that is practically a regional sport. Seasoned ground turkey and a creamy mushroom base underneath, a full armor of tater tots on top, baked until the tots go golden and crackly. Each serving runs 563 calories with 28 grams of protein for about $3.03 (at everyday cost). It is the friendliest dish in this whole collection to make on a Sunday and eat all week.

Ingredients

  • 93/7 Ground Turkey (Jennie-O): 3 lb (1334 g)
  • Yellow Onion: 1.5 (162 g)
  • Garlic: 3.5 tbsp (29 g)
  • Olive Oil: 3 tbsp (40 g)
  • Salt (Morton): 0.5 tbsp (9 g)
  • Black Pepper (Great Value): 1.75 tbsp (12 g)
  • Smoked Paprika (Great Value): 0.5 tbsp (4 g)
  • Cream of Mushroom Soup (Campbell's (USDA SR)): 3 cans (876 g)
  • Cheddar Cheese, Shredded (Great Value): 11.75 oz (332 g)
  • Tater Tots (Ore-Ida (Kraft Heinz)): 78.25 oz (2217 g)

    About $3.03 a serving (at everyday cost), and most of that is the turkey. Tater tots, canned soup, and shredded cheddar are three of the steadiest low-price items in the freezer and center aisles, which is exactly why this dish has fed the upper Midwest for sixty years.

    Shop Smart

    • The single large bag of tater tots beats buying several small ones almost every time. Check the per-ounce tag, then buy the big bag and keep what you do not use frozen for the next batch.
    • Cream of mushroom soup is the base and you need three cans. Store-brand condensed soup performs the same in a bake, since it is getting stirred into meat and covered in cheese either way.
    • Buy the block-free route on cheese only if it is cheaper. Pre-shredded 8 oz bags are usually the better deal on cheddar, and a hotdish does not care about the anti-caking starch.
    • Ground turkey is the whole cost story here. Watch for it to hit a sale price and buy several packs, because it freezes for months without losing anything.

    Make It

    1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
    2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high and brown the ground turkey, breaking it into small crumbles as it cooks.
    3. Add the diced onion and the garlic, cook 5 minutes until the onion turns soft and translucent, then season with salt, black pepper, and smoked paprika.
    4. Turn off the heat and stir in all three cans of condensed cream of mushroom soup along with about half the shredded cheddar. Do not thin it with water; the tots release moisture as they bake.
    5. Spread the turkey mixture into a greased 9x13 pan and press it flat so the tot layer sits level.
    6. Arrange the tater tots over the top in tight rows, edge to edge. A packed layer bakes into a crust; a scattered one just steams.
    7. Bake at 400F for 40 to 45 minutes, until the tots are deep golden and the edges of the pan are bubbling.
    8. Scatter the rest of the cheddar over the tots, return the pan for 5 minutes to melt it, then rest 10 minutes before portioning.

    Portion It

    Put the pan on the scale, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Cut squares to that weight rather than eyeballing them, because the corners always look bigger than they are. Every serving comes to 563 calories and 28 grams of protein.
