      Makes 14 servings · ~574 cal · 33g protein · 57g carbs · 22g fat · ~$1.37 per serving.

      Maqluba means upside down in Arabic, and the traditional finish is a piece of theater: the whole pot gets flipped onto a platter at the table and everyone holds their breath. For meal prep we skip the drama and scoop it out, which changes nothing about the taste. What matters is the layering, chicken on the bottom, then vegetables, then rice, all cooking in one pot with allspice and cinnamon running through it. Each bowl comes in at 574 calories and 33 grams of protein for about $1.37.

      Shop Smart

      • Eggplant is the priciest vegetable in this recipe and it swings hard with the season. If it is expensive the week you shop, lean harder on the broccoli florets, which are tracked cheap and roast the same way.
      • Frozen broccoli florets are cheaper than fresh, need no trimming, and roast perfectly well straight from the bag. This is one of the places where frozen genuinely beats fresh on both price and convenience.
      • Almonds go on top for crunch. Buy the big bag rather than a small pouch, toast them yourself in a dry pan, and the rest of the bag will feed a month of snacks.
      • Allspice and cinnamon are the signature of Palestinian rice dishes, and both are far cheaper in the bagged section of an international grocer than in a branded jar on the spice aisle.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear and leave it to soak while you cook everything else.
      3. Cube the eggplant, toss it with some of the olive oil and salt, and roast it at 425F for 20 to 25 minutes with the broccoli florets on a second pan until both are browned at the edges. Roasting instead of frying saves you a lot of oil.
      4. Meanwhile, brown the chicken thighs in the rest of the olive oil in your largest heavy pot over medium-high, seasoned with the ground allspice, ground cinnamon, garam masala, ground turmeric, salt and black pepper.
      5. Add the diced onion and garlic to the pot for 4 minutes, then the diced tomatoes and bay leaves. Cook 5 minutes until the tomato darkens.
      6. Layer it properly: chicken and sauce on the bottom of the pot, then the roasted eggplant and broccoli, then the drained rice spread evenly across the top. Do not stir it. The layers are the recipe.
      7. Pour hot water or broth gently over the back of a spoon until the liquid sits about an inch above the rice. Cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat 30 to 35 minutes, then rest it covered another 15 minutes off the heat.
      8. Scoop it out in sections rather than flipping it, toasting the almonds in a dry pan while you work, and scatter them over the top.

      Portion It

      Set the pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Fourteen containers, no arguments. Each one carries 574 calories, and hold the toasted almonds in a separate bag so they stay crunchy through the week.
