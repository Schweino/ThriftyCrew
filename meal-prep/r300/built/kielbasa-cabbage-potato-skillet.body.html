      Makes 14 servings · ~550 cal · 26g protein · 71g carbs · 20g fat · ~$1.88 per serving.

      Six ingredients, one skillet, and nothing in it costs much. Diced potatoes crisped in olive oil, cabbage cooked down until it sweetens, garlic and onion, and browned coins of smoked sausage tying it all together. It lands at 26 grams of protein for about $1.88, and it reheats better than most skillet meals because the potatoes hold their shape.

      Shop Smart

      • Buy potatoes in the nine pound bag. This recipe eats a lot of them, and the big bag runs close to half the per pound price of loose potatoes.
      • Smoked sausage is the one real expense. It is about three quarters of the batch cost. Buy the ropes on sale, freeze them, and this recipe gets a lot cheaper.
      • A few heads of cabbage feed this entire batch. Nothing else in the produce aisle stretches like that for the money, and it keeps for weeks in the crisper.
      • Salt and black pepper are the whole seasoning list. No spice blend to buy. The browning on the sausage and the sweetness of the cooked cabbage do the work.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Dice the potatoes small and even so they cook through at the same rate. Core and slice the cabbage into ribbons, and slice the onion.
      3. Heat the olive oil in your largest skillet and add the potatoes in a single layer. Let them sit undisturbed so they brown before you stir. Cover and cook until tender.
      4. Push the potatoes to the side, add the sliced sausage coins, and brown them so the edges get some color.
      5. Add the onion and cook until it softens, then stir in the garlic just until it smells good.
      6. Pile in the cabbage a handful at a time, letting each addition wilt before adding the next.
      7. Season with salt and black pepper, cover, and cook until the cabbage goes tender and sweet at the edges.
      8. Stir everything together in your big pot, scraping the browned bits off the bottom of the skillet into it.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on your scale and subtract the empty pot weight from Step 1. Divide that total by 14 and scoop the exact amount into each container, stirring between scoops so the sausage stays even. Every bowl lands near 550 calories and 26 grams of protein.
