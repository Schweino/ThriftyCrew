      A la king was a fixture on American hotel menus for decades, then it fell out of fashion and everybody forgot how good it is. Turkey and mushrooms in a cream sauce, peas for color, olives for a salty little surprise, spooned over egg noodles. Each serving carries 40 grams of protein and 727 calories for about $2.64.       Shop Smart

      • White mushrooms in 8 oz packs cost roughly half what pre-sliced baby bellas do and there are eleven cups of them in this recipe. Slicing them yourself is the single biggest saving available here.
      • Heavy cream is the second cost driver. Buy pints rather than half pints, since the price per ounce drops sharply and cream freezes if you do not use it all.
      • Canned sliced olives in the 6 oz cans are far cheaper than anything from the olive bar and they are getting stirred into a cream sauce, where nobody can tell the difference.
      • Two frozen turkey breast roasts cover this batch. Cook both at once and freeze the extra diced meat for another dish.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Melt the butter in your largest pot over medium heat and cook the sliced mushrooms and diced onion for 10 to 12 minutes, until all the mushroom liquid has cooked away and they start to brown.
      3. Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir constantly for two full minutes. This is a big roux and it needs the time to lose its raw taste.
      4. Pour in the chicken broth gradually, whisking hard after each addition so no lumps form, and bring it to a simmer until it thickens.
      5. Stir in the heavy cream and season with the garlic powder, salt, and plenty of black pepper, then let it bubble gently for 5 minutes.
      6. Fold in the diced turkey breast and warm it through without boiling, since hard boiling will make the turkey tighten and go stringy.
      7. Add the frozen peas and the sliced olives at the very end and let the residual heat warm them for 3 to 4 minutes, which keeps the peas bright instead of gray.
      8. Cook egg noodles separately and spoon the a la king over them just before portioning, so the noodles do not swell and drink the sauce overnight.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pot of a la king, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Ladle that weight over each portion of noodles so the sauce, not the noodles, sets the serving size. Each serving comes to 727 calories and 40 grams of protein.
