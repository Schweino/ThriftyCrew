      Makes 14 servings · ~604 cal · 26g protein · 41g carbs · 37g fat · ~$1.50 per serving.

      This is the soup that a certain endless-breadstick chain is famous for, rebuilt at home for a fraction of what a bowl of it costs at the restaurant. Crisped bacon, hot Italian sausage, tender potatoes and kale in a cream broth. It lands at 26 grams of protein for about $1.50, and the honest truth is the homemade version tastes better because you control how much sausage goes in.

      Shop Smart

      • A quarter pound of bacon is all you need. You want the rendered fat and the smoke, not a plate of bacon. Buy a pound and use the rest elsewhere this week.
      • Heavy cream is the biggest non meat line. Two pints carries the batch. Evaporated milk is a cheaper swap that still gives you body if the cream price is ugly.
      • Potatoes in the six pound bag. This uses a lot of them and bagged runs close to half the loose price. Russets break down slightly and thicken the broth on their own.
      • Kale wilts down to almost nothing. Two bunches look like a mountain and disappear into the pot, so do not be tempted to buy four.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Chop the bacon, dice the onion, mince the garlic, slice the potatoes into rounds and strip the kale leaves from their stems.
      3. Render the chopped bacon in a large pot until it crisps, then lift it out and leave the fat behind.
      4. Brown the hot Italian sausage in the bacon fat, breaking it into crumbles, until it is cooked through and browned in spots. Drain off any excess grease.
      5. Add the diced onion and cook until soft, then add the garlic and italian seasoning for a minute.
      6. Pour in the chicken broth, add the sliced potatoes, and simmer until they are tender enough to cut with a spoon.
      7. Turn the heat down and stir in the heavy cream, keeping it below a boil so it does not separate.
      8. Fold in the kale and the crisped bacon, cook just until the kale wilts, then season with salt and black pepper.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on your scale and subtract the empty pot weight from Step 1. Divide that total by 14 and ladle the exact amount into each container, stirring as you go so the sausage and potatoes stay even. Every bowl lands near 604 calories and 26 grams of protein.
