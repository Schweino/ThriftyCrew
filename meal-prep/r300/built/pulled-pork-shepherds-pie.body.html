      Makes 14 servings · ~564 cal · 36g protein · 38g carbs · 29g fat · ~$2.04 per serving.

      A shepherd's pie is a filling under a mashed potato roof, and once you understand that, the filling is up for negotiation. This one uses slow cooked pulled pork in a tomato and balsamic gravy with carrots, peas and corn, all of it under a layer of mashed potatoes with cream and butter worked in. It bakes until the top browns in ridges. Of everything in this section, this is the dish that reheats closest to the way it came out of the oven.

      Shop Smart

      • Russet potatoes in the big bag make the best mash. They are starchy, they whip up fluffy, and they are the least expensive potato in the store by a wide margin.
      • Frozen peas and canned corn are pennies a serving. They add color, sweetness and bulk to the filling so a smaller amount of pork goes further.
      • Balsamic vinegar and Worcestershire are the depth. Between them, two cheap bottles give this gravy the savory backbone that usually requires wine or hours of reduction.
      • Run a fork across the top before it bakes. Those ridges are what brown, and browning is flavor you get for free.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Season the pork shoulder with salt, black pepper and Italian seasoning, sear it in the olive oil, then slow cook it until it shreds and pull it apart.
      3. Boil the peeled and quartered potatoes in salted water until they fall off a knife, then drain them well and let the steam escape for a minute.
      4. Mash the potatoes with the butter and heavy cream, season with salt and black pepper, and stop as soon as they are smooth. Overworked potatoes turn gluey.
      5. Cook the diced onion and sliced carrots in a skillet until soft, add the minced garlic, then sprinkle the flour over and stir for two minutes.
      6. Add the diced tomatoes, the beef broth, the balsamic vinegar and the Worcestershire sauce and simmer until it thickens into a gravy, then fold in the pulled pork, the frozen peas and the corn.
      7. Spread the filling in a large baking dish, spoon the mashed potatoes over the top and rake ridges into them with a fork, then bake at 400F for 30 minutes until browned and bubbling.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pie in its dish, subtract the dish weight, divide by 14, and cut portions to that exact weight so nobody gets all potato and no filling. Every serving lands near 564 calories with 36 grams of protein.
