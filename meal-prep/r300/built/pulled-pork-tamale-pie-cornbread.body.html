      Makes 14 servings · ~584 cal · 26g protein · 55g carbs · 28g fat · ~$1.22 per serving.

      Tamale pie is what happens when somebody looks at the four hours it takes to make real tamales and decides there has to be a faster way. There is. Put the shredded pork and green chiles in a pan, pour a corn batter over the top, and bake it. You get the same corn and chile and pork combination, with a soft golden crust instead of a husk, and you get it in an afternoon instead of a weekend with your whole family in the kitchen.

      Shop Smart

      • Grits are the cheap route to a corn topping. They are the same ground corn as cornmeal at a lower price per pound in most stores. Check both shelf tags and buy whichever is cheaper.
      • Canned diced green chiles are the whole flavor. They cost about a dollar a can and they bring the mild heat that makes this read as tamale pie instead of just cornbread over meat.
      • Pork loin works here and costs less than shoulder. Because it is shredded into a saucy filling rather than served in slices, the leaner cut does not hurt you.
      • Sour cream in the batter is worth the line item. It keeps the corn topping from drying out, which is the one thing that ruins a tamale pie on day three.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Season the pork loin with salt, chili powder, garlic powder, ground cumin, dried oregano, paprika and black pepper, sear it in the vegetable oil, then slow cook it until it shreds easily.
      3. Shred the pork, drain most of the liquid, and stir in the canned diced green chiles.
      4. Spread the pork and chile mixture across the bottom of a large greased baking dish and scatter about half the shredded cheddar over it.
      5. Whisk the grits, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt together in a bowl.
      6. In another bowl whisk the eggs with the heavy cream, the melted butter and the sour cream, then stir the wet into the dry just until it comes together. Do not overmix or the topping goes tough.
      7. Pour the batter over the pork, spread it to the edges, top with the remaining cheddar, and bake at 375F for 35 to 40 minutes until the top is golden and set.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pie in its dish, subtract the dish weight, divide by 14, and cut squares to that exact weight so nobody gets all crust and no filling. Every serving lands near 584 calories with 26 grams of protein.
