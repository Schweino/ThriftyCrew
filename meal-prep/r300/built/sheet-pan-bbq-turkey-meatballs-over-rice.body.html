      Makes 14 servings · ~568 cal · 29g protein · 78g carbs · 14g fat · ~$2.00 per serving.

      Meatballs on a sheet pan are one of the great meal prep discoveries. No frying, no splatter, no standing at the stove turning them one at a time. They roast alongside pineapple, onion and red pepper, everything gets glazed in barbecue sauce, and it all goes over rice. Each serving lands at 568 calories with 29 grams of protein for about $2.00.       Shop Smart

      • Canned pineapple chunks in juice cost a fraction of fresh pineapple and roast better because they are already tender. Drain them well or they will steam the pan.
      • Barbecue sauce is one of the most heavily discounted condiments in the store, especially around summer holidays. Buy the store brand and buy it on sale.
      • Bread crumbs bind the meatballs and cost pennies per batch by the canister. Do not buy the seasoned kind at a premium; you are adding garlic powder and paprika anyway.
      • Rice from the big bag is the cheapest base you can serve anything over, and this recipe needs less than four cups dry to feed all fourteen containers.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Mix the ground turkey with the eggs, bread crumbs, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper using your hands, stopping the moment it comes together.
      3. Roll the mixture into golf-ball sized meatballs and arrange them on two oiled sheet pans with space between each one. Crowded meatballs steam instead of browning.
      4. Scatter the drained pineapple chunks, the onion wedges, and the red bell pepper pieces around the meatballs, then drizzle everything with the vegetable oil.
      5. Roast at 425F for 20 minutes, until the meatballs are browned and the pineapple edges start to caramelize.
      6. Brush the meatballs and the vegetables generously with the barbecue sauce and return the pans for 8 to 10 minutes so the glaze tightens and the centers reach 165F.
      7. Cook the rice while the pans are in the oven, so everything comes together hot at the same time.
      8. Spoon the meatballs, pineapple, and peppers over the rice and finish with the sliced green onions.

      Portion It

      Weigh the rice and the sheet pan contents together, subtract your empty pot weight, and divide by 14. Count the meatballs out evenly first, then let the scale settle the rice. Each serving delivers 568 calories and 29 grams of protein.
