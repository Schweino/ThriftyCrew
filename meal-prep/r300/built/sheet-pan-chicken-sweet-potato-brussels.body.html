      Makes 14 servings · ~568 cal · 53g protein · 38g carbs · 22g fat · ~$2.46 per serving.

      People call sheet-pan dinners lazy. I call them efficient. Chicken, sweet potatoes, brussels sprouts, red onion and apple all roast on the same hot metal while you go do something more interesting, and the oven does the browning that a skillet would make you earn one batch at a time. Each container lands 568 calories and 53 grams of protein for about $2.46. There is a good reason that number sits where it does, and I will tell you exactly what it is in a minute.

      Shop Smart

      • Brussels sprouts are the biggest produce line in this recipe by a wide margin. Buy them loose by the pound instead of trimmed in a bag, and buy them in fall and winter when they are in season and the per-pound price falls through the floor.
      • Chicken thighs are the other heavy line. The big club packs run cheaper per pound than the tray packs, so buy the biggest pack you will use before it turns. Thighs are already the budget cut here, and they stay juicy in a hot oven where breast would dry out.
      • Sweet potatoes and red onion are the cheap seats here. Buy them by the bag rather than by the each, since the bag price per pound is almost always better and both keep for weeks in a cool cabinet.
      • Skip the fancy apples. You want a firm variety that holds its shape in a hot oven, and the loose bin apples do that just as well as the ones with a sticker and a story.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the oven to 425F with racks in the upper and lower thirds, and pull out two large sheet pans.
      3. Peel and cube the sweet potatoes. Toss them with a third of the olive oil, some italian seasoning, the cinnamon, salt and pepper, spread them on one pan, and give them a 15 minute head start in the oven.
      4. While those roast, halve the brussels sprouts, cut the red onion into thick wedges, and core and chop the apples. Toss all three with another third of the oil, more italian seasoning, salt and pepper.
      5. Cut the chicken into large chunks and toss with the last of the oil, the minced garlic, the rest of the italian seasoning, salt and pepper.
      6. Pull the sweet potato pan, push the potatoes to one side, and add the sprouts, onion and apple. Spread the chicken in a single layer on the second pan so it roasts instead of steams.
      7. Roast both pans 18 to 22 minutes, swapping racks halfway, until the chicken reads 165F and the sprouts are dark and crispy at the leaf edges.
      8. Scrape everything into your big mixing pot, pan juices included, and fold it together so the cinnamon and the sweet potato starch coat the whole thing.

      Portion It

      Set the loaded pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide what is left by 14. Scoop that same number into every container so no one gets the light one. Each bowl carries 568 calories and a serious hit of protein, and it reheats better than most roasted vegetables have any right to.
