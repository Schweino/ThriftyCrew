      Makes 14 servings · ~579 cal · 35g protein · 40g carbs · 30g fat · ~$1.68 per serving.

      Biscuits and gravy is a Southern breakfast that has never once apologized for itself. The problem is that it does not travel; biscuits go soggy and gravy separates. So this version builds the whole thing as a strata instead. Cubed bread soaks up an egg custard, seasoned turkey sausage and cheddar go through it, and peppered gravy pours over the top. Each serving carries 35 grams of protein and 579 calories for about $1.68.

      Shop Smart

      • Season plain ground turkey with sage, black pepper, and a pinch of red pepper instead of buying packaged breakfast sausage. You get the same flavor at the price of plain ground meat.
      • Day-old sandwich bread is ideal here and often marked down. Stale bread soaks up custard better than fresh does, so the discount rack is actually the better product.
      • Gravy mix is one of the cheapest shelf-stable items in the store and a container makes many batches. Store brand and name brand taste the same under cheese.
      • Eggs are the cheapest protein per gram in this recipe. Buy two dozen at whatever store has them as a loss leader that week.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Brown the ground turkey in a large skillet over medium-high, breaking it into small crumbles and seasoning it heavily with salt and black pepper so it reads like breakfast sausage.
      3. Cube the sandwich bread into roughly one inch pieces and spread them across a greased and buttered 9x13 pan.
      4. Scatter the browned turkey and about two thirds of the shredded cheddar over the bread cubes and toss lightly so it is not all sitting on top.
      5. Whisk the eggs with about half the milk, a good pinch of salt, and plenty of black pepper, then pour the custard over the pan and press the bread down so every cube gets wet.
      6. Whisk the gravy mix into the remaining milk in a saucepan and cook over medium heat until it thickens, then stir in the melted butter and a heavy hand of black pepper.
      7. Pour the gravy evenly over the top of the pan. Let the whole thing sit 20 minutes so the bread finishes absorbing, or refrigerate it overnight and bake it in the morning.
      8. Bake at 350F for 45 to 50 minutes until the center is set, adding the rest of the cheddar for the last 10 minutes, then rest 15 minutes before portioning.

      Portion It

      Weigh the whole pan, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Strata puffs unevenly, so cut to the scale rather than to the eye. Each serving comes to 579 calories and 35 grams of protein.
