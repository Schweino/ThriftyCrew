      Makes 14 servings · ~578 cal · 32g protein · 82g carbs · 14g fat · ~$2.73 per serving.

      Carbonada is the Chilean answer to a cold week: beef and every vegetable in the drawer, simmered together until the pot goes thick. Traditionally it leans on zapallo squash, and sweet potato plays that role here. Rice cooks right in and turns a soup into dinner. Each bowl carries 32 grams of protein and 578 calories for about $2.73.

      Shop Smart

      • Potatoes are the biggest vegetable line here. Buy the largest bag your household will get through, because the per pound price falls off a cliff as the bag gets bigger.
      • Frozen peas and green beans beat fresh on both price and convenience in a soup. They go in at the end straight from the bag with no prep at all.
      • Sweet potato stands in for the traditional zapallo squash. It brings the same sweetness and body and it is on the shelf year round.
      • One can of corn is plenty. Buy it when the canned vegetables go on the multi can promotion and keep a few in the pantry.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the chopped onion, garlic, red bell pepper, and celery in the vegetable oil until soft, about 8 minutes.
      3. Cut the beef into small cubes, season with salt and pepper, and brown it in the same pot in batches.
      4. Add the paprika, cumin, and oregano and stir for a minute so the spices bloom in the fat.
      5. Cover everything with water, bring it to a simmer, and cook 35 to 40 minutes until the beef starts to give.
      6. Add the potatoes, sweet potatoes, and carrots and simmer 15 minutes more.
      7. Stir in the rice and cook another 15 to 18 minutes, until the rice is tender and the soup has gone properly thick.
      8. Add the green beans, corn, and peas in the last 5 minutes, then taste and correct the salt.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Ladle that weight into each of 14 containers, stirring hard between scoops because everything in this pot sinks. Each bowl lands at 578 calories with 32 grams of protein.
