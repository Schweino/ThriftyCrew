      Shop Smart

      • Chicken breast is the biggest expense, so buy the family pack rather than the small tray. The per pound gap between the two is one of the most consistent savings in the entire store.
      • Barbecue sauce is worth buying by the large bottle. It costs far less per ounce than the small one, it keeps for months, and this recipe uses a real amount of it.
      • Buy block cheese and shred it yourself when it is cheaper than the bags, which it usually is. Bagged shreds also carry anti-caking starch that keeps them from melting as smoothly.
      • Frozen green beans and a bag of potatoes round out the plate for very little. Both hold up better in a container all week than most fresh vegetables would.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the oven to 400F. Season the chicken breasts with salt and pepper and wrap each one in bacon, tucking the ends underneath so they do not unravel in the oven.
      3. Sear the wrapped chicken in a hot dry pan for 2 minutes a side to start rendering the bacon fat. Bacon that goes straight into the oven wrapped around chicken comes out pale and flabby, and this step is the fix.
      4. Arrange the chicken in a large baking dish and brush a generous coat of barbecue sauce over the top of each piece.
      5. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165F in the thickest part.
      6. Scatter the shredded cheddar and mozzarella over the top and return it to the oven for 5 to 8 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbling at the edges.
      7. While the chicken bakes, cut the potatoes into wedges, toss with salt and pepper, and roast them on a separate sheet pan until crisp.
      8. Steam or roast the green beans just until tender and still green, then let everything rest 5 minutes before packing.

      Portion It

      Weigh one piece of chicken into each container, then split the potatoes and green beans by weight across all fourteen. Set the dish on the scale and subtract the empty weight if you want to check yourself. Each portion lands at 668 calories, and the bacon and cheese keep the chicken from drying out on reheat, which is a real advantage over plain baked breast.
