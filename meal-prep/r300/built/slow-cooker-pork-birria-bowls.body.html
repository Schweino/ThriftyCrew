      Makes 14 servings · ~692 cal · 44g protein · 33g carbs · 44g fat · ~$2.41 per serving.

      Birria started in Jalisco as a goat stew and became famous everywhere else as the thing you dip a cheesy taco into. The braising liquid, the consomme, is the whole reason people lose their minds over it. This version uses pork shoulder and chipotles in adobo instead of a wall of dried chiles, which means no toasting, no soaking, no blending. Slow cooker in the morning, crisped cheesy tortillas at dinner, and a cup of consomme to dunk them in.

      Shop Smart

      • Chipotles in adobo replace a bag of dried chiles. One or two small cans deliver smoke, heat and color with no toasting or soaking. That is a shortcut worth taking on a weeknight.
      • Cheese is the second biggest line here, so buy it on sale and freeze it. Shredded blends freeze and thaw without losing anything, and this recipe uses a lot of it.
      • Corn tortillas are cheaper than flour and they crisp better. Dipping them in the consomme and griddling them is the whole move, and corn holds up to it.
      • Do not throw out the braising liquid. That consomme is free flavor you already paid for. Portion it into small containers and reheat it alongside.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cube the pork shoulder, season it with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, ground cumin and dried oregano, and sear it hard in the vegetable oil in batches.
      3. Add the seared pork to the slow cooker with the diced onion, minced garlic, the chipotles in adobo, the chicken broth and the bay leaves.
      4. Cook on low for 8 hours, until the pork falls apart when you press it with a spoon.
      5. Lift the meat out and shred it, then skim the red fat off the top of the braising liquid and save that fat separately. Keep the rest of the liquid as your consomme.
      6. Dip each tortilla in the reserved red fat, lay it on a hot griddle, add shredded pork and the Mexican cheese blend, fold it over, and crisp both sides.
      7. Stir the lime juice into the consomme and chop the cilantro to scatter over everything at the end.

      Portion It

      Set your mixing pot of shredded pork on the scale, subtract the empty pot weight from Step 1, divide by 14, and portion that exact weight into each container, with consomme, tortillas, cheese and salsa packed separately so you can crisp fresh tacos at reheat.
