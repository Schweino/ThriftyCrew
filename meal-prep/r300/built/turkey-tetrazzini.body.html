      Makes 14 servings · ~621 cal · 42g protein · 44g carbs · 31g fat · ~$2.41 per serving.

      Tetrazzini sounds Italian, but it was invented in San Francisco and named after an opera singer, which makes it about as American as a casserole gets. Spaghetti and turkey bound in a mushroom cream sauce, covered in mozzarella and parmesan, baked until the top blisters. Each serving carries 42 grams of protein and 621 calories for about $2.41. That is a restaurant pasta plate for less than the tip.

      Shop Smart

      • Spaghetti is the cheapest pasta shape on the shelf and this recipe wants a lot of it. Buy the 16 oz boxes, since the store brand and the name brand come out of similar machines.
      • White mushrooms in the 8 oz packs are less than half the price of sliced baby bellas and cook down to the same thing in a cream sauce. Slice them yourself and skip the convenience markup.
      • The reduced-fat cream cheese and the reduced-fat mozzarella are not diet compromises here, they are just what keeps the sauce from turning greasy in the fridge. Buy whichever brick and bag are on sale.
      • A frozen turkey breast roast is usually the cheapest cooked turkey you can get outside of November. Roast it, dice it, and you have the protein for this dish and the next one.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the spaghetti in salted water two minutes short of the box time, then drain it. It will finish cooking in the sauce, and mushy tetrazzini is a sad thing.
      3. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat, add the diced onion and sliced mushrooms, and cook 8 to 10 minutes until the mushrooms give up their water and it cooks back off.
      4. Add the garlic and cook one minute, then sprinkle the flour over everything and stir for two minutes so the raw flour taste cooks out.
      5. Pour in the chicken broth a little at a time, whisking, then add the cream cheese in chunks and the heavy cream. Stir until the sauce is smooth and thick enough to coat a spoon.
      6. Season with salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning, then fold in the diced turkey breast and the drained spaghetti until every strand is coated.
      7. Spread everything into a greased 9x13 pan and cover the top with the shredded mozzarella and the parmesan.
      8. Bake at 375F for 25 to 30 minutes, until the cheese is browned in spots and the edges bubble, then rest 10 minutes before portioning.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pan, subtract the empty pan weight, and divide by 14. Pasta bakes settle and slice cleanly once they have rested, so cut to the scale rather than by eye. Each serving delivers 621 calories and 42 grams of protein.
