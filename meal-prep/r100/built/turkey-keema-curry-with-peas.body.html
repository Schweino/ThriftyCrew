      Makes 14 servings · ~641 cal · 36g protein · 89g carbs · 13g fat · ~$2.23 per serving.

      Keema is the weeknight curry of the Indian home kitchen: spiced ground meat and peas in a gravy that comes together in one pot. This turkey version lands 36 grams of protein per bowl over rice for about $2.23, with cumin, turmeric, and fresh ginger doing the talking.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice per the package and set it aside.
      3. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot over medium and cook the onions until golden, about 8 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger, and jalapeno and cook 2 minutes more.
      4. Add the ground cumin, turmeric, and coriander and toast in the aromatics for 30 seconds.
      5. Add the ground turkey and brown it, breaking it into small crumbles, until no pink remains and it reaches 165F.
      6. Pour in the chicken broth and simmer 10 to 15 minutes until the gravy thickens and coats the meat.
      7. Stir in the frozen peas and cook 3 minutes, then finish with the lemon juice and most of the cilantro.
      8. Serve the keema over the rice with the remaining cilantro on top.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot with rice and keema, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Portion to that number and each bowl lands at about 641 calories with 36 grams of protein.
