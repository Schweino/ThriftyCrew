      Shop Smart

      • Chicken thighs run about $2.55 a pound in the club pack, cheaper than breast, and they stay tender when reheated. For creamy skillet meals, thighs are the better tool and the better price.
      • Shelf-stable gnocchi at about $1.97 a pack is the fastest pasta in the store: no boiling pot required, it cooks right in the sauce.
      • Frozen chopped spinach delivers the same nutrition as fresh bags for about a third of the cost, and it is already prepped.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cube the chicken thighs and toss with the Italian seasoning, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
      3. Sear the chicken in the vegetable oil in your big pot in batches until browned and cooked through to 165 F. Set aside.
      4. Add the butter to the pot and saute the diced onion until soft, about 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic for 1 minute.
      5. Add the gnocchi and the chicken broth. Cover and simmer 5 to 6 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the gnocchi are tender and most of the broth is absorbed.
      6. Stir in the heavy cream and the thawed, squeezed-dry spinach and simmer 2 to 3 minutes until the sauce coats the gnocchi.
      7. Return the chicken, stir in the parmesan, and taste for salt.
      8. Portion into 14 containers while warm.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scale that amount into each container. Each bowl lands at about 624 calories with 36 grams of protein.
