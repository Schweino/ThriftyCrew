      Shop Smart

      • Ground beef is two-thirds of this batch's cost, so buy the 3 lb pack at the lower per-pound price and freeze the extra portion for the next batch.
      • Store-brand tomato sauce cans hover around a dollar each. Compare per-ounce against the jarred pasta sauces; the plain cans win and you season them yourself anyway.
      • Buy cheddar in bags when it drops under $2 per 8 oz and stash them in the freezer. Shredded cheese thaws and melts just fine in a casserole.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the pasta shells in salted water until just shy of al dente, then drain.
      3. In your largest pot, brown the ground beef with the diced onion over medium-high heat, breaking it up, until no pink remains; ground beef should hit 160F. Drain excess fat, then add the garlic for the last minute.
      4. Stir in the tomato sauce, beef broth, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer 5 to 7 minutes so it thickens slightly.
      5. Fold in the drained shells and about two-thirds of the cheddar, stirring until the cheese melts into the sauce.
      6. Scatter the remaining cheddar and the mozzarella over the top, drop the heat to low, and cover 3 to 4 minutes until the top layer melts.
      7. Let it rest 5 minutes off the heat; the sauce tightens up and clings to the shells.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scoop even portions into 14 containers and stack them for the week. Each bowl carries 616 calories and 41 grams of protein.
