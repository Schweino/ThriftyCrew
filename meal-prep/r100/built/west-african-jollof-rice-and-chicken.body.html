      Shop Smart

      • Chicken thighs carry the batch at about $15.93 for 6.5 pounds. Thighs hold up to the long simmer in the tomato base where breast meat would give up and turn to sawdust.
      • Canned tomato sauce beats fresh tomatoes here on price and consistency both, about $1.06 for the base of the whole pot. The flavor comes from cooking it down, not from paying produce prices.
      • The spice list looks long, thyme, curry powder, smoked paprika, cayenne, but every one is a pantry jar. The out-of-pocket for this batch is chicken, rice, an onion, and a pepper.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Season the chicken thighs with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and half the curry powder and thyme.
      3. Sear the thighs in the vegetable oil in your largest pot until browned on both sides, then set them aside. They finish cooking later.
      4. Cook the onion and green bell pepper in the same pot until soft, then add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute.
      5. Add the tomato sauce with the smoked paprika, remaining curry powder, thyme, and cayenne, and cook it down 8 to 10 minutes until it darkens and the oil starts to separate. This step is where jollof flavor lives, do not rush it.
      6. Stir in the rice to coat, pour in the chicken broth, and nestle the chicken on top. Cover tightly and simmer on low until the rice is tender and the chicken reads 165F, about 25 minutes. Resist stirring, the bottom crust is a feature.
      7. Scatter the frozen peas and jalapeno over the pot, cover 3 more minutes, then fold everything together.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Portion to that number and each bowl lands at about 591 calories with 50 grams of protein.
