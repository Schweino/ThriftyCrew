      Shop Smart

      • Pork chops carried this whole batch at about $4.10 a pound. Thin-cut or family packs go on sale often; grab 4 lbs and this is the recipe.
      • The sauce is ketchup, crushed tomatoes, sugar, and a splash of broth, about 12 cents a bowl for restaurant-level flavor.
      • Cook the rice a day ahead if you can. Chilled rice fries up better, and it lets you buy the big bag of rice that costs half as much per pound.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice, ideally ahead so it can chill. Pound the pork chops to an even half inch and marinate 15 minutes with the soy sauce, oyster sauce, a little sugar, five-spice powder, salt, and pepper.
      3. Scramble the eggs in butter in your biggest pan, add the rice, and toss over high heat until every grain is coated. Spread the fried rice into baking dishes.
      4. Dredge the chops in the flour, shaking off the extra, and pan fry 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and 145F inside. Lay them over the rice.
      5. In the same pan, soften the onion and carrots, then add the ketchup, crushed tomatoes, remaining sugar, and a splash of chicken broth. Simmer 5 minutes.
      6. Pour the sauce over the chops, scatter the cheese on top, and bake at 400F until melted and bubbling, 10 to 15 minutes.
      7. Rest 5 minutes, then portion into 14 containers.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished baking dishes, subtract your empty dish weights, and divide the total by 14, aiming for one chop portion per box. Each serving lands at about 655 calories and 42 grams of protein.
