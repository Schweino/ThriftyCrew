      Shop Smart

      • The 7 pounds of chicken breast is the biggest line item at about $15.61. Warehouse-club packs like Member's Mark run close to $2.23 a pound, roughly half what small trays cost at a regular store.
      • The curry sauce is built on onions, carrots, and potatoes, three of the cheapest vegetables in the store. Ten onions sounds like a lot until you see that 3 pounds costs $3.42.
      • Skip the boxed Japanese curry roux bricks. Curry powder, flour, butter, and a spoon of tomato paste make the same sauce for a fraction of the price, and you control the salt.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Start the rice: rinse 4.25 cups dry rice and cook it in your rice cooker or a covered pot while everything else happens.
      3. Make the curry base: heat the vegetable oil in your big pot and cook the sliced onions over medium heat 15 to 20 minutes until soft and golden. Stir in the garlic, curry powder, and tomato paste and cook 2 minutes.
      4. Add the chopped potatoes, carrots, chicken broth, Worcestershire sauce, and sugar. Simmer 20 minutes until the vegetables are tender, then stir in the cornstarch mixed with a little cold water and the butter. Simmer until the sauce thickens and coats a spoon. Season with salt and pepper.
      5. Bread the chicken: slice the breasts into cutlets about a half inch thick. Dredge each in flour, then beaten egg, then bread crumbs, pressing the crumbs on.
      6. Fry the cutlets in batches in a skillet with a thin layer of oil, 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden and the inside reads 165 F on a thermometer. Rest on a rack, then slice into strips.
      7. Portion rice into 14 containers, ladle curry sauce over, and top with sliced katsu. Keeping the katsu on top helps it stay crisper in the fridge.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot of curry, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is your sauce weight per container, and split the rice and sliced katsu evenly on top. Each bowl lands at about 877 calories with 65 grams of protein.
