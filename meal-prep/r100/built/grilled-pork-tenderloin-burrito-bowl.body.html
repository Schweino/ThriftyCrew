      Shop Smart

      • Whole pork tenderloin ran about $4 a pound here. Skip the pre-marinated ones; you pay extra for sugar water and lose control of the seasoning.
      • The salsa is canned diced tomatoes, jalapeno, red onion, and lime for about $6 total. A tub of fresh pico that size would cost double.
      • Rice cooked in chicken broth instead of water is a 62 cent upgrade that makes the whole bowl taste intentional.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Pat the tenderloins dry and rub them all over with most of the taco seasoning, salt, and black pepper.
      3. Grill over medium-high heat, or sear in a hot pan and finish in a 400F oven, until the thickest part reads 145F, about 15 to 20 minutes. Rest 10 minutes, then dice and toss with the BBQ sauce.
      4. While the pork cooks, simmer the rice in the chicken broth with the diced yellow onion, covered, until tender.
      5. Make the salsa: drain the diced tomatoes well, then stir them with minced jalapeno, diced red onion, lime juice, chopped cilantro, and a pinch of taco seasoning.
      6. Portion rice into 14 containers, then divide the BBQ pork, salsa, and shredded cheddar on top.

      Portion It

      Weigh the rice pot and the bowl of diced pork, subtract the empty pot number, and divide each by 14 so every container gets an even cut. Each bowl lands at about 571 calories and 39 grams of protein.
