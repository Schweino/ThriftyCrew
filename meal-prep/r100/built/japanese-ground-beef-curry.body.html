      Makes 14 servings · ~590 cal · 49g protein · 63g carbs · 15g fat · ~$3.49 per serving.

      Shop Smart

      • The homemade roux trick is the big saver: flour plus curry powder and garam masala costs about $2.42 here, while boxed curry roux for a batch this size runs north of $15.
      • Beef is 70 percent of the bill at $34.58 for 5.5 lb. When the big pack of 93/7 goes on sale, this recipe earns its keep; ground turkey is a fine stand-in when it does not.
      • Potatoes, carrots, and onions bulk the pot for about $4.42 total. That is how 5.5 lb of meat feeds 14 people twice over.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice: rinse, simmer covered with water until tender, and set aside.
      3. Heat the vegetable oil in your biggest pot. Brown the ground beef, breaking it up, until no pink remains.
      4. Add the diced onion, carrots, and potatoes and cook 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic and ginger for 1 minute.
      5. Sprinkle in the flour, curry powder, and garam masala and stir 2 minutes so the raw flour cooks out and the spices toast.
      6. Slowly stir in the beef broth, then the soy sauce and Worcestershire. Simmer uncovered 15 to 20 minutes until the potatoes are tender and the curry thickens to a gravy.
      7. Stir in the peas, simmer 3 minutes, and season with salt and pepper.
      8. Portion rice into 14 containers and ladle the curry over.

      Portion It

      Weigh the rice pot and the curry pot, subtract the empty pot number, and divide each by 14 so every box gets an even ladle. Each bowl comes out to about 590 calories and 49 grams of protein.
