      Shop Smart

      • Splitting the meat between beef and hot Italian sausage is the trick: the sausage runs about half the per-pound price of beef and brings its own seasoning for free.
      • Store-brand marinara at about $1.82 a jar is fine here because the cheese layer and sausage do the flavor work. Save the fancy jar for a dish where the sauce stands alone.
      • Cream cheese, ricotta, and sour cream all go on sale around holidays. Every one of them freezes acceptably for baked dishes like this, so stock the freezer when they dip.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the spaghetti two minutes short of package time and drain.
      3. Brown the ground beef and hot Italian sausage together in your big pot until no pink remains, then drain. Stir in the marinara and Italian seasoning and simmer 5 minutes.
      4. Mix the ricotta, softened cream cheese, and sour cream in a bowl until smooth.
      5. Layer in a greased 9x13 pan: half the spaghetti, all the cheese mixture, the rest of the spaghetti, then all the meat sauce.
      6. Top with the mozzarella and bake at 350 F for 25 to 30 minutes until melted and bubbling at the edges.
      7. Rest 10 minutes before cutting so the layers hold.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pot of finished meat sauce before layering, subtract the empty pot number from step 1, and spread it evenly. After baking and resting, cut the pan into 14 squares and box them. Each serving comes in at 566 calories and 35 grams of protein.
