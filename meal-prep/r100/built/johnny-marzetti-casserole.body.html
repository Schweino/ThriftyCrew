      Makes 14 servings · ~568 cal · 38g protein · 56g carbs · 23g fat · ~$2.68 per serving.

      Johnny Marzetti is the church-basement casserole that fed half the Midwest for a century, and it still earns its spot: beefy tomato sauce, wide egg noodles, mushrooms, olives, and a melted cheddar lid.       Shop Smart

      • The beef and pork are two thirds of the batch cost. Ground beef in 3 pound packs runs well under the small-tray price per pound, so buy the big pack and freeze what you do not use.
      • Canned diced tomatoes are one of the most stable prices in the store. Stock up when they dip near 90 cents a can and this casserole gets even cheaper.
      • Skip the pre-sliced mushrooms. The whole 8 ounce pack is usually the same price or less, and slicing them yourself takes about ninety seconds.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the egg noodles two minutes short of the package time, drain, and set aside. They finish cooking in the oven.
      3. Brown the ground beef and ground pork with the diced onion in your big pot over medium-high heat until no pink remains. Drain excess fat.
      4. Add the diced bell peppers and sliced mushrooms and cook 5 minutes until they soften.
      5. Stir in the diced tomatoes, brown sugar, dijon, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Simmer 10 minutes so the sauce thickens up.
      6. Fold in the noodles, olives, and half the cheddar until everything is coated.
      7. Spread into a greased 9x13 pan (it will be full) or two smaller pans, top with the rest of the cheddar, and bake at 350 F for about 20 minutes until bubbly and melted.

      Portion It

      Before it goes in the oven, weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot number from step 1, and divide by 14. That is your per-serving weight. After baking, portion it into 14 containers by that number and every bowl lands at 568 calories and 38 grams of protein.
