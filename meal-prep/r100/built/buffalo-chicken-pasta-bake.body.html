      Shop Smart

      • Chicken breast at the 3 lb size beats the small trays on price per pound, and this recipe only needs 2.25 lb, so the extra goes to the freezer.
      • One-third fat cream cheese costs the same as full fat, melts smoother into a sauce, and trims the calories. Buy the store-brand bricks, not the tubs; bricks are cheaper per ounce.
      • The gallon of milk is the play even though you only need 7 cups. Per ounce it crushes the half gallon, and cereal exists.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Preheat the oven to 375F. Cube the chicken breast, season with salt and pepper, and cook it in a skillet over medium-high until it reaches 165F. Set aside.
      3. Boil the ziti in salted water until 2 minutes shy of al dente, then drain.
      4. In your largest pot, melt the butter over medium heat, whisk in the flour, and cook 1 minute. Gradually whisk in the milk and simmer 3 to 4 minutes until it thickens enough to coat a spoon.
      5. Whisk in the cream cheese until smooth, then the ranch seasoning and buffalo wing sauce.
      6. Fold in the cooked chicken and ziti until everything is coated, then spread into two 9x13 pans.
      7. Top with the mozzarella and bake 15 to 20 minutes until melted and bubbling at the edges. Rest 5 to 10 minutes before combining into your weighed pot.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot weight, and divide by 14. Scoop even portions into 14 containers; the sauce keeps it creamy through a week of reheats. Each bowl checks in at 606 calories and 41 grams of protein.
