      Shop Smart

      • Chicken thighs beat breasts here twice: they cost less per pound and they stay juicy through the long simmer and the fridge week.
      • Poblanos are the flavor backbone at about $4.25 for what this batch needs. They are usually the cheapest fresh chile on the wall, and charring them is free flavor.
      • A small handful of peanuts from a $2.58 jar replaces the pricey nuts and seeds a traditional mole calls for. The jar covers several batches.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Char the poblanos under the broiler or over a burner flame until blistered all over. Steam them in a covered bowl 10 minutes, then peel, seed, and roughly chop.
      3. Cook the rice: 4.75 cups dry rice in your rice cooker or a covered pot while the sauce comes together.
      4. In your big pot, saute the chopped onion and jalapeno until soft. Add the chili powder, garlic powder, oregano, and black pepper and toast 1 minute.
      5. Blend the poblanos, onion mixture, peanuts, and about half the chicken broth until smooth. Pour the sauce back into the pot with the rest of the broth.
      6. Cut the chicken thighs into large chunks, season with salt, and simmer them in the sauce 20 to 25 minutes until they reach 165 F and the sauce has thickened.
      7. Portion rice into 14 containers, ladle chicken and mole sauce over, and finish with chopped cilantro.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14 for the chicken and sauce. Split the rice evenly underneath. Each bowl lands at about 560 calories with 52 grams of protein.
