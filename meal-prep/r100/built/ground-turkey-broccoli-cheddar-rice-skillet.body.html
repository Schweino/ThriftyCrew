      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat most of the olive oil in your biggest pot. Brown the ground turkey, breaking it up, until it reaches 165F with no pink remaining. Add the onion and cook until soft, then the garlic for 1 minute.
      3. Stir in the rinsed rice, chicken broth, oregano, basil, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Cover and simmer about 15 minutes until the rice is nearly tender.
      4. Stir in the milk, Dijon mustard, and broccoli florets. Cover and cook 5 more minutes until the broccoli is bright and tender.
      5. Pull the pot off the heat and stir in the cheddar a handful at a time until the whole thing turns creamy.
      6. In a small skillet, toast the bread crumbs in the butter until golden, then stir in the parmesan.
      7. Portion into 14 containers and sprinkle the crumb topping over each so it stays crisp.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scoop that weight into each container, add the crumb topping, and every bowl lands at about 592 calories and 35 grams of protein.
