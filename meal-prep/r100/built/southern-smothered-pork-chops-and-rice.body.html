      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice per the package and set it aside.
      3. Season the pork chops with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and cayenne, then dredge them in the flour. Save the leftover seasoned flour, that is your gravy starter.
      4. Fry the chops in vegetable oil over medium-high heat, about 4 minutes per side, until golden and 145F inside. Work in batches and set them on a plate.
      5. Pour off all but a few tablespoons of the oil, then cook the onions in the drippings until soft and browned at the edges. Add the garlic for the last minute.
      6. Stir in a few spoonfuls of the reserved seasoned flour and cook 1 to 2 minutes, then whisk in the chicken broth slowly so it stays smooth. Simmer until it thickens into gravy.
      7. Stir in the heavy cream, return the chops, and let them smother in the gravy for 10 minutes on low.
      8. Serve the chops and gravy over the rice.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Portion rice, chop, and gravy to that number and each bowl lands at about 896 calories with 45 grams of protein. This one is a heavyweight, built for big days.
