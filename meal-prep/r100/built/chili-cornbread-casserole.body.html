      Shop Smart

      • The 93/7 ground beef is the big spend at $21.60 for 3.5 pounds. Beans, tomatoes, and corn stretch it across 14 servings, which is the oldest chili trick in the book because it works.
      • Three boxes of JIFFY corn muffin mix cost $2.16 total. There is no cheaper from-scratch cornbread once you price out cornmeal, flour, and leavening separately.
      • Canned kidney beans, diced tomatoes, and crushed tomatoes are all store-brand items where the name brand buys you nothing but a prettier label.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Brown the ground beef in olive oil in your big pot, breaking it up until no pink remains. Add the diced onion and green bell pepper and cook until soft.
      3. Stir in the garlic, chili powder, cayenne, brown sugar, and tomato paste and cook 2 minutes until dark and fragrant.
      4. Add the Worcestershire, hot sauce, chicken broth, crushed tomatoes, diced tomatoes, and drained kidney beans. Simmer uncovered 20 minutes until thick. Season with salt and pepper.
      5. Pour the chili into a greased baking dish (or two, this is a big batch). Heat the oven to 375 F.
      6. Mix the cornbread topping: corn muffin mix, drained corn, milk, sour cream, melted butter, and eggs. Stir just until combined and spread it gently over the chili.
      7. Bake 25 to 30 minutes until the cornbread is golden and a toothpick in the topping comes out clean.
      8. Cool 15 minutes, then portion into 14 containers, making sure each gets its share of the cornbread lid.

      Portion It

      Weigh the chili pot before it goes in the baking dish, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14 so each container gets an even base under its square of cornbread. Each serving lands at about 696 calories with 38 grams of protein.
