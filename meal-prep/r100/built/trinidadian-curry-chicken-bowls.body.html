      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Blend or finely chop the cilantro, garlic, jalapeno, and half the onion into a rough green seasoning paste. Toss the chicken thighs, cut into large chunks, with the paste and a big pinch of salt. Let it sit at least 15 minutes, longer if you have it.
      3. Stir the curry powder, turmeric, cumin, and garam masala with a few tablespoons of water to make a slurry.
      4. Heat the vegetable oil in your largest pot over medium-high. Pour in the curry slurry and fry it, stirring, until the water cooks off and the oil separates and the kitchen smells serious, 2 to 3 minutes.
      5. Add the chicken and remaining onion and stir to coat every piece in the fried curry. Cook 5 minutes, letting the chicken release its own liquid.
      6. Add the potatoes, peeled and cut in chunks, plus enough water to come halfway up. Cover and simmer until the potatoes are tender, the gravy thickens, and the chicken reads 165F, about 25 minutes.
      7. Taste for salt and black pepper, and mash a few potato chunks against the pot wall if you want the gravy thicker.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Portion to that number and each bowl lands at about 577 calories with 58 grams of protein, the highest protein count on this page.
