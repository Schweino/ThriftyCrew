      Makes 14 servings · ~562 cal · 49g protein · 45g carbs · 17g fat · ~$1.45 per serving.

      Chicken inasal is the pride of Bacolod: thighs marinated in vinegar, lime, lemongrass, and ginger, then basted with golden annatto butter and served over rice. Each bowl delivers 49 grams of protein at a lean 562 calories for about $1.45. That orange butter baste is what makes it unmistakably inasal.

      Shop Smart

      • Chicken thighs at $17.36 for 7 pounds are the whole main event. Thighs take a hard sear and a sweet-sour marinade far better than breasts, and cost less doing it.
      • The achiote paste looks pricey per jar, but this batch only uses about a third of one and the jar keeps for months. It is the color and earthiness of the dish; do not skip it.
      • White vinegar, lime juice, and brown sugar make a marinade that costs about $2 total. Bottled barbecue marinades run $4 each and would be wrong for this dish anyway.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Make the marinade: whisk the white vinegar, lime juice, lemongrass paste, garlic, ginger, brown sugar, salt, and black pepper.
      3. Cut the chicken thighs into large pieces, toss with the marinade, and refrigerate at least 1 hour, up to overnight.
      4. Cook the rice: 3.75 cups dry rice in a rice cooker or covered pot.
      5. Make the annatto butter: melt the butter with the achiote paste over low heat, mashing until the butter turns deep orange.
      6. Grill or sear the chicken over high heat, 5 to 6 minutes per side, basting generously with the annatto butter, until charred at the edges and cooked through to 165 F.
      7. Brush the cooked chicken with any remaining baste (bring leftover baste to a brief boil first if it touched raw chicken).
      8. Portion rice into 14 containers and top with the basted chicken.

      Portion It

      Weigh your pot or tray of finished chicken, subtract the empty container weight you wrote down, and divide by 14 to split it evenly over the rice. Each bowl finishes at about 562 calories with 49 grams of protein.
