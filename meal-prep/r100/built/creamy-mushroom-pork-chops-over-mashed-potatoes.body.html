      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Start the potatoes: peel, chunk, and boil in salted water 15 to 20 minutes until fork tender. Drain and mash with a spoonful of the butter and a pinch of salt. Cover and keep warm.
      3. Season the chops with salt and pepper and dust lightly with some of the flour. Sear in olive oil in your big skillet, 4 to 5 minutes per side, until golden and the centers read 145 F. Rest on a plate.
      4. Melt the butter in the same skillet and cook the sliced mushrooms without crowding until browned, about 8 minutes. Stir in the garlic for 1 minute.
      5. Sprinkle in the remaining flour and stir 1 to 2 minutes.
      6. Whisk in the chicken broth and Worcestershire, scraping up the browned bits, then stir in the heavy cream. Simmer 3 to 4 minutes until the gravy coats a spoon.
      7. Return the chops and any juices to the gravy for a minute to warm through.
      8. Portion mashed potatoes into 14 containers, top with a chop, and ladle mushroom gravy over each.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pot of mashed potatoes, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14 for the base, then split the chops and gravy evenly on top. Each plate lands at about 591 calories with 40 grams of protein.
