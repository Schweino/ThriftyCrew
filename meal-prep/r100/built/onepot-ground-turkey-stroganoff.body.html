      Shop Smart

      • Seven packs of mushrooms sounds extravagant until you see they ran about $1.36 each on our board. Mushrooms stretch the turkey and eat like meat.
      • Ground turkey in the 4 pound pack averaged about $4.33 a pound. Watch for it, because turkey sales are quieter than beef sales but they come around just as often.
      • Light sour cream and a third-fat cream cheese give you the full creamy finish at a lower price and calorie load than heavy cream. Nobody has ever noticed.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Melt the butter in your big pot and cook the diced onion 3 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 more.
      3. Add the ground turkey and brown it, breaking it up, until it reads 165 F with no pink. Season with the thyme, salt, and pepper.
      4. Add the mushrooms and cook 6 to 8 minutes until they release their water and it cooks off.
      5. Pour in the chicken broth and Worcestershire, bring to a boil, and stir in the egg noodles. Simmer 8 to 10 minutes, stirring so nothing sticks, until the noodles are just tender.
      6. Kill the heat. Stir in the dijon, sour cream, and cream cheese until the sauce is smooth.
      7. Fold in the thawed spinach until warmed through, then taste for salt.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot number you wrote down in step 1, and divide by 14. Scoop that weight into each of 14 containers and every bowl lands at 568 calories and 38 grams of protein.
