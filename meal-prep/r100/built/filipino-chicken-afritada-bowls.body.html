      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cut the chicken thighs into large chunks and pat dry. Brown them in batches in some of the vegetable oil in your big pot. Set aside.
      3. Fry the diced potatoes and carrots in the remaining oil 5 minutes until they pick up color. Set aside with the chicken.
      4. Saute the diced onion until soft, then the garlic for 1 minute.
      5. Add the diced tomatoes and fish sauce, bring to a simmer, and cook 10 minutes to concentrate.
      6. Return the chicken, potatoes, and carrots. Cover and simmer 20 to 25 minutes until the chicken reads 165 F and the potatoes are tender.
      7. Stir in the sliced green and red bell peppers and the peas for the last 5 minutes so they keep some snap. Season with black pepper and salt to taste.
      8. Portion into 14 containers, distributing chicken and vegetables evenly.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scale that into each container. Each bowl comes in at about 675 calories with 57 grams of protein.
