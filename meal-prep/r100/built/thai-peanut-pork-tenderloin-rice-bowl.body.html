      Shop Smart

      • Pork tenderloin does the protein work at about $11.91 for 3 pounds. It is one of the leanest cuts in the case and goes on sale constantly, buy at the dip and freeze.
      • The coconut milk sauce only needs 1.4 cans. Buy two, use what the recipe calls for, and the leftover can starts your next curry.
      • Peanut butter is the sauce backbone at about $1.17 worth. The store-brand jar melts into coconut milk exactly like the name brand does, and one jar covers several batches.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice per the package. While it is hot, stir in half the lime juice and the fresh cilantro. Cover and set aside.
      3. Cut the pork tenderloin into medallions about an inch thick and season with salt, black pepper, and ground coriander.
      4. Sear the medallions in olive oil over medium-high heat, about 3 to 4 minutes per side, until they hit 145F. Rest them on a plate.
      5. In the same pan, add the garlic and cook 30 seconds, then pour in the coconut milk and whisk in the peanut butter and brown sugar. Simmer until it thickens into a smooth peanut sauce, then finish with the rest of the lime juice.
      6. Stir the spinach into the warm peanut sauce until wilted through.
      7. Build bowls: lime rice down, sliced pork on top, peanut sauce spooned over everything.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot of rice, pork, and sauce, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Portion to that number and each bowl lands at about 583 calories with 29 grams of protein.
