      Makes 14 servings · ~578 cal · 27g protein · 39g carbs · 34g fat · ~$2.08 per serving.

      The John Wayne casserole is a biscuit-bottomed Tex-Mex stack: taco-seasoned beef, green chiles, a creamy sour cream and mayo layer, and a blanket of cheddar. It eats like a potluck legend because it is one. Each serving carries 27 grams of protein for about $2.08, which is less than most gas station burritos.

      Shop Smart

      • Refrigerated biscuits are the swing item here. Store-brand tubes often run a dollar less than Pillsbury per can, and inside a casserole you will never taste the difference.
      • Ground beef in the 3 pound family pack beats the small tray on price per pound. Use 2.5 pounds here and freeze the rest for taco night.
      • The Ro-Tel style tomatoes with green chilies almost always have a store-brand twin at 20 to 30 cents less per can. Grab that one.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Press the biscuit dough into a greased 9x13 pan (plus a small second pan if needed) to form a solid crust, and bake at 350 F for about 10 minutes so it sets.
      3. Brown the ground beef with most of the diced onion in your pot until no pink remains, then drain. Stir in the taco seasoning with a splash of water.
      4. Add the diced tomatoes with green chilies and the diced green chiles. Simmer 5 minutes until thick.
      5. In a bowl, mix the sour cream, mayonnaise, half the cheddar, and the remaining onion.
      6. Layer beef over the biscuit crust, add the bell pepper, spread the sour cream mixture on top, and finish with the rest of the cheddar.
      7. Bake at 350 F for 20 to 25 minutes until the cheese is melted and the edges bubble.

      Portion It

      Weigh your pot of finished beef filling before assembly, subtract the empty pot number from step 1, and split it evenly as you build. After baking, cut the casserole into 14 squares and box them up. Each serving comes in at 578 calories and 27 grams of protein.
