      Makes 14 servings · ~613 cal · 49g protein · 56g carbs · 21g fat · ~$2.94 per serving.

      Stroganoff is what happens when mushrooms, butter, and sour cream decide to make pork look fancy. This one delivers 49 grams of protein per bowl over wide egg noodles, and it costs about $2.94 a serving. Comfort food math that actually works in your favor.

      Shop Smart

      • Pork tenderloin is the big line item at about $20.84 of this batch. Whole tenderloins go on sale all the time, so when they dip under $4 a pound, buy two and freeze one for the next round.
      • Buy whole white mushrooms in 8 oz packs and slice them yourself. Pre-sliced runs more per pound for about three minutes of knife work.
      • Light sour cream in the store-brand tub tastes identical once it hits a warm sauce. Two 16 oz tubs cover the batch with a little left for taco night.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the egg noodles in salted water until just al dente, drain, and set aside. They finish softening in the sauce, so do not overcook them here.
      3. Cut the pork tenderloin into bite-size pieces and season with salt, black pepper, and the smoked paprika. Sear in batches in about half the butter over medium-high heat until they hit 145F, then move them to a plate.
      4. Melt the rest of the butter in the same pot and cook the mushrooms and onions until browned and their liquid cooks off, about 8 to 10 minutes. Stir in the garlic for the last minute.
      5. Stir in the tomato paste and flour and cook for 1 minute, then whisk in the chicken broth, apple cider vinegar, and lemon juice. Simmer until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon, seasoning with the Italian seasoning as it goes.
      6. Return the pork and any juices to the pot and warm through. Pull the pot off the heat, then stir in the light sour cream. Off the heat matters, boiling sour cream is how you get a grainy sauce.
      7. Fold the noodles into the sauce until everything is coated and glossy.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. That is your gram target per container. Scoop, weigh, repeat, and every bowl lands at about 613 calories with 49 grams of protein.
