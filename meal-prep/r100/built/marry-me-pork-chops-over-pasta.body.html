      Shop Smart

      • Pork chops are two thirds of the batch, so this is a recipe to cook when chops hit the weekly board on sale. Every 50 cents off per pound saves you about $3 a batch.
      • Real parmesan from the 8 ounce tub beats the green shaker can on both flavor and per-ounce price. Grate it yourself.
      • Sun-dried tomatoes keep for months. One jar covers this batch for about $1.39 worth and hangs around for the next marry me anything.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice: 3.75 cups dry with water per your usual method.
      3. Pat the chops dry and season all over with the cajun seasoning, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
      4. Sear the chops in olive oil in your big pot over medium-high, 3 to 4 minutes per side in batches, until they reach 145 F inside. Set aside to rest.
      5. In the same pot, melt the butter and cook the garlic 1 minute. Pour in the chicken broth and scrape up the browned bits.
      6. Add the heavy cream, chopped sun-dried tomatoes, oregano, and basil, and simmer 5 minutes until it thickens slightly.
      7. Off the heat, stir in the parmesan until smooth. Slice the rested chops and return them to the sauce.
      8. Serve the saucy pork over the rice.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pot of pork and sauce, subtract the empty pot number from step 1, and divide by 14. Portion that weight over even scoops of rice in 14 containers. Every bowl lands at 669 calories and 45 grams of protein.
