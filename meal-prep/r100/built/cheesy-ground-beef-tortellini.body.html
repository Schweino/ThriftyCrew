      Shop Smart

      • Tortellini is the splurge in this batch, so compare the dry, refrigerated, and frozen versions per ounce; dry store-brand is usually the winner by a wide margin.
      • One-third fat cream cheese makes the sauce creamy for less money and fewer calories than heavy cream, and the bricks are cheaper per ounce than the tubs.
      • Marinara at $1.82 a jar plus cream cheese beats any jarred rosa or vodka sauce on price, and it tastes like you made it on purpose.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Brown the ground beef in your largest pot over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks, until no pink remains; ground beef should reach 160F. Drain excess fat.
      3. Season with the garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, salt, and pepper, then stir in the marinara sauce and bring to a simmer.
      4. Add the cream cheese in chunks and stir until it melts into a creamy rosa-style sauce, 3 to 4 minutes.
      5. Meanwhile, boil the cheese tortellini in salted water until they float and are just tender, then drain.
      6. Fold the tortellini into the sauce gently so they do not tear.
      7. Scatter the mozzarella over the top, cover, and heat on low 3 to 4 minutes until melted. Rest 5 minutes before portioning.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight, and divide by 14. Scoop carefully so each container gets its share of tortellini and sauce. Every bowl comes in at 566 calories with 42 grams of protein.
