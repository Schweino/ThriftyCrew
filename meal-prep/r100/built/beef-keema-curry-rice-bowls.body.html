      Makes 14 servings · ~567 cal · 34g protein · 78g carbs · 12g fat · ~$2.09 per serving.

      Keema is the Indian answer to weeknight ground beef: curried, gingery, studded with peas, and simmered until the spices soak all the way in. Each bowl delivers 34 grams of protein and 567 calories for about $2.09. It also freezes better than almost anything else we make.

      Shop Smart

      • The ground beef is 70 percent of this batch's cost. Buy the 4 lb pack for the lower per-pound price and freeze the extra half pound toward your next batch.
      • Curry powder, cumin, and turmeric from the jar cost well under a dollar per batch. Skip the pre-made simmer sauce jars; you are paying $4 for spices you already own.
      • Frozen peas are the budget hero here: cheaper than canned per ounce, better color, and the bag keeps for months.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Start the rice: cook 6.5 cups dry rice with water per package directions while you make the keema.
      3. Heat the olive oil in your largest pot over medium heat. Cook the diced onions 6 to 8 minutes until golden at the edges, then add the garlic and ginger for 1 minute more.
      4. Add the ground beef, turn the heat to medium-high, and brown it thoroughly, breaking it into small crumbles, until no pink remains; ground beef should reach 160F.
      5. Stir in the curry powder, cumin, turmeric, salt, and pepper and toast the spices in the fat for 1 minute.
      6. Add the tomato paste and a splash of water, then simmer 10 minutes so the flavors marry and the mixture thickens.
      7. Stir in the frozen peas for the last 3 minutes, then kill the heat and fold in most of the chopped cilantro.
      8. Combine the rice and keema in your weighed pot and top with the remaining cilantro.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot weight, and divide by 14. Scoop that gram target into 14 containers and dinner is settled for two weeks of workdays. Each bowl carries 567 calories and 34 grams of protein.
