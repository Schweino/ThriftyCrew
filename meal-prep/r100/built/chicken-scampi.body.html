      Makes 14 servings · ~871 cal · 60g protein · 75g carbs · 38g fat · ~$2.15 per serving.

      The Olive Garden favorite, rebuilt at home: golden chicken strips, bell peppers, and red onion over spaghetti in a garlic butter sauce. Each bowl carries 60 grams of protein at 871 calories for about $2.15. The restaurant version is mostly butter and pasta anyway, and those are two of the cheapest things you can buy.

      Shop Smart

      • Six pounds of chicken breast for $13.66 is the anchor here. Club packs at about $2.23 a pound are the play; small grocery trays can run nearly double.
      • Store-brand spaghetti covers 36 ounces for $2.19. Pasta is one of the last places in the store where a name brand buys you almost nothing.
      • One pound of butter at $3.06 makes the entire sauce rich. Compare that to a $6 jar of alfredo that would cover half the batch.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the spaghetti 1 minute short of package time, drain, and toss with a spoonful of oil so it does not clump.
      3. Slice the chicken into strips, season with salt and pepper, and dredge in the flour. Sear in batches in vegetable oil until golden and cooked through to 165 F. Set aside.
      4. In the same pot, saute the sliced green and red bell peppers with the red and yellow onion until they soften with a little char, about 5 minutes. Set aside with the chicken.
      5. Melt the butter, add the garlic and red pepper flakes, and cook 1 minute. Sprinkle in a couple spoonfuls of the leftover dredging flour and whisk 1 minute more.
      6. Whisk in the chicken broth and lemon juice and simmer until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon. Stir in the parsley.
      7. Return the chicken, peppers, and onions, then toss with the spaghetti and half the parmesan.
      8. Portion into 14 containers and top with the remaining parmesan.

      Portion It

      Weigh the loaded pot, subtract the empty pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scale that into each container. Every bowl comes in around 871 calories with 60 grams of protein.
