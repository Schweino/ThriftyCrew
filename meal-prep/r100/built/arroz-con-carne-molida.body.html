      Shop Smart

      • The 93/7 ground beef is the biggest line item. Watch for the big club-size packs, which run cheaper per pound, and stock the freezer when it dips.
      • Store-brand diced tomatoes with green chilies taste the same as the name brand Ro-Tel and usually cost 20 to 30 percent less. Check the price per ounce tag and let math decide.
      • Shredded cheese goes on sale constantly. Buy the 8 oz bags when they drop below $2 and freeze what you do not use; it melts fine after thawing.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Brown the ground beef in your largest skillet or pot over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks, until no pink remains (ground beef should hit 160F). Drain excess fat.
      3. Stir in the taco seasoning, diced onion, minced jalapeno, and garlic. Cook 3 to 4 minutes until the onion softens.
      4. Add the dry rice and stir for 1 to 2 minutes so it toasts lightly in the beef drippings.
      5. Pour in the diced tomatoes with green chilies, the corn with its liquid, and enough water to cook the rice (about 2 cups of water per cup of dry rice, minus the can liquid). Season with salt and pepper.
      6. Bring to a boil, then cover and reduce to a low simmer for 18 to 20 minutes, until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed. Stir once halfway so nothing sticks.
      7. Kill the heat, scatter the Mexican cheese blend over the top, and cover for 3 to 4 minutes until it melts into the rice.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scoop that weight into each of 14 containers and you have identical dinners all week. Every bowl comes in at 703 calories with 43 grams of protein.
