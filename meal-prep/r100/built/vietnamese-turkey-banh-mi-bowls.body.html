      Shop Smart

      • Ground turkey handles the protein at about $12.71 for 3.5 pounds, cheaper than the grilled pork a banh mi shop would use and it soaks up the lemongrass and fish sauce just as well.
      • Cucumbers and carrots are your volume for about $6 combined. Quick-pickling them in vinegar and sugar makes cheap produce taste like a specialty condiment.
      • Sriracha and rice vinegar are one-bottle buys. This batch uses maybe $1.50 of each, and both keep practically forever in the pantry.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice per the package and set it aside.
      3. Quick-pickle the vegetables: shred the carrots and slice a third of the cucumbers thin, then toss with the rice vinegar and sugar. Let them sit at least 20 minutes, stirring once or twice.
      4. Make the sriracha mayo: stir about two thirds of the sriracha into the mayonnaise with half the lime juice.
      5. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over high heat. Brown the ground turkey, breaking it up, then add the garlic, lemongrass paste, and remaining sriracha, and cook until the turkey reaches 165F.
      6. Stir the fish sauce and remaining lime juice into the turkey and let it sizzle 1 more minute.
      7. Slice the remaining cucumbers fresh.
      8. Build bowls: rice, lemongrass turkey, pickled veg, fresh cucumber, green onions, and a drizzle of sriracha mayo. Pack extra mayo separately if you like it cold.

      Portion It

      Weigh the rice and turkey together, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14, then split the pickles, cucumber, and mayo evenly. Each bowl lands at about 674 calories with 29 grams of protein.
