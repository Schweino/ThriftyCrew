      Shop Smart

      • Fresh mint is $3.49 for two little packs, one of the priciest herbs per ounce in the store. If you have a yard, mint grows like a weed; one planting ends that line item forever.
      • Orzo is the quiet hero: 24.75 oz of pasta for about $1.95 carries a third of every bowl.
      • Feta at $4.54 is the biggest non-meat line. Buy the block and crumble it yourself, and grab the 4 lb turkey pack so the per-pound price stays down.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the oven to 400F. In a big bowl, mix the ground turkey, eggs, bread crumbs, grated red onion, garlic, chopped mint, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper until just combined.
      3. Roll the mix into meatballs, about 3 per serving, and set them on lined sheet pans. Slice the bell peppers into strips and spread them on the pans around the meatballs with a little of the olive oil.
      4. Bake 18 to 22 minutes until the meatballs hit 165F in the center and the peppers have softened.
      5. Meanwhile, boil the orzo until just tender, drain, and toss it with the butter and lemon juice while it is hot.
      6. Stir the Greek yogurt with half the feta and a squeeze of lemon to make the sauce.
      7. Portion orzo, meatballs, and peppers into 14 containers. Top with the yogurt sauce, olives, and the rest of the feta.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pot of orzo, subtract the empty pot number, and divide by 14 for the base, then split the meatballs evenly, about 3 per box. Each finished bowl comes out to about 629 calories and 38 grams of protein.
