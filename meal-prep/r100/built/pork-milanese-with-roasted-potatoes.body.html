      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the oven to 425 F. Cube the potatoes, toss with a spoonful of the vegetable oil, salt, and pepper, and roast 30 to 35 minutes until browned, flipping once.
      3. Slice the pork tenderloin into medallions, then pound each between plastic wrap to about a quarter inch thin.
      4. Set up three dishes: flour with salt and pepper, eggs beaten with the milk, and the bread crumbs.
      5. Dredge each cutlet flour, then egg wash, then crumbs, pressing so the coating sticks.
      6. Pan-fry in batches in the hot vegetable oil, 2 to 3 minutes per side, until deep golden and 145 F inside. Drain on a rack, not paper towels, so the crust stays crisp.
      7. Salt the cutlets the second they leave the pan, and pack them alongside the roasted potatoes.

      Portion It

      Weigh your finished cutlets and potatoes in the pot, subtract the empty pot number from step 1, and divide by 14 to keep servings honest. Each portion lands at 629 calories and 35 grams of protein. Reheat in an air fryer or oven so the crust comes back to life.
