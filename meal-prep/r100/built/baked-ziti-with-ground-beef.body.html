      Makes 14 servings · ~628 cal · 41g protein · 59g carbs · 26g fat · ~$2.20 per serving.

      Baked ziti is the casserole that never lets you down: beefy marinara, creamy ricotta, and a browned mozzarella top. This meal prep version delivers 41 grams of protein and 628 calories per serving at about $2.20. It reheats like it was born for it.

      Shop Smart

      • Ground beef is the priciest line here, so watch the per-pound price on the bigger packs and grab extra when it drops; it freezes clean in 1 lb portions.
      • Store-brand marinara at around $1.82 a jar does the same job as the $8 fancy stuff once it bakes under cheese for half an hour. Save the splurge for a dish where the sauce is the star.
      • Ricotta tubs go on sale around holidays. Check the price per ounce against the small tubs; the 15 oz size is almost always the better buy.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Preheat the oven to 375F. Boil the ziti in salted water until just shy of al dente, about 2 minutes under the package time. Drain.
      3. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large skillet and brown the ground beef with the diced onion, breaking it up as it goes, until no pink remains (160F for ground beef). Add the garlic for the last minute, then stir in the marinara, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes.
      4. In a bowl, mix the ricotta, eggs, half the Parmesan, salt, and pepper.
      5. In two 9x13 pans, layer: a third of the meat sauce, half the pasta, half the ricotta mixture in spoonfuls, then repeat, finishing with sauce on top.
      6. Top with the mozzarella and remaining Parmesan. Bake 25 to 30 minutes until bubbly and browned at the edges.
      7. Rest 10 minutes before combining into your weighed pot; it holds together better and will not steam-soften in the containers.

      Portion It

      Transfer everything to the pot you weighed, put it on the scale, subtract the empty-pot number, and divide by 14. Scoop even portions into 14 containers. Each one comes out to 628 calories and 41 grams of protein.
