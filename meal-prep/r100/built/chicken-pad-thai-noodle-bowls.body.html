      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Soak the rice noodles in hot tap water 20 to 30 minutes until pliable but not fully soft, then drain. They finish cooking in the pot.
      3. Stir the sauce together: fish sauce, brown sugar, oyster sauce, and lime juice. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
      4. Slice the chicken thin, season with salt and pepper, and sear it in batches in a little of the oil over high heat until cooked through to 165 F. Set aside.
      5. In the same pot, cook the sliced onion and garlic in more oil until fragrant. Push everything to one side, pour in the beaten eggs, and scramble until just set.
      6. Add the drained noodles and the sauce. Toss and fry 2 to 3 minutes, until the noodles soak up the sauce and turn glossy. Return the chicken and toss once more.
      7. Portion into 14 containers and top each with a spoonful of chopped peanuts so they keep their crunch.

      Portion It

      Put the whole pot on the scale, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Weigh that into each container and split the peanuts on top. Each bowl works out to about 791 calories with 36 grams of protein.
