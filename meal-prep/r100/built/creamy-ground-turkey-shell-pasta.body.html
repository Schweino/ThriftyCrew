      Makes 14 servings · ~587 cal · 27g protein · 55g carbs · 28g fat · ~$2.02 per serving.

      Shells in a savory cream sauce with browned turkey, mushrooms, and parmesan. It is hamburger-helper comfort, made from scratch: 27 grams of protein, 587 calories, and about $2.02 a bowl. The from-a-box version costs nearly as much and feeds a fraction of the people.

      Shop Smart

      • Ground turkey at $10.10 for 2.25 pounds is the biggest line. When 93/7 turkey goes on sale, it regularly beats ground beef by a dollar or more per pound.
      • Store-brand pasta shells cover 28 ounces for $1.71. Pasta is the cheapest calorie in this dish, which is why the box gets to be big.
      • White mushrooms add meaty bulk for $1.59. They shrink in the pan but pull savory weight far beyond their price.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the pasta shells 1 minute short of package time, drain, and set aside.
      3. Brown the ground turkey in half the olive oil in your big pot, breaking it up until no pink remains. Season with salt, pepper, and the Italian seasoning. Set aside.
      4. Add the remaining oil and saute the sliced mushrooms, diced onion, and diced bell pepper until the mushrooms release their water and brown, about 8 minutes. Stir in the garlic for 1 minute.
      5. Sprinkle the flour over the vegetables and stir 1 to 2 minutes to cook it out.
      6. Slowly whisk in the beef broth, then the heavy cream. Simmer 3 to 4 minutes until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon.
      7. Return the turkey, stir in the parmesan, and fold in the shells until coated.
      8. Portion into 14 containers while the sauce is still loose.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scale that into each container. Each bowl comes out to about 587 calories with 27 grams of protein.
