      Shop Smart

      • Ground turkey at 93/7 gives you 3.75 pounds of lean protein for $16.71. It routinely undercuts lean ground beef by a couple dollars a pound.
      • Shelf-stable gnocchi packs are the shortcut: $6.91 covers the batch and there is no pasta pot to boil since they cook in the sauce.
      • Canned tomato sauce, a bit of paste, and heavy cream build a vodka-sauce-style base for a fraction of what a jarred rosa sauce charges.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Brown the ground turkey in the olive oil in your big pot, breaking it up until no pink remains. Season with salt and pepper.
      3. Add the diced onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic, Italian seasoning, and tomato paste for 2 minutes.
      4. Pour in the tomato sauce and chicken broth and bring to a simmer.
      5. Add the gnocchi and simmer 5 to 6 minutes, stirring so they do not stick, until tender.
      6. Stir in the heavy cream and parmesan and simmer 2 minutes more until the sauce is creamy and coral colored.
      7. Scatter the mozzarella over the top, cover, and let it melt 2 to 3 minutes off the heat (or broil briefly if your pot is oven safe).
      8. Portion into 14 containers, giving each its share of the melted cheese top.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scale that into each container. Each bowl lands at about 795 calories with 42 grams of protein.
