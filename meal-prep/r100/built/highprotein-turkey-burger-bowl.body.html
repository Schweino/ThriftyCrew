      Shop Smart

      • Potatoes remain the best calorie deal in the store: about $2.73 covers 13 of them, and the 7 lb bag at $3.08 leaves extras for breakfast.
      • The burger sauce leans on Greek yogurt instead of straight mayo, which cuts cost and fat while quietly adding protein.
      • Cherry tomatoes are the priciest produce line at $5.37. If the clamshells are not on sale, chopped roma tomatoes save a couple dollars and nobody complains.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat the oven to 425F. Dice the potatoes, toss with a little oil, salt, and paprika, and roast 30 to 35 minutes until crisp at the edges.
      3. Brown the ground turkey in a big skillet, breaking it up, until it reaches 165F with no pink remaining. Season with the Worcestershire, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper.
      4. Whisk the burger sauce: Greek yogurt, mayonnaise, ketchup, Dijon, honey, apple cider vinegar, and finely chopped dill pickles.
      5. Shred the cabbage, halve the cherry tomatoes, and dice the yellow onion.
      6. Portion potatoes and turkey into 14 containers, then top with cabbage, tomatoes, onion, cheddar, and extra pickles.
      7. Keep the sauce in a corner of each box or in small cups and add it after reheating so the crunch survives.

      Portion It

      Weigh the skillet of turkey and the pan of potatoes, subtract the empty pot number, and divide each by 14 so every container gets an even share. Each bowl comes out to about 565 calories and 38 grams of protein.
