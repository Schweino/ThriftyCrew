      Makes 14 servings · ~701 cal · 25g protein · 58g carbs · 40g fat · ~$1.29 per serving.

      Poppy seed chicken is Southern potluck royalty: tender chicken folded into a rich, buttery cream sauce flecked with poppy seeds, baked over rice under a crunchy buttered cracker topping until bubbling. Pure comfort, zero pretension. Each serving brings 25 grams of protein for about $1.29, one of the cheapest dinners on our whole board.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice: 3.75 cups dry with water per your usual method.
      3. Cube the chicken breast and cook it in a skillet with a spoonful of the butter over medium-high until it reaches 165 F. Set aside.
      4. In your big pot, melt the rest of the butter, setting aside about 3 tablespoons of it for the topping, and cook the diced onion 4 minutes, then the garlic for 1 more.
      5. Whisk in the flour and cook 1 minute, then gradually whisk in the chicken broth and heavy cream until smooth and simmering.
      6. Drop in the cream cheese and stir until melted, then add the poppy seeds, salt, and pepper.
      7. Fold in the chicken and the cooked rice, then spread into a greased 9x13 pan.
      8. Crush the butter crackers, toss them with the reserved melted butter, scatter them over the top, and bake at 350 F for 20 to 25 minutes until the topping is golden and the edges bubble.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot before it goes into the pan, subtract the empty pot number from step 1, and divide by 14. After baking, portion by that weight into 14 containers. Each serving comes in at 701 calories and 25 grams of protein.
