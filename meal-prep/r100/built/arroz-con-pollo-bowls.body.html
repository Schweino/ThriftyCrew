      Makes 14 servings · ~628 cal · 53g protein · 60g carbs · 17g fat · ~$1.80 per serving.

      Arroz con pollo is the dish half of Latin America grew up on: chicken thighs simmered right into seasoned rice so every grain picks up the flavor. This batch packs 53 grams of protein and 628 calories per bowl for about $1.80. Olives and peas at the end make it look fancier than the price tag admits.

      Shop Smart

      • Boneless skinless chicken thighs run cheaper per pound than breasts and stay juicier through a long simmer. The big 7 lb club packs are the best per-pound deal in the case.
      • Buy the small 6 oz can of tomato paste, not the tube. The can costs less per ounce and this recipe uses the whole thing, so nothing dries out in the fridge.
      • Frozen peas beat canned on both price and texture, and a 12 oz store-brand bag covers this batch for under a dollar.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Season the chicken thighs with salt, pepper, and cumin. Heat the vegetable oil in your largest pot over medium-high and brown the thighs in batches, about 3 minutes per side. Set them aside; they finish cooking later.
      3. In the same pot, cook the diced onion and green bell pepper for 4 to 5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
      4. Stir in the dry rice, tomato paste, oregano, turmeric, sugar, and bay leaves. Toast for 2 minutes, stirring, so the rice takes on color and flavor.
      5. Pour in the chicken broth, scrape up the browned bits, and nestle the thighs back in along with any juices.
      6. Bring to a boil, cover, and drop to a low simmer for 20 to 25 minutes, until the rice is tender and the chicken reads 165F.
      7. Turn off the heat, scatter the olives and frozen peas over the top, and re-cover for 5 minutes. The residual heat warms them without turning the peas to mush.
      8. Pull the bay leaves, shred or chop the chicken into bite-size pieces, stir everything together, and finish with the chopped cilantro.

      Portion It

      Put the whole pot on the scale, subtract the empty-pot weight, and divide by 14. Scoop that number into each container and stack them in the fridge. Every bowl delivers 628 calories and 53 grams of protein.
