      Makes 14 servings · ~561 cal · 25g protein · 74g carbs · 19g fat · ~$1.40 per serving.

      Smoked turkey sausage over cheesy parmesan grits with an egg on top, this is Southern breakfast-for-dinner that works any hour. Each bowl carries 25 grams of protein for about $1.40, the cheapest bowl on this page by a country mile.

      • Yellow Onion: 3 (308 g)

      • White Vinegar (generic): 2.75 tbsp (42 g)
      • Eggs (Great Value): 14 large eggs (700 g)
      • Parmesan Cheese (Great Value): 1 oz (31 g)

      Shop Smart

      • Smoked turkey sausage at about $8.86 for 2.75 pounds is pre-cooked, pre-seasoned protein. You are paying for flavor someone else already built, which is rare good value in the meat case.
      • Grits are one of the last truly cheap staples standing, the canister covers this batch and several more. Old-fashioned grits cost the same as quick and taste better.
      • Eggs pull double duty as protein and topping at roughly $0.10 to $0.20 each depending on the week. One egg per bowl is the cheapest protein add-on in the store.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Bring the chicken broth plus enough water for your grits ratio to a boil, then whisk in the grits slowly. Drop the heat to low and simmer, stirring often, until thick and creamy, about 20 minutes.
      3. Stir the parmesan into the grits with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder.
      4. While the grits cook, slice the smoked turkey sausage into coins and brown them in the olive oil with the onion until both pick up color. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes for the last minute.
      5. Cook the eggs: bring a pot of water with the white vinegar to a bare simmer and poach the eggs 3 to 4 minutes, or fry them if that is more your speed. For meal prep, slightly firm yolks hold up best.
      6. Build bowls: cheesy grits down, sausage and onions over the top, egg on top of that.
      7. Reheat gently with a splash of water stirred into the grits, they tighten up in the fridge and loosen right back.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot of grits and sausage, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14, then add one egg per container. Each bowl lands at about 561 calories with 25 grams of protein.
