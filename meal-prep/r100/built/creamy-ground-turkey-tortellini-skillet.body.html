      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Brown the ground turkey in the olive oil in your big pot, breaking it up until no pink remains. Season with salt, pepper, and half the Italian seasoning.
      3. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste and cook 2 minutes until the paste darkens a shade.
      4. Add the tomato sauce, chicken broth, and remaining Italian seasoning. Bring to a simmer.
      5. Add the tortellini and simmer 4 to 6 minutes, stirring gently, until they are tender and puffed.
      6. Stir in the heavy cream and the thawed, squeezed-dry spinach and simmer 2 minutes more.
      7. Turn off the heat and stir in most of the parmesan until melted.
      8. Portion into 14 containers and dust with the remaining parmesan.

      Portion It

      Weigh the loaded pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scale that amount into each container. Each bowl finishes at about 567 calories with 31 grams of protein.
