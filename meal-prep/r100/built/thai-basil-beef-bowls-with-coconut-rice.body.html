      Shop Smart

      • The 93/7 ground beef is the big line at about $13.63 for 3.5 pounds. Leaner beef costs more per pound but you are not paying to drain fat down the sink, which matters when the sauce needs to cling.
      • Canned coconut milk is one of the most price-volatile items in the store. Stock up when it drops near $2 a can, it keeps for ages in the pantry.
      • Skip the pre-shredded carrots. Whole carrots run about $1.34 for what this recipe needs and shred in under two minutes with a box grater.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Make the coconut rice: combine the rice, coconut milk, and enough water to hit your usual rice liquid ratio, add a pinch of salt, and cook covered on low until tender. Fluff and set aside.
      3. Shred the carrots, slice the jalapeno and red bell pepper, and chop the green onions.
      4. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over high heat and brown the ground beef hard, breaking it up and letting it crisp. Add the garlic and jalapeno for the last 2 minutes.
      5. Stir in the soy sauce, lime juice, brown sugar, and a pinch of cayenne. Let it bubble until the liquid reduces to a glaze that coats the beef.
      6. Turn off the heat and fold in the fresh basil and bell pepper so they stay bright.
      7. Build bowls: coconut rice on the bottom, basil beef on top, shredded carrots and green onions to finish.

      Portion It

      Weigh your rice and beef together in the pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Portion to that number and each bowl lands at about 593 calories with 31 grams of protein.
