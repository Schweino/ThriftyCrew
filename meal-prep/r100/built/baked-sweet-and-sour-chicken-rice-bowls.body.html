      Shop Smart

      • Chicken breast in the 5 lb club pack is the price-per-pound winner, and the extra pound goes in the freezer for next week's batch.
      • Cornstarch, sugar, ketchup, and vinegar are all pantry cheapos that last many batches, so the real recurring cost of this dish is basically just the chicken and eggs.
      • Skip the bottled sweet and sour sauce. The homemade version here costs well under a dollar for the whole batch and you control the sugar.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Preheat the oven to 325F. Cut the chicken breast into bite-size cubes and season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
      3. Set up a dredge: cornstarch in one bowl, beaten eggs in another. Toss the chicken in cornstarch, then coat in egg.
      4. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Brown the chicken in batches, 1 to 2 minutes per side; it just needs color, not doneness yet. Move it to one or two 9x13 baking dishes.
      5. Whisk the sauce: sugar, ketchup, white vinegar, apple cider vinegar, and soy sauce. Pour it evenly over the chicken.
      6. Bake for about 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes so the sauce thickens and glazes every piece. Chicken is done at 165F.
      7. While it bakes, cook the rice with water per package directions.
      8. Combine the rice and glazed chicken in your big pot, scraping every bit of sauce out of the baking dishes.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty-pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Portion into 14 containers so every lunch is the same size. Each bowl checks in at 678 calories and 37 grams of protein.
