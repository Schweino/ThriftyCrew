      Makes 14 servings · ~570 cal · 31g protein · 63g carbs · 20g fat · ~$1.48 per serving.

      Jambalaya is Louisiana's one-pot argument that rice, meat, and seasoning are all a dinner really needs. Chicken thighs and smoked sausage simmer straight into the rice with tomatoes and Cajun spice. Each bowl runs 31 grams of protein and 570 calories at about $1.48. The pot does the dishes math for you: there is one.

      Shop Smart

      • Smoked turkey sausage delivers the same smoky backbone as pork andouille at a noticeably lower price per pound. Watch for the 13 oz ropes on multibuy deals.
      • Boneless skinless chicken thighs beat breasts here on both price and texture; they stay tender through the simmer instead of drying out.
      • Make your own Cajun seasoning from paprika, garlic powder, oregano, and cayenne you already own. The little jars of blends charge you a markup for someone else's measuring spoons.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat some of the olive oil in your largest heavy pot over medium-high. Brown the sliced smoked sausage 3 to 4 minutes until the edges crisp, then set aside.
      3. Add more oil and brown the chicken thigh chunks, seasoned with salt and pepper, until golden on the outside. They finish cooking in the rice.
      4. Add the diced onions and cook 5 to 6 minutes until soft, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom.
      5. Stir in the diced tomatoes, chicken broth, Cajun seasoning, garlic powder, dried basil, parsley, and black pepper, then bring to a boil.
      6. Add the dry rice, return the sausage and chicken, and stir once. Cover and simmer on low 22 to 25 minutes until the rice is tender and the chicken reaches 165F.
      7. Rest off the heat, covered, for 10 minutes, then fluff and taste for salt.

      Portion It

      Weigh the finished pot, subtract the empty-pot number, and divide by 14. Scoop that weight into each of 14 containers so every serving gets its fair share of sausage. Each bowl brings 570 calories and 31 grams of protein.
