      Makes 14 servings · ~575 cal · 27g protein · 85g carbs · 14g fat · ~$2.34 per serving.

      Warm cumin-spiced beef over rice with a generous swipe of hummus, cool cucumber, olives, and feta. It is the fast-casual Mediterranean bowl, except you keep the extra ten dollars. Each bowl brings 27 grams of protein for about $2.34, and there is no actual cooking beyond browning beef and making rice.

      • Rice (Member's Mark): 7 cups dry (1300 g)
      • Hummus (Great Value classic): 3.5 cups (784 g)
      • Cherry Tomatoes: 0.5 cups (60 g)

      • Red Onion: 1 (96 g)
      • Olives (generic): 3.5 cups (472 g)
      • Feta Cheese (Great Value crumbled): 7 oz (198 g)
      • Black Pepper (Great Value): 0.5 tbsp (4 g)

      Shop Smart

      • Store-brand hummus in the 10 ounce tubs was the best per-ounce deal on our board. The fancy brands charge nearly double for the same chickpeas.
      • Feta is the priciest line per ounce in this bowl. Buy the block or tub on sale and crumble it yourself, and never pay for pre-crumbled at full price.
      • Beef is the anchor cost. This recipe only needs 1.75 pounds across 14 servings because the hummus and feta carry flavor, which is exactly why it stays cheap.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice: 7 cups dry with water per your usual method.
      3. Brown the ground beef in your big pot over medium-high until no pink remains, then drain.
      4. Season the beef with the oregano, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper and cook 2 more minutes so the spices wake up.
      5. Chop the cucumber, halve the cherry tomatoes, and thin-slice the red onion.
      6. Build each bowl: rice on the bottom, a scoop of hummus on one side, spiced beef on the other, then cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, olives, and feta.
      7. Keep the fresh veg toppings in their own corner of the container so everything stays crisp until you eat.

      Portion It

      Weigh the pot of seasoned beef, subtract the empty pot number from step 1, and divide by 14 so each bowl gets an even share. Do the same with the rice. Each finished bowl comes in at 575 calories and 27 grams of protein.
