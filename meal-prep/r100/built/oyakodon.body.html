      Shop Smart

      • Boneless skinless thighs at about $2.48 a pound beat breast on price and are the traditional cut here anyway. The right choice is also the cheap one for once.
      • Eggs do heavy protein lifting at roughly 12 cents each by the dozen. When eggs go on sale, this recipe should move to the top of your list.
      • Mirin is the one specialty buy. One bottle covers this batch with plenty left, and a splash of it upgrades stir fry sauce and teriyaki for months.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice: 7 cups dry with water per your usual method.
      3. In your big pot, combine the chicken broth, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar and bring to a simmer.
      4. Add the chicken thighs cut into bite-size pieces and most of the green onions. Simmer 8 to 10 minutes until the chicken reads 165 F.
      5. Beat the eggs lightly, just until streaky, not uniform.
      6. Pour the eggs evenly over the simmering chicken, cover, and cook 2 to 3 minutes. For meal prep that reheats safely, cook the eggs fully set, not runny.
      7. Spoon the chicken and egg mixture with plenty of broth over rice in each container and top with the remaining green onions.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot number from step 1, and divide by 14, then ladle that weight over even scoops of rice. Every bowl lands at 747 calories and 48 grams of protein.
