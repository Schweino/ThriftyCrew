      Makes 14 servings · ~567 cal · 30g protein · 57g carbs · 23g fat · ~$2.35 per serving.

      Bright lemon rice cooked in chicken broth, topped with garlicky browned turkey, briny olives, and crumbled feta. Each bowl brings 30 grams of protein for about $2.35. It tastes like a Greek taverna plate that somehow costs less than a gas station sandwich.

      Shop Smart

      • The bottle of lemon juice covers the juice of about 7 lemons here for $1.30. Fresh lemons would run three times that, and the simmer hides the difference.
      • The 4 lb pack of 93/7 ground turkey beats the 1 lb trays on price per pound, and this recipe uses 3.75 lb of it, so there is almost no leftover to babysit.
      • Feta is the biggest line after the meat at $4.86. A block you crumble yourself usually beats the pre-crumbled tub per ounce.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Heat a splash of the olive oil in your biggest pot and cook the diced onion until soft, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute.
      3. Add the ground turkey and break it up as it browns. Cook until it reaches 165F and no pink remains. Season with salt and pepper.
      4. Stir in the rinsed rice, chicken broth, lemon juice, and enough water to cook the rice. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook 15 to 18 minutes until the rice is tender.
      5. Stir in the rest of the olive oil and the sliced olives, then taste for salt. Lemon rice should make your mouth water a little.
      6. Portion into 14 containers and top each with crumbled feta.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot number you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scoop that weight into each container and every bowl lands at about 567 calories and 30 grams of protein.
