      Shop Smart

      • Cheese is the second biggest line at $5.15 for 21 oz. The store brand 8 oz bags at $1.97 each are the play; the shredded blend melts the same as the name brand.
      • Salsa verde and canned green chiles do all the flavor work for about $7 combined. That replaces the roasting, peeling, and blending a restaurant bills you for.
      • The 4 lb turkey pack keeps the per-pound price down, and canned corn and seasoned black beans are shelf-stable, so stock up whenever they dip under a dollar.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice: rinse, simmer covered with water until tender, and set aside.
      3. Heat the olive oil in your biggest skillet or pot. Cook the diced red onion and bell pepper until soft, about 6 minutes, then add the garlic and cook 1 minute.
      4. Add the ground turkey and break it up as it browns. Cook to 165F with no pink remaining, then stir in the chili powder, cumin, paprika, oregano, salt, and black pepper.
      5. Stir in the green chiles, salsa verde, drained black beans, and drained corn. Simmer 8 to 10 minutes so the flavors settle in.
      6. Scatter the cheese over the top, cover, and let it melt for 2 to 3 minutes. Finish with lime juice and chopped cilantro.
      7. Portion the rice into 14 containers and top each with an even share of the skillet.

      Portion It

      Weigh the rice pot and the skillet, subtract the empty pot number, and divide each by 14 so every box gets its share of both. Each bowl comes out to about 723 calories and 40 grams of protein.
