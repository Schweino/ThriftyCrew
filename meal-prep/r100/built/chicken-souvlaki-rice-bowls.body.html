      Shop Smart

      • The marinade is pantry math: olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, and oregano. No bottled Greek dressing needed, and the flavor is better anyway.
      • Plain Greek yogurt from a $2.79 tub plus grated cucumber, garlic, and lemon makes the sauce. A pre-made tzatziki tub costs about double for half the amount.
      • Zucchini, red onion, and bell pepper roast down sweet and cost under $6 combined for the whole batch. Vegetables carry volume here so the chicken does not have to.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Marinate the chicken: cube the breasts and toss with most of the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Let it sit at least 30 minutes in the fridge.
      3. Cook the rice: 4.5 cups dry rice in a rice cooker or covered pot.
      4. Roast the vegetables: toss the chopped zucchini, red onion, and bell pepper with the remaining oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at 425 F for 20 to 25 minutes until browned at the edges.
      5. Sear the marinated chicken in a hot skillet in batches, 6 to 8 minutes, until charred at the edges and cooked through to 165 F.
      6. Make the yogurt sauce: stir the Greek yogurt with the grated cucumber (squeeze the water out first), a spoon of garlic, a squeeze of lemon juice, and a pinch of salt.
      7. Portion rice into 14 containers, then divide the chicken, roasted vegetables, olives, and halved cherry tomatoes. Pack the yogurt sauce in small cups on the side so it stays cold and fresh.

      Portion It

      Weigh your pot of cooked chicken and vegetables, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14 to portion evenly over the rice. Each bowl finishes at about 562 calories with 39 grams of protein.
