      Shop Smart

      • The 93/7 ground beef carries most of the cost here, so grab the 4 lb pack for the better per-pound price and freeze the extra half pound.
      • Store-brand spaghetti runs under a dollar a box. It is the same durum wheat as the name brands; the only difference is the marketing budget you are not paying for.
      • Mix your own taco seasoning from chili powder, cumin, paprika, and garlic powder. The packets charge a serious markup per ounce for a tablespoon of spices.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until just shy of al dente, then drain, saving a cup of pasta water.
      3. Meanwhile, brown the ground beef in your largest pot over medium-high heat, breaking it up, until no pink remains; ground beef should hit 160F. Drain excess fat.
      4. Add the garlic and diced jalapenos and cook 2 to 3 minutes, then stir in the taco seasoning and tomato paste.
      5. Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juice and simmer 8 to 10 minutes until the sauce thickens.
      6. Fold in the spaghetti with a splash of the reserved pasta water, then stir in the Mexican cheese blend until it melts into the sauce.
      7. Top with the mozzarella, cover on low heat 3 to 4 minutes until melted, then finish with the sliced olives and chopped cilantro.

      Portion It

      Weigh the loaded pot, subtract the empty-pot weight, and divide by 14. Tong even portions into 14 containers; twirl as needed. Each bowl checks in at 603 calories and 43 grams of protein.
