      Makes 14 servings · ~736 cal · 42g protein · 85g carbs · 26g fat · ~$2.48 per serving.

      Ground turkey does the volume, a little chorizo does the flavor, and cream cheese ties it all into a silky sauce over shells with zucchini and blistered cherry tomatoes. Each bowl serves up 42 grams of protein at 736 calories for about $2.48. The chorizo-to-turkey ratio is the money move here.

      Shop Smart

      • Three quarters of a pound of chorizo at $1.81 seasons three full pounds of turkey. Buying flavor in small amounts and volume in lean meat is one of the best tricks in budget cooking.
      • Cherry tomatoes are the splurge line at $6.86. If they are not on sale, a can of diced tomatoes gets you most of the way there for a third of the price.
      • Two bricks of store-brand 1/3 fat cream cheese make the sauce for $2.20. A jar of alfredo covering this much pasta would run over $6.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Boil the pasta shells 1 minute short of package time. Before draining, scoop out and save about 3 cups of the starchy pasta water.
      3. Brown the chorizo in a splash of oil in your big pot, breaking it up, then add the ground turkey and cook until no pink remains, seasoning with the Italian seasoning, paprika, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
      4. Add the diced zucchini and cook 4 to 5 minutes until just tender.
      5. Add the halved cherry tomatoes and cook 3 to 4 minutes until they blister and start to break down.
      6. Drop the cream cheese in chunks and stir with a ladle of the reserved pasta water until it melts into a smooth sauce. Add more pasta water until it is silky.
      7. Fold in the drained shells, lemon juice, parmesan, and basil, tossing until every shell is coated.
      8. Portion into 14 containers while the sauce is still loose; it thickens as it cools.

      Portion It

      Weigh the full pot, subtract the empty pot weight you wrote down, and divide by 14. Scale that into each container. Every bowl lands at about 736 calories with 42 grams of protein.
