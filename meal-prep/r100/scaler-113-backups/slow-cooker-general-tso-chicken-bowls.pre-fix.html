      That takeout craving hits, and the app is right there in your pocket ready to charge you fifteen bucks. This slow cooker General Tso's beats it on every front, packing 57 grams of protein into a bowl that costs about two dollars. One batch makes 14 servings, so you get a week and a half of dinners for less than a single delivery order.

      Ingredients

      • Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast (Member's Mark): 3,100 g (6.8 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 900 g (4.5 cups)
      • Hoisin Sauce: 120 g (120 g)
      • Soy Sauce: 120 g (120 g)
      • Brown Sugar: 90 g (90 g)
      • Cornstarch: 60 g (60 g)
      • Garlic: 8 g (3 clove)
      • Broccoli Florets (Great Value): 550 g (6.5 cups)

      Shop Smart

      • Chicken breast is the whole game here, so buy the big family pack when it dips near $2.88 a pound and freeze what you don't cook this week.
      • Hoisin and cornstarch last months in the pantry, so buy them once and forget them. You'll use a splash across a dozen recipes.
      • Frozen broccoli florets are cheaper than fresh and never go bad on you, so keep two bags in the freezer and skip the wilted-crown guilt.

      Make It

      1. Add the chicken breast, hoisin, soy sauce, brown sugar, and minced garlic to the slow cooker and give it a stir.
      2. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 7 hours, until the chicken shreds easily with a fork.
      3. Stir the cornstarch into a few tablespoons of cold water, then mix it in and let the sauce thicken for about 20 minutes.
      4. Toss the broccoli florets in during the last 30 minutes so they stay bright and tender-crisp.
      5. Shred or chunk the chicken right in the sauce, then serve it over cooked white rice.
