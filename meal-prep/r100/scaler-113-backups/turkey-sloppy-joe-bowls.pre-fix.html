      Adapted from The Real Food Dietitians.

      Makes 14 servings · ~681 cal · 46g protein · 56g carbs · 29g fat · ~$3.26 per serving.

      The messy sandwich you grew up on, turned into a tidy bowl over crispy potatoes. Each serving carries 46 grams of protein for about $3.45. Fourteen servings means the whole family or the whole week is handled.

      Ingredients

      • 93/7 Ground Turkey (Jennie-O): 3,160 g (7.0 lb)
      • Fries (Great Value): 3,051 g (467 pieces)
      • Tomato Paste (Great Value): 400 g (24 tbsp)
      • Yellow Onion: 300 g (1.9 cups)
      • Green Bell Peppers: 240 g (2 medium pepper)
      • Shredded Carrots: 170 g (6 oz)
      • Honey (Great Value): 42 g (2 tbsp)
      • Honey Dijon Mustard (Great Value): 40 g (8 tsp)

      Shop Smart

      • The 7 lb ground turkey pack is your cheapest protein play here, so buy the big size.
      • Buy frozen fries by the bag for the base. They are cheaper and last longer than fresh potatoes you cut yourself.
      • Honey and Dijon are one-bottle buys that last for months, so the cost per batch is basically nothing.

      Make It

      1. Bake the fries according to the bag while you get the meat going.
      2. Brown the ground turkey in a large skillet, then add the chopped onion, peppers, and shredded carrots.
      3. Stir in the tomato paste, honey, and honey Dijon mustard to build that sweet-tangy sloppy joe sauce.
      4. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick and simmer until the veggies are tender.
      5. Spoon the sloppy joe mix right over the baked fries and dig in.
