      Adapted from Trifecta Nutrition.

      Makes 14 servings · ~642 cal · 50g protein · 59g carbs · 19g fat · ~$2.96 per serving.

      Bright, zesty, and a little addictive, this Thai-style larb is proof that healthy prep never has to be boring. Each bowl brings 50 grams of protein for about $3.23 a serving. One skillet makes 14 servings.

      Ingredients

      • 93/7 Ground Turkey (Jennie-O): 3,300 g (7.3 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 950 g (4.8 cups)
      • Yellow Onion: 300 g (1.9 cups)
      • Lime Juice: 90 g (6 tbsp)
      • Honey (Great Value): 42 g (2 tbsp)
      • Green Bell Peppers: 300 g (3 medium pepper)
      • Olive Oil: 27 g (2 tbsp)

      Shop Smart

      • The 8 lb ground turkey pack is your best value, so buy the big size and portion it out.
      • A bottle of lime juice is cheaper than fresh limes for a big batch and lasts a long time in the fridge.
      • Fresh green peppers around a dollar each add crunch and color without much cost.

      Make It

      1. Heat a little olive oil in a large skillet and brown the ground turkey.
      2. Add the chopped onion and green peppers and cook until they soften.
      3. Stir in the lime juice and honey to build that classic sweet-and-sour larb balance.
      4. Let it simmer a couple minutes so the flavors soak in, then season with salt to taste.
      5. Serve it over cooked white rice with extra lime on the side.
