      Adapted from Well Plated.

      Makes 14 servings · ~570 cal · 67g protein · 56g carbs · 10g fat · ~$2.36 per serving.

      Chicken cacciatore is rustic Italian at its finest, all peppers, mushrooms, and tomato simmered until the flavors marry. This version tosses it with penne for a hearty pasta bowl at 67 grams of protein and about $2.70 a serving. One batch makes 14 servings, so a Sunday in the slow cooker sets up more than a week of dinners.

      Ingredients

      • Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast (Member's Mark): 3,300 g (7.3 lb)
      • Penne Pasta (Barilla): 980 g (2.2 lb)
      • Traditional Pasta Sauce (Great Value): 900 g (3.5 cups)
      • White Mushrooms: 450 g (6.4 cups)
      • Green Bell Peppers: 240 g (2 medium pepper)
      • Yellow Onion: 160 g (1.0 cup)
      • Olive Oil: 20 g (1 tbsp)
      • Garlic: 9 g (3 clove)

      Shop Smart

      • A jar of traditional pasta sauce beats making it from scratch on both time and cost, so grab the store-brand jar and doctor it with the veg.
      • Peppers and mushrooms are cheapest in season and on sale, so let the markdown bin pick your vegetables.
      • Cook the pasta separately and store it apart from the sauce, so it won't turn to mush over a week of reheating.

      Make It

      1. Add the chicken, pasta sauce, sliced mushrooms, chopped bell peppers, onion, olive oil, and garlic to the slow cooker.
      2. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 7 hours until the chicken shreds and the vegetables are tender.
      3. Shred the chicken into the sauce and stir everything together.
      4. Boil the penne separately until just al dente and drain it.
      5. Toss the pasta with the cacciatore sauce, or spoon the sauce over each portion of pasta.
