      Ingredients

      • Boneless skinless chicken breast (Member's Mark): 3.5 lb (1587 g, raw)
      • Jasmine rice (Member's Mark): 3.5 cups dry (700 g)
      • Broccoli florets (Great Value): 7 cups (595 g)
      • Hot honey (Better Goods): about 7 tbsp (147 g)

      Shop Smart

      • Chicken on sale, frozen, is the move. Buy a big pack when the price drops and freeze it in two-pound bags.
      • A little hot honey goes far. One bottle glazes several batches, so the per-serving cost stays tiny even though it feels like a treat.
      • Frozen broccoli is cheaper than fresh, lasts for months, and cooks right from the bag.

      Make It

      1. Weigh your empty mixing pot and write the number down for portioning later.
      2. Cook the rice. Three and a half cups dry jasmine with water per the package, then set aside.
      3. Cube the chicken and cook in a large skillet over medium-high heat until it reaches 165F inside.
      4. Steam or microwave the broccoli until bright and tender.
      5. Turn the heat to low, drizzle the hot honey over the chicken, and toss until glazed.
      6. Combine the rice, hot honey chicken, and broccoli in your big pot and stir.

      Portion It

      Set the full pot on your scale and subtract the empty-pot weight from Step 1. Divide that total by 14 and scoop the exact amount into each container. Every bowl lands near 357 calories and 31 grams of protein at just 3 grams of fat.
