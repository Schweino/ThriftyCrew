      Adapted from The Protein Chef.

      Makes 14 servings · ~567 cal · 57g protein · 66g carbs · 7g fat · ~$1.88 per serving.

      Orange chicken you can meal prep, and it is the cheapest bowl on the whole list. Bright, sweet, and savory, it packs in the protein while keeping the fat low and the price under two bucks. One batch makes 14 servings.

      Ingredients

      • Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast (Member's Mark): 3,100 g (6.8 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 980 g (4.9 cups)
      • Orange Juice: 240 g (1.0 cup)
      • Soy Sauce: 60 g (60 g)
      • Honey (Great Value): 84 g (4 tbsp)
      • Cornstarch: 32 g (32 g)
      • Sesame Oil: 14 g (14 g)
      • Garlic: 8 g (3 clove)
      • Broccoli Florets (Great Value): 500 g (5.9 cups)

      Shop Smart

      • A carton of orange juice is far cheaper than bottled orange sauce and gives you the real citrus flavor.
      • Cornstarch thickens the sauce into that glossy takeout texture for pennies.
      • Buy the chicken breast in bulk to keep this the cheapest bowl in your rotation.

      Make It

      1. Add the chicken breast and minced garlic to the crock.
      2. Pour in the orange juice, soy sauce, honey, and a splash of sesame oil.
      3. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 8 hours until the chicken is tender.
      4. Shred the chicken, then whisk the cornstarch with a little water and stir it in to thicken the sauce.
      5. Steam the broccoli and cook the rice.
      6. Serve the orange chicken over rice with broccoli.
