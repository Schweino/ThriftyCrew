      Adapted from Begin With Balance.

      Makes 14 servings · ~579 cal · 55g protein · 62g carbs · 9g fat · ~$2.70 per serving.

      Salsa verde does the heavy lifting here, so you get bright, tangy pork with almost no effort. It stacks up 55 grams of protein per bowl over rice and corn, all for right around $2.99 a serving. Fourteen servings from one batch is a whole lot of lunch for the money.

      Ingredients

      • Pork Tenderloin: 3,300 g (7.3 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 940 g (4.7 cups)
      • Salsa Verde (Herdez): 450 g (30 tbsp)
      • Diced Green Chiles (Old El Paso): 120 g (8 tbsp)
      • Sweet Whole Kernel Corn (Great Value): 750 g (3.0 cups)
      • Chicken Broth (Swanson): 480 g (2.0 cups)
      • Lime Juice: 45 g (3 tbsp)
      • Garlic: 12 g (4 clove)

      Shop Smart

      • Pork tenderloin runs lean and clean, but watch the tag. If it is over $4 a pound, check for pork loin instead and save real money on the same batch.
      • A single jar of salsa verde does most of the seasoning, so you skip buying a handful of separate spices.
      • Buy lime juice in a bottle rather than fresh limes for batch cooking. It keeps in the fridge and you only use a splash.

      Make It

      1. Set the pork tenderloin in the slow cooker.
      2. Pour the salsa verde, diced green chiles, and chicken broth over the top.
      3. Add a squeeze of lime juice and the minced garlic.
      4. Cover and cook on low for 7 to 8 hours until the pork pulls apart easily.
      5. Shred the pork and stir it back into all those juices, then fold in the corn.
      6. Spoon over cooked white rice and divide into containers.
