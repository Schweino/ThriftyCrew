      Makes 14 servings · ~651 cal · 52g protein · 59g carbs · 19g fat · ~$2.97 per serving.

      Sweet, savory, and a little garlicky, these bowls taste like a treat but cost like a bargain. Each one packs 52 grams of protein for about $3.29 a serving. Fourteen servings means your week of lunches is sorted.

      Ingredients

      • 93/7 Ground Turkey (Jennie-O): 3,300 g (7.3 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 900 g (4.5 cups)
      • Broccoli Florets (Great Value): 700 g (8.2 cups)
      • Teriyaki Sauce (Kikkoman): 200 g (11 tbsp)
      • Honey (Great Value): 63 g (3 tbsp)
      • Olive Oil: 27 g (2 tbsp)
      • Garlic: 27 g (9 clove)

      Shop Smart

      • Grab the 8 lb ground turkey pack for the best price per pound on your main ingredient.
      • Buy frozen broccoli florets instead of fresh. They are cheaper, keep longer, and cook up just fine here.
      • One bottle of teriyaki and a squeeze of honey go a long way, so buy them once and reuse for months.

      Make It

      1. Heat a little olive oil in a large skillet and brown the ground turkey.
      2. Stir in the minced garlic and cook until fragrant, about a minute.
      3. Add the teriyaki sauce and honey and let it simmer so the turkey soaks up the glaze.
      4. Steam or microwave the broccoli florets until just tender and fold them in.
      5. Serve over cooked white rice with the sauce spooned on top.
