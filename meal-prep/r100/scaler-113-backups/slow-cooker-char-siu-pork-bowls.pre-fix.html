      Adapted from Making Life Delicious.

      Makes 14 servings · ~562 cal · 55g protein · 51g carbs · 13g fat · ~$1.63 per serving.

      Char siu is that sweet, glossy Chinese barbecue pork you see hanging in restaurant windows, and here it comes out of the slow cooker for pennies. Each bowl runs 55 grams of protein over rice and broccoli at just $1.76. Fourteen servings from one batch is a steal you will want on repeat.

      Ingredients

      • Pork Loin: 3,300 g (7.3 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 640 g (3.2 cups)
      • Broccoli Florets (Great Value): 900 g (10.6 cups)
      • Honey (Great Value): 120 g (6 tbsp)
      • Chicken Broth (Swanson): 240 g (1.0 cup)
      • Hoisin Sauce: 120 g (120 g)
      • Soy Sauce: 120 g (120 g)

      Shop Smart

      • Hoisin sauce is the secret to that char siu taste. One jar lasts through several recipes, so buy it once and it earns its shelf space.
      • Stick with pork loin over tenderloin here. It stays juicy through the long cook and cuts your protein cost nearly in half.
      • Honey in the squeeze bottle keeps forever and you only need a few tablespoons, so it barely dents this recipe's cost.

      Make It

      1. Lay the pork loin in the slow cooker.
      2. Stir together the hoisin sauce, soy sauce, honey, and chicken broth, then pour it over.
      3. Cover and cook on low for 7 to 8 hours until the pork is fall-apart tender.
      4. Shred the pork and stir it back through the glaze.
      5. Steam the broccoli florets until bright green and just tender.
      6. Serve over cooked white rice with the broccoli alongside and pack it up.
