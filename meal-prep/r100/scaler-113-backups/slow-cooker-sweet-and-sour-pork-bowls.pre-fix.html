      Makes 14 servings · ~581 cal · 55g protein · 67g carbs · 8g fat · ~$2.89 per serving.

      This sweet and sour pork is the meal-prep answer to your favorite Chinese takeout. Lean pork tenderloin cooks low and slow with peppers, onion, and pineapple in a honey soy glaze, then goes over rice. It runs a touch higher at around three dollars a serving, but you still get 55 grams of protein across 14 bowls.

      Ingredients

      • Pork Tenderloin: 3,200 g (7.1 lb)
      • Rice (Member's Mark): 950 g (4.8 cups)
      • Green Bell Peppers: 357 g (3 medium pepper)
      • Red Bell Pepper: 300 g (300 g)
      • Yellow Onion: 250 g (1.6 cups)
      • Pineapple Chunks: 300 g (300 g)
      • Honey (Great Value): 120 g (6 tbsp)
      • Soy Sauce: 120 g (120 g)

      Shop Smart

      • Tenderloin costs more than loin, so watch the sales. Buy tenderloin only when it drops below four dollars a pound and stock the freezer.
      • Canned pineapple in juice is cheaper than fresh and adds sweetness for free. Grab the canned pineapple in juice, not syrup, to skip the added sugar.
      • Bell peppers are cheapest in the multi-packs. Buy peppers by the bag when your store carries them that way.

      Make It

      1. Add the pork tenderloin, sliced peppers, and onion to the slow cooker.
      2. Stir the honey, soy sauce, and pineapple with its juice together, then pour it over the top.
      3. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 7 hours until the pork is tender.
      4. Cook the rice while the pork finishes.
      5. Cut or shred the pork into bite-sized pieces and stir it back through the sauce.
      6. Spoon the pork and vegetables over rice and drizzle with extra sauce.
