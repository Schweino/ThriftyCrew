      Adapted from Hungry Hobby.

      Makes 14 servings · ~588 cal · 65g protein · 47g carbs · 15g fat · ~$2.59 per serving.

      Crack chicken earned its nickname honestly, because the creamy, cheesy, bacon-flecked flavor is genuinely hard to stop eating. This bowl packs 65 grams of protein for about $3.07 a serving, served over tender potatoes. It runs a touch pricier than the others, but 14 servings from one pot still makes it a bargain against takeout.

      Ingredients

      • Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast (Member's Mark): 3,450 g (7.6 lb)
      • Potato: 3,500 g (7.7 lb)
      • 1/3 Fat Cream Cheese (Great Value): 140 g (10 tbsp)
      • Cheddar Cheese, Shredded (Great Value): 170 g (1.5 cups)
      • Ranch Seasoning Mix (Hidden Valley): 45 g (5 tbsp)
      • Hickory Smoked Bacon (Great Value): 60 g (8 cooked slices)
      • Chicken Broth (Swanson): 240 g (1.0 cup)

      Shop Smart

      • Bacon is the priciest bit here, so use it as a topping, not a base, and a little goes a long way for that smoky hit.
      • Skip the packet and mix your own ranch seasoning from dried herbs, which is cheaper and lets you dial back the salt.
      • The lower-fat cream cheese and a modest handful of cheddar keep this rich without wrecking the budget, so buy the store-brand blocks.

      Make It

      1. Add the chicken, cream cheese, ranch seasoning, and chicken broth to the slow cooker.
      2. Nestle the cut potatoes around the chicken so they cook in the creamy broth.
      3. Cover and cook on low for 6 to 7 hours until the potatoes are tender and the chicken shreds.
      4. Cook and crumble the bacon while everything finishes.
      5. Shred the chicken, stir in the shredded cheddar until melted, then top with the crumbled bacon and serve.
