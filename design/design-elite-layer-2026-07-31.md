# The Elite Layer: from "clean and fast" to "wow"

Written 2026-07-31 by Fable, synthesized from a 5-lens design review (visual craft, interaction, mobile app-feel, data storytelling, skeptic teardown), each lens filtered by a feasibility-and-taste judge, plus a completeness critic. 62 proposals survived judging; this document is the curated, deduplicated result. Raw surviving proposals with full specs: `elite-layer-raw-proposals-2026-07-31.json` in this folder.

READ FIRST: `redesign-board-mealprep-2026-07-31.md` (v1). v1 is the foundation (performance, sticky diet, search/sort, free badges, floating trip bar, store colors, design tokens) and everything here assumes it. Nothing in this file replaces a v1 item; several items here deliberately merge INTO v1 items and say so.

Brad's question was "will a user look at it and say Wow, this is amazing, it looks great and it is super easy to use?" v1 alone: no. v1 plus this layer: that is the design intent, and each piece below earns its place against that test.

---

## The concept: a kept ledger you can shop from

The site's personality is already "a careful person checked these prices and wrote them down." The elite layer commits to that identity in three moves:

1. **Materiality.** The interfaces borrow the physical language of money kept well: register receipts with perforated edges and dotted leaders, a ledger's double rules and index tabs, one chalkboard for records, ink stamps for freshness. Not a theme park; each motif appears where its real-world counterpart would (costs look like receipts, the item list looks like a ledger, records look like the board behind a counter).
2. **App-grade feel.** On phones the pages behave like a native app: bottom sheets, a full-screen Aisle Mode with wake lock for shopping, Cook Mode for recipes, state that survives reloads, 44px targets, haptic ticks, one motion vocabulary.
3. **Numbers that prove themselves.** The rare data (7 stores every morning, 23 weeks of history, record lows) stops hiding in prose and becomes the visual layer: a masthead tally, sparklines, savings deltas, a protein-per-dollar leaderboard. Every displayed number is computed from the same source the page already trusts, never a second copy of the math.

Gold #E2A43C is reserved for money moments only (savings, records, totals, FREE). That discipline is what keeps the wow from becoming noise.

## Design system additions (extends the v1 token include)

- **Type scale.** Georgia display at 3 fixed steps (page title ~2.1em, section head 1.45em, card head 1.1em), letter-spacing -0.015em at the two large steps. Section heads become a stanza: gold letterspaced EYEBROW, Georgia head, one-line muted dek. 56px above every stanza, consistent sub-spacing, replacing today's ad hoc margins. Applied to EVERY section head in all three builders in one pass (half-adopted reads worse than none). Prices: tabular-nums + weight 750, always plain text nodes (no superscript spans; PS builders and JS templates must stay interchangeable).
- **Depth.** Three levels replacing flat 1px gray borders. Resting: 1px solid #e7e2d4 + 3px solid #ddd6c2 bottom (letterpress). Raised (hover/expanded/featured): translateY(-1px), shadow 0 6px 16px rgba(22,38,63,.08), gold bottom border. Overlay: existing deep shadow. Radius unified: 14px containers, 10px chips. Cards only, never buttons or pills.
- **Motion vocabulary.** One easing cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1); durations 100ms press / 200ms state / 300ms choreography; one shared rAF number-tween (~15 lines, announces to aria-live once at the end, never per frame); one SVG check snippet (stroke-dashoffset draw). Every interactive element gets :active (touch parity) and :focus-visible (2px gold ring). The whole motion layer sits inside @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference). Nothing animates on page load on the hub (Ads landing). Transforms and opacity only, never layout properties.
- **Navy bands.** Full-column navy panel class (masthead, trip summary, hub leaderboard, membership CTA); cream reserved for asides. Two rules enforced by a cheap self-check grep in the builders, not just a comment: never two navy bands adjacent, navy never behind body prose.
- **Icons.** 8 functional icons max (basket, tag, receipt, flame, pin, calendar, envelope, check), adapted from one MIT stroke set onto a 24px grid, 1.75px round caps, currentColor, inline. Shipping gate: a 16px-actual-size contact sheet reviewed before any builder references them. Never on board rows (node budget); eyebrows, category heads, stat band, footer only.
- **Bottom-edge stacking policy (binding).** One z-index ladder in the tokens include: full-screen modes (Aisle/Cook) > interstitial > primary action bar (trip bar on board, mini-scaler on recipe) > transient pills (resume, toasts). At most ONE bar plus ONE transient visible; the email slide-in never fires while the trip bar is up; the interstitial is fully suppressed while Aisle or Cook mode is open; everything respects safe-area-inset-bottom. Log suppression counts for a week after shipping so a bug cannot silently cost signups.
- **Light-only, on the record.** color-scheme: light on :root; one navy theme-color for all schemes; no dark variants anywhere. Recorded in the tokens comment so no future agent helpfully adds dark mode to one surface. (Brad signs this.)

## The six signature moments

These are the "show a friend" features. Everything else supports them.

1. **Aisle Mode (board).** From the trip planner output, each store gets a gold "Shop this store" button opening a full-screen overlay built at tap time (zero initial nodes): navy header with store name + color dot, giant 64px tap rows (item 1.1em, price 1.6em bold right-aligned) on cream, tap anywhere to strike through and sink the row, footer with "4 of 11" progress + remaining total counting down, navigator.wakeLock so the screen never sleeps in the aisle (re-acquire on visibilitychange, silent skip where unsupported), check state in localStorage per store per board week so a reload in the store resumes exactly, hardware-back closes (pushState/popstate), overscroll-behavior:contain. Zero network requests after open; footer line: "Works with no signal. Your checkmarks are saved on this phone." Effort L, wow 5.
2. **Cook Mode (recipe).** From the jump nav: full-screen step-by-step built from the existing Make It ol. Tap right two-thirds to advance, left third to go back (no swipe handlers; they fight scroll). Georgia ~1.5em steps, counter chip, gold progress bar, half-height ingredients sheet reading the scaler's CURRENT quantities, wake lock, last-step per slug in localStorage, under 4 KB. Inline fallback: steps are also tappable in the normal page (dim done steps, gold check on the number badge, "Picking up at step 4" restore line). Effort M, wow 5.
3. **The receipt (recipe).** "What This Batch Costs" restyled as a literal register receipt: #fffdf6 paper, CSS zigzag tear edges (8px teeth), dotted leaders, ui-monospace quantities, TOTAL under a 3px double rule, the store line as a rotated gold ink stamp. Unchecking an ingredient draws a 200ms strike-through, dims the row, rolls the total down with the shared tween, and accumulates a gold line: "Already in your kitchen: $6.40". Above the tabs, one computed sentence: "Shopping this batch at each ingredient's cheapest store saves $6.40 versus everyday prices this week" (suppressed under $1; it is the subtraction of two totals the widget already computes, and it RESOLVES v1 recipe item 1's two-price confusion with a reason instead of just labels). Function untouched; classnames and CSS plus the animation layer. Effort M, wow 5.
4. **The demo basket + receipt trip output (board).** A build-time "Try it: the family staples basket" chip beside the planner: one tap checks ~15 curated ids (build fails if coverage drops below 15, alternates swap in), drives the EXISTING solver and scrolls to output. Output restyled as mini receipts per store: tear edges, store color as the receipt's top band, dotted leaders, subtotal over a single rule, horizontal scroll-snap rail at 375px (80vw cards, next-card peek). Under it, the solver's own honest line, plus, when a single store covers every picked item, the comparison: "All at one store, even the best one: $47.30. You keep $5.20." (suppressed when no store has full coverage; never an invented denominator). Share button: navigator.share plain-text list grouped by store with prices (clipboard fallback, under 1,500 chars). Effort M, wow 5.
5. **Sparklines (board).** In the lazily built expanded panel: a ~110x28 inline SVG polyline of the 23-week cheapest-price history, gold dot on the record low, "Record: $1.98, Apr 22" label. Data from the SAME lazy price-history.json fetch the history pill already uses (worker URL, cached after first interaction; zero initial bytes). Labeled "best price in Omaha, weekly" so it never reads as one store's chart. Suppressed under 6 weeks of history. Free tier: the line shape plus the record label visible; numeric axis values, per-week figures, and alerts stay behind the existing gate (Brad signs the tease depth). Hard dependency: v1 P0-1 ships first. Effort M, wow 5.
6. **The shuffle (hub).** Changing sort (cheapest / protein-per-dollar / lowest cal) runs a FLIP animation on the visible slice only (~12-16 cards): measure, reorder DOM, invert, 300ms ease-out with 15ms stagger; offscreen cards jump instantly under content-visibility. Watching $0.87 chicken recipes physically slide to the top demonstrates the brand argument better than copy. Interaction-only (never on load; Ads page), travel capped at one viewport (fade instead beyond). Effort M, wow 5.

## Per-surface additions (beyond v1 and the signatures)

### Board (all wave 2, behind the freeze)

- **ONE masthead, not five (binding conflict ruling).** The lenses independently proposed five top-of-page modules (live-tally hero, This Week stat band, freshness receipt, wrong-store tax headline, ledger banner) plus a Biggest Drops card row. Together they would rebuild the pileup v1 P2-7 just dieted. Merged spec: ONE navy masthead band under the H1/dek (SEO text untouched), radial depth gradient (#1E3A5F to #16263F), inset gold hairline, three lines max:
  1. Freshness: "Checked this morning · 7 stores · new ads Wednesday (2 days)". Timestamp = max NewestUpd across store files; degrades honestly to a date when a pull is stale (and a not-today timestamp also feeds send-alert triage). Countdown from the ad-schedule window already in the build.
  2. Tally in small caps tabular: "430 items · 2,600+ prices verified · 23 weeks tracked". Every figure computed in-build, round-down only.
  3. The wrong-store stat sentence: "The same item costs a median 34% more at the wrong store. We check so you never pay it." (median within-row spread across rows with 4+ priced stores, membership cells excluded via the existing nomem filter; if the computed number is under 15%, the line is dropped, not massaged) with the week's biggest drop as a tappable gold chip ("Pork loin down 31%") that scrolls to and expands that row. Drop computation joins price-history like the record badges do, reuses the existing 30%-outlier guard, requires 2+ prior weeks, floor rule: under 10% show a record low instead; flat weeks read "Prices held steady this week". Must-fire fixture on a known drop + clean twin on a flat week, wired into test-auditors.
  Kill: the separate Biggest Drops 3-card module, the standalone stat band, duplicating tally numbers in pg-board-sub. Acceptance test: first rows visible within 1.5 viewports of the H1 at 375px, net BETTER than today after the v1 trust-copy collapse.
- **Ledger rows.** Dotted leaders between name and price (min-width 12px, order: name, store dot-chip, leader, price), warm hairlines #eee9dc, +2px row padding, category heads as index tabs (8px gold thumb tab left, count in a navy pill right), 3px double rule opening each section. CSS + one spacer span per row.
- **Chalkboard records strip.** The one dark panel: #101B2E, chalk-white chips, gold prices, secondary text #b9c4d4 (AA), eyebrow THE RECORD BOARD. No texture SVGs, no handwriting fonts. Rides v1 P2-7's strip tightening. The chalkboard treatment appears nowhere else.
- **Record flags on rows.** The existing record badge surfaces as a small gold flag in the same slot as v1's SALE tick, capped per category; expanded panel of record rows adds one line to the existing alerts pill: "Want an email next time this happens? That is the $1 membership." One-shot shimmer only on records set or tied THIS week, single IntersectionObserver, build-time cap (~15; above that, no shimmer classes at all and a log line). Hard dependency: the $0.00 record data fix (v1 P0-3) first.
- **Live savings ticker in the trip bar.** v1's floating bar becomes a money readout: on pick change, debounce 300ms, call the SAME solver function (refactor to one shared function; never a parallel sum) and show the cheapest-trip total with a 250ms count-up. Savings line baseline: best single-store total among stores pricing >= 80% of the basket ("even the best one-store trip costs $X more"); below 80% coverage, count + total only. Copy past Brad.
- **Bottom-sheet store comparison at <=640px.** Row tap opens the lazy panel as a bottom sheet (70vh, 16px top radius, CHEAPEST first with gold border, scrim/X/hardware-back dismiss ONLY, no drag physics, no lying drag handle). Desktop keeps inline expansion. Row expansion inline (desktop) animates grid-template-rows 0fr to 1fr, 220ms, staggered store cards, one-time gold underline sweep on the CHEAPEST card; expand-all skips animation.
- **Scroll-synced rail.** One IntersectionObserver marks the current category's chip active and auto-centers it in the rail (container scrollTo, not scrollIntoView, to avoid vertical jumps). Real-device test against content-visibility; fallback to throttled offsetTop.
- **Checkbox.** One spec everywhere (conflict ruling): 24px, real input visually replaced, SVG stroke-draw check in gold, 140ms, cream row pulse on check, instant uncheck, navigator.vibrate(10) on Android, reduced-motion instant. No flying tokens.
- **Persistence + resume pill.** Board picks keyed by commodity id + board week stamp; restore silently on load; same-week saved list shows a dismissible pill under the rail ("Your list from earlier is loaded, 12 items · Clear"); prior-week lists are DISCARDED (stale picks against re-priced rows are a board-data-integrity bug; stamp check goes into the guard fixtures).

## The states nobody designs (from the completeness critic; all in scope)

- **Empty states that recover.** Board zero-result search: cream card with "No matches for chik. Check the spelling, or ask us to track it." + Clear + /suggest-an-item/ link. Hub zero-result filters: "Nothing under 400 calories in Thai this week" + one-tap relax chips computed by re-running the REAL filter function with each constraint dropped (never parallel logic). Never a blank void under a sticky bar.
- **Loading and failure.** Shared rule: async slots show nothing for 300ms, then a cream 3-line skeleton pulse; on failure, honest copy, never a spinner forever ("Could not load the 23-week history right now. The prices above are still today's." / "Showing everyday prices; live store prices would not load"). Trip ticker shows the last total dimmed while solving, never a flash to $0.00. Every degraded state gets a forced-failure test (block the URL, 375px).
- **The unlock moment.** On Portal success (hashchange + visibilitychange cookie recheck), re-run the existing TC_PAID detection (build-deals-page.ps1 already fetches /members/api/member/ at ~line 1388, so tier detection exists) and flip the page live without a reload: gates open, blurs lift, join CTAs hide, one gold toast: "You are in. Price history and alerts are unlocked." Same pass makes every surface consistent for returning members: paying members never see $1 buttons, the interstitial, or FREE badges pitched at them.
- **Email capture after-state.** Once a free signup exists, the capture panel swaps to its receipt-stamped after state ("You are on the Friday list. First board lands Friday morning.") and the slide-in never fires again. Copy must also read correctly for paid members.
- **The formerly-free gate.** Shared links outlive the Friday rotation, so the gated-recipe CTA becomes a warm no: "This one went back behind the counter when the free five rotated Friday." + the five currently-free recipes as real cards + the $1 pitch. Confirm which slot Ghost 6 exposes for paid-gap content before promising per-recipe dynamic cards; fall back to a static five refreshed by the rotation chain. Same pass styles the 404 page (search, free five, board + hub links, site voice).
- **Print styles.** One @media print block: nav/CTAs/floating everything hidden; recipes print as ingredients + receipt (checked state preserved, real checkbox squares, leaders intact) + steps; trip output prints as the store-grouped list with totals; the board suppresses mass printing (collapsed rows only). Print-preview added to the per-wave verification checklist so it cannot silently rot.
- **Focus and screen readers.** :focus-visible gold ring everywhere; tweened numbers announce once at end via the shared helper; real checkboxes; aria-expanded on rows; sheets and modes trap focus, close on Escape, return focus to the trigger (the pushState handling doubles for this).
- **Branded link previews (OG images).** 1200x630 navy cards via the existing GDI+ pipeline (Georgia title, gold price, shelf-tag motif, wordmark), uploaded once per post, og_image/twitter_image ONLY (never feature_image). Recipes: title + per-serving price + protein per dollar. Board: weekly tally, regenerated weekly. Rides the resume/change-gate publish path. Effort L; the only wow that reaches people before they visit.

Nothing anywhere REQUIRES a photo; the typographic system is the universal fallback. Brad shoots or it stays text, both by design.
6. Light-only on the record.
7. De-Ghost nav look (from a screenshot) and Portal accent/copy pass (gold accent, receipt-line benefits; config only, no iframe hacks).
8. Interstitial restyle timing.
9. Freeze exceptions, if any (unchanged from v1: the two live display bugs).

## Success criterion

A first-time phone visitor should have three reactions in the first minute: "this looks genuinely crafted" (masthead, receipt, ledger, chrome), "it responds like an app" (press states, sheets, the ticker), and "these numbers are real" (freshness, provenance, sparklines). And one moment worth showing someone else: uncheck rice and watch the receipt pay you back, or Aisle Mode in the store. That is the bar v1 alone did not clear.
