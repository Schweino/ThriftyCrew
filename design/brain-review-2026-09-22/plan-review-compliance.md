# Compliance with estate rules and safety (CLAUDE.md x3, .claude/rules/*, critic.md "Anything the plan must not do"), including settings.json blast radius

Mostly sound in intent. The plan honours most of critic.md's must-not list: no edit-time similarity recall, the chash cap stays, GIT_INDEX_FILE is not scrubbed in commit-msg, no -SyncScopes from a worktree, C:\Codex\CLAUDE.md is left to Brad, every new rung starts in shadow, and most new gates get a cutoff date. It is not yet safe to hand to a lower-effort builder. Four gaps would break sessions or gates. (1) There are six settings.json edits and no procedure for making them. That file is re-read live, holds bypassPermissions, and runs 13 hook commands with no timeouts. (2) The scheduled-task SKILL steps rely on a sync that does not exist, and editing a live SKILL before its mirror lands reds every other session's push. (3) W6.4 runs the graph ingest in the main tree, which the digest and critic both forbid. (4) W4.5's brain commit-msg hook would refuse on day one: by my trailer heuristic, 15 of 19 recent Claude code commits in the brain repo carry no Store: line, and brain commits cannot resolve the ThriftyCrew plan path that section 4 tells them to cite. There are also measured medium defects. W0.1's widened PATH_RE creates new refusals, so section 10's "only widens what passes" is false. W2.4's lock times out inside its own holder's retry budget and has no MUST FIRE for the timeout branch. W3.2's header edit to pricing-math-lib trips the conclusion-currency ratchet, which sits at its mark. W5.2 RULE 3 is red on day one for two agents. The sleep pass commits without a pathspec. Smaller items: a null trap in the triage validator, cache and prune races, and a ratchet exemplar in check-skills that fails on a fall. Each issue below gives the exact replacement text. Every one was checked against the tree or by a command whose exit code I read; the one heuristic count is labelled as such.
