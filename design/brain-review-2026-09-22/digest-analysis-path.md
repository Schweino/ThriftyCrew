# analysis-path

During an analysis stretch the brain delivers almost nothing by mechanism, and nothing at all about the analysis METHOD. Always-on text is the channel that actually works. All six ThriftyCrew .claude/rules files, measurement.md included, load at session start in every TC and TC-worktree session measured (23 of 23 and 12 of 12), even though their front matter says globs + alwaysApply:false. So the scoping is not operative. It also means measurement.md reaches only sessions whose cwd is under ThriftyCrew: 0 of 9 C:\Codex sessions got it. Read/Grep/Glob/WebFetch fire no PreToolUse hook. On Bash/PowerShell, recall-intent-hook fires only when the description contains a judgement verb or a store-subject word. That leaves it silent on analysis verbs like measure, compare and count: of 997 measurement-worded descriptions, 215 fire. Its offers are never logged, so tier 2 cannot be measured. On Edit/Write it is inert: 1 of 1,938 calls carry a description. PostToolUse only logs opens. At the answer, Stop runs the Consulted gate and the absence (Checked:) rung, but the Consulted gate never sees what the prompt hook offered. recall-hook.py never records offers into session state, so the gate counted an offer on 25 of 224 prompt-offered turns in 7 days (41 of 1,188 since 09-10). The footer appears 73% of the time only because the injected block asks for it. SubagentStop runs neither rung: 0 of 699 gate rows carry an agent id. Retrieval matches the subject, not the method. Over 7 days experiment-craft was offered 4 times out of 973 offers and opened once out of 1,572 opens. Over 14 human-typed analysis prompts, experiment-craft was offered 0 of 14, knowledge-search ran 2 of 14, and footers named something in 4 of 14. Record-level checks exist and work for MEASURE docs: provenance ratchet, conclusion currency and the Knowledge consulted rule. Chat answers, triage verdicts and delegated findings have no analysis check except the absence rung.
