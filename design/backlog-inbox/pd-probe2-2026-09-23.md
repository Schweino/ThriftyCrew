**What is wrong.** The probe's literal bars table (W0.3 step 4) resolves each bar's landing from the FIRST commit on the
main ref whose message carries `Plan: design/PLAN-push-derived-conflicts-2026-09-23.md <item id>`, the id matched as a
whole token, so `W2.1` never matches `W2.1R`. Section 15.4 of the plan says W2.1R "replaces W2.1" and W2.2R "replaces
W2.2", and 15.9 orders the push-main lane by the R ids. The table still names the old ids: B1 judges `W2.1`, B2 and B3
`W2.2`, B8 and B9 `W2.1, W2.2, W2.3`, B10 `W2.1, W2.2, W2.3, W6.1`. If the W2.1R and W2.2R landing commits carry
`Plan: ... W2.1R` and `Plan: ... W2.2R`, as the brief convention for this build says, those six bars read NOT LANDED for
ever and `-Due` never pages for them: a silent alarm, not a loud one. W0.3b did not change these rows, because a bar's
landing is part of its definition and is not what W0.3b was asked to change. It did rewrite B6's metric text to 15.6's
amended B6, and left B6's item as `W6.1`: 15.9 lands W6.1 after W2.1R, so the later of the two is W6.1 either way.

**First rung.** Decide one of the two, and write it into the plan's section 15.4: (a) the W2.1R and W2.2R landing
commits carry both ids on their Plan line (`Plan: design/PLAN-push-derived-conflicts-2026-09-23.md W2.1R W2.1`), which
the whole-token match already reads; or (b) the W2.1R lane edits the probe's bars table to name `W2.1R` and `W2.2R` in
the same commit. (a) needs no code and cannot be forgotten by a later lane only if the brief template says it.

## on this box, most landed changes fall in the contended stratum that b6 and b10 now split off

`OPEN` `2-WAY` `RUNG1 MEASURE`

**Source.** A plain `ops/probe-push-convergence.ps1 -Cost` run at 2026-09-23T15:41Z, after W0.3b, over the 7-day window
2026-09-16T15:41:16Z to 2026-09-23T15:41:16Z: 353 kept push-main rows, 247 changes, 238 landed. Every row predates W0.1,
so every change is population `unknown` and no row carries a session; the strata were decided by the hour rule alone,
from 321 reflog landings of `refs/remotes/origin/main`.

**What it shows.** 148 of 238 landed changes (62.2%) had an attempt begun in a UTC clock hour with 8 or more landings,
which 15.6 calls contended; 90 of 238 (37.8%) were quiet. Once W0.1R's `session` field arrives, parallel-run membership
can only move changes from quiet to contended. B6's quiet stratum needs at least 10 landed chain-touching changes, and
chain-touching is about 27% of landings over the same 7 days (87 of 323, `-History` section 1). So at this rate the
quiet stratum collects chain changes at roughly 0.378 x 0.27 of the landing rate, and B6's 14-day read-out may reach
its minimum N in the contended stratum well before the quiet one. This is one week of rows, all from copies before
W0.1, and one threshold value (8, the plan's first plausible value): it is a reason to look at the stratum counts on
B6's read-out date, not a verdict on the threshold.

**First rung.** On B6's read-out, run `-Cost -Bar B6` and read the quiet and contended change counts before judging
either; if the quiet stratum is under its minimum N, B6 reports NO VERDICT there rather than a pass, which is what the
probe prints.
