## the push plan's pm_blob rule, read literally, excludes every row for an item that does not change push-main

`OPEN` `2-WAY` `RUNG1 DOC`

**Source.** Building W0.3 of `design/PLAN-push-derived-conflicts-2026-09-23.md` (the `-Cost -Bar` row selection in
`ops/probe-push-convergence.ps1`), 2026-09-23.

**What is wrong.** Section 5 ("Rollout is per checkout") and W0.3 step 2 say a bar's treated rows are those whose
`pm_blob` maps to "the first origin/main commit carrying that blob of `ops/push-main.ps1`" at or after the item's
landing commit. A blob maps to the commit that INTRODUCED it. An item that does not change `ops/push-main.ps1` lands
on a commit newer than the one that introduced the push-main blob every updated checkout runs: W1.1 changes the hook
and its suite, and W4.1 is built in wave 1 without its push-main step 7. So, read literally, every row is
`older-copy` for B2b (W1.1) and B5 (W4.1), and neither bar could collect a treated row until somebody next changes
push-main. B4 escapes only because W3.2, the later of its two items, changes push-main (section 11).

**What the harness does instead** (fixtured in its self-test: the CLEAN TWIN "for an item that did not change
push-main, a row on the push-main current at its landing and a base holding it is treated" and the two MUST FIRE
cases beside it). A row is treated when its `pm_blob` maps to the push-main commit current AT the landing or a later
one, and the main it gated on (`preflight_sha`, else `branch_base`) already holds the landing commit. The second
clause also keeps out an item's own attempts before it landed, which the literal rule would have counted. Mutants
dropping either clause went red in their named cases.

**First rung.** Edit the plan's section 5 bullet and W0.3 step 2's third sub-bullet to state the rule the harness
applies, so the next lane that reads the plan and not the script is not misled. Nothing else needs to move.
