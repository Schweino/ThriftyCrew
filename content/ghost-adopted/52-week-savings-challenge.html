Why this one works

Big savings goals fail because they feel impossible on day one. This challenge does the opposite. Week one asks for a single dollar, which nobody can talk themselves out of. By the time the weekly amount gets real, saving is already a habit and the momentum carries you. That is the quiet trick here. It is not about the dollar amounts, it is about showing up 52 times in a row.

Make it automatic

If checking off a box feels like one more chore, let a machine do it.
