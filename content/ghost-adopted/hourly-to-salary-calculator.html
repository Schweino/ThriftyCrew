The 2,080 hour shortcut

A standard full-time year is about 2,080 hours, which is 40 hours a week across 52 weeks. That is why a quick way to turn an hourly wage into a yearly salary is to multiply by 2,000 and round up a little. Fifteen dollars an hour is roughly $31,000 a year. Thirty dollars an hour is roughly $62,000. It is a rough shortcut, but it gets you close enough to size up an offer on the spot.

Why the weeks matter

If you are paid hourly and take unpaid time off, your real yearly pay drops, because you only earn while the clock runs. A salaried worker usually gets paid the same whether they take a vacation week or not. That is one of the quiet differences between hourly and salaried work, and it is worth doing the math on before you compare two offers that look similar on paper.
