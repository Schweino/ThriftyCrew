We've priced Thrifty Crew about as low as a membership can go. $1 a month, or $10 for a whole year. Here's how cancellations and refunds work, in plain terms.

Try it before you buy

You never have to guess whether it's for you. Several full money lessons and a budget recipe are free, with no card required. Read those first and decide at your own pace.

Cancel anytime

You're never locked in. Cancel your All Access membership yourself, anytime, in two clicks: sign in, open your Account, and choose Cancel subscription. No phone calls, no hoops. Your access continues to the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

All sales are final

Because the price is kept so low, and because you can try it free first and cancel anytime to stop any future charge, we keep things simple: all payments are final and non-refundable. Subscribing gives you immediate access to the full library, so a charge isn't reversed after the fact. (If the law where you live requires otherwise, we'll of course honor it.)

Something went wrong?

If there was a genuine mistake, a double charge, a technical glitch, anything that doesn't look right, email contact@simplemoneyplaybook.com and we'll make it right.
