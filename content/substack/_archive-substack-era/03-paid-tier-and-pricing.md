# Simple Money Playbook — Paid Tier & Pricing Setup

Substack → Settings → Payments (turn on paid subscriptions, connect Stripe first).

---

## Recommended pricing
- **Monthly: $1/month**  ← the "a dollar is nothing" anchor
- **Annual: $10/year**  ← two months free; this is the churn-killer — push it
- **Founding member (optional): $75+**  ← let early believers pay more to support the launch

> Why annual matters: an annual subscriber can't churn for 12 months. Every annual sale you make
> is a year of locked-in revenue. Make the annual option visible and frame the savings.

## "Why subscribe" / paywall pitch (paste into the subscribe page / paywall copy)

> **Give your teen the head start no one gave us — for a dollar a month.**
>
> Free subscribers get occasional lessons. **Paid members get the full weekly program:**
>
> - ✅ A new practical lesson **every single week** (52 a year)
> - ✅ A **"Try this together" activity** with each one — ready to use that week
> - ✅ **Conversation starters** for you and your teen
> - ✅ Full access to the **growing library** of every past lesson
> - ✅ The complete year-long roadmap: money basics → work & reputation → growing money &
>   avoiding debt traps → character & planning the road ahead
>
> All for **$1/month — or $10/year** (two months free).
>
> A capable, money-smart kid isn't built by one big talk. It's built by small, steady moments,
> week after week. This is the easiest way to make those moments happen.
>
> **Start today. Your kid's "future self" will thank you.**
