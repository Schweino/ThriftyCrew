# Simple Money Playbook — Launch & Publishing Plan

How to turn the 52 finished lessons into a live, low-churn membership on Substack.

---

## The core strategy: build a "vault," then drip
A new subscriber who sees an empty publication leaves. A new subscriber who sees **15+ lessons
already waiting** feels they got $50 of value for $5 — and *that* is what stops week-one churn
(where most cancellations happen).

So we don't publish week-by-week from zero. We **pre-load a vault**, then auto-drip new lessons.

## Step-by-step launch

**1. Set up the shell (copy from files 01–04)**
- Publication name, descriptions, branding → `01-publication-setup.md`
- About page → `02-about-page.md`
- Connect Stripe, turn on paid tier ($5/mo + $40/yr) → `03-paid-tier-and-pricing.md`
- Welcome email → `04-welcome-email.md`

**2. Pre-load the vault (do this BEFORE telling anyone)**
- Publish **Weeks 1–13 (all of Quarter 1)** as posts, set to **paid-subscriber-only**.
- Backdate or just publish quickly in sequence so the archive looks full and rich.
- Make **Week 1 free/public** as a permanent sample so visitors can taste the quality before paying.
- Optionally also make one strong later lesson (e.g. Week 27 "Time Is Your Superpower") free — it's
  a great hook.

**3. Set the weekly cadence**
- Going forward, publish **one new lesson per week** (Week 14, 15, 16…). Substack lets you schedule
  posts in advance — load several at once so it runs hands-off.
- Pick a consistent day (e.g. Sunday evening or Monday morning — parents plan the week then).

**4. Launch to your first people**
- Announce to anyone who bought the book, plus friends/family, email list, etc.
- Offer a **founding-member** option for early supporters.

## After launch: the near-passive engine
- All 52 lessons are written, so for the first year you're mostly **scheduling**, not writing.
- **Once a year**, spend ~a week refreshing the seasonal lessons (jobs, back-to-school, etc.) — then
  the same calendar recycles. This is what makes it close to passive.

## Anti-churn checklist (the part that actually matters)
- ☑ Vault pre-loaded (instant value on day one)
- ☑ Annual plan ($40/yr) offered up front (locks in 12 months)
- ☑ One free sample lesson always visible
- ☑ Welcome email sets the "one a week, don't binge" expectation
- ☑ Consistent publish day (builds a habit)
