**TITLE:**

Chicken Marinara Pasta

**SUBTITLE:**

A hearty, pizza-inspired high-protein batch — about two weeks of dinners from one cook session.

**BODY:**

**Makes 14 servings · ~651 cal · 70g protein · 55g carbs · 18g fat · ~$2.85 per serving.**

## Ingredients

- **Chicken breast** — 2,267 g (~5 lb)
- **Penne pasta (Barilla Protein)** — 784 g (2 boxes)
- **Marinara sauce (Great Value)** — 2,608 g (~92 oz, about four 24-oz jars)
- **Cottage cheese (fat-free)** — 226 g (~8 oz, about 1 cup)
- **Turkey pepperoni (Great Value)** — 426 g (~15 oz)
- **Mozzarella, reduced-fat shredded (Great Value)** — 453 g (~16 oz / 1 lb)

## Estimated Cost

*Rough estimate only — prices swing by region and by what's on sale.*

- Chicken breast (~5 lb): ~$15
- Marinara sauce (4 jars): ~$6
- Turkey pepperoni (~15 oz): ~$9
- Penne pasta (2 boxes): ~$5
- Mozzarella (~16 oz): ~$4
- Cottage cheese (~⅓ of a $3, 24-oz tub): ~$1
- **Batch total: ~$40 at 14 servings → about $2.85 per serving**

*These are generally non-sale prices — you can go even cheaper by catching chicken on sale and using store-brand for everything else.*

That's roughly **$2.85 a meal** — versus the **$25** we'd drop on a single takeout dinner.

## Shop Smart

- **Buy chicken on sale and freeze it.** Same play as always — grab bulk family packs when it's marked down.
- **Store-brand marinara is the move.** Great Value costs a fraction of the name brands — season it up if it needs a lift.
- **Watch for turkey pepperoni deals.** It's the priciest add-on here, so stock up when it's on sale (it keeps a while).
- **Buy cheese in bulk and compare price per ounce.** Costco/Sam's usually wins on mozzarella and cottage cheese; scan your local ads too.

## Make it

1. **Weigh your empty pot first** and write down the number — you'll need it to portion at the end.
2. Boil the penne until al dente; drain and set aside.
3. Cube and cook the chicken breast. Season to your liking — salt, pepper, garlic, Italian seasoning.
4. **Make the sauce:** blend the cottage cheese into the marinara until smooth, then warm it through.
5. Combine the chicken, sauce, and penne in the pot and stir to coat.
6. Stir in the shredded mozzarella and turkey pepperoni until the cheese melts through. (No broiling needed.)

## Portion it

Set the full pot on your large scale, subtract the empty-pot weight you noted in Step 1, and divide by 14. Scoop that amount into each glass container — every meal lands right at ~651 cal / 70g protein.
