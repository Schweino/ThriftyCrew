Daily Fareway price check for the Thrifty Crew Omaha grocery board (www.thriftycrew.com/omaha-grocery-prices). Fareway is a BROWSER store (shop.fareway.com storefront, Instacart platform - no headless API) with a weekly ad (Sun-Sat) + a monthly ad. When its ad drops off, the new pricing can only be fetched with a browser; the headless daily job can't see it, and the weekly Wednesday agent alone would leave a Fareway sale that ends mid-week (e.g. the Sunday weekly-ad flip) stale until Wednesday. This tiny agent re-fetches Fareway same-day on the days its prices are scheduled to move. All scripts + data live in C:\Codex\ThriftyCrew\grocery\ .

STEP 0 - GUARD (do this FIRST, before opening a browser): run
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File C:\Codex\ThriftyCrew\grocery\fareway-daily-due.ps1
It is WINDOW-AWARE (reads ad-schedule.json + sale-windows.json). Act on the FIRST token:
  - FRESH  - Fareway already refreshed today (weekly agent, a prior run, or manual). Report "Fareway already fresh, nothing to do" and STOP.
  - IDLE   - no Fareway ad flip and no Fareway sale starts/reverts today. Report "Nothing due today, skipping" and STOP. This is the normal, expected result on most days - doing nothing is CORRECT, not a failure.
  - DUE    - a Fareway sale boundary is due today (a sale reverts or a new sale starts). Proceed with the storefront pull (STEP A).
  - DUE ... ADFLIP - the weekly (or monthly) ad rolled today; ALSO pull the ad images (STEP B). A plain DUE (no ADFLIP) means skip STEP B.
WHY event-driven: Fareway is re-fetched exactly on the days its prices are scheduled to change, not blindly every day. Accepted gap: a brand-new UNADVERTISED Fareway flash sale (no date in any feed) is only caught by the weekly Wednesday pull. If the logs are missing the guard fails SAFE (DUE).
