## FLAGSHIP POST (ready to publish) — "The Debt-Payoff Meal Plan"

**Slug:** `debt-payoff-meal-plan`
**Target query space:** "how to pay off debt on a budget," "cheap meals to save money," "meal prep to pay off debt" — food+finance compound, near-zero competition.
**Meta title:** The Debt-Payoff Meal Plan: How a Week of Cheap Dinners Frees Real Money
**Meta description:** You can't out-budget a takeout habit. Here's a week of meal-prep dinners at ~$2–3 a plate, and exactly how much it frees up to throw at debt.
**Tags:** Meal Prep, Financial Lessons (so it shows in both sections)

**BODY:**

You can't out-budget a takeout habit.

I've watched people track every receipt, cut every subscription, and still stay stuck — because the biggest leak in a normal week isn't the streaming service. It's the four or five times "I'll just grab something" turns into $15. Do that Monday through Friday and it's roughly **$300 a month walking out the door**, one harmless choice at a time.

Here's the good news: this is the single easiest place to widen your gap. Not by eating worse — by cooking once and eating all week. Let me show you the exact math.

### The swap

A takeout dinner runs $12–15. A meal-prep dinner from the recipes here runs about **$2–3 a plate.** Same full stomach. The difference is money you didn't know you had.

Here's a week of dinners built entirely from our recipes — cook two or three batches on Sunday, eat all week:

- **[Chicken Alfredo](https://map-to-success.ghost.io/free-chicken-alfredo/)** — 14 servings, about **$2.40** each
- **[Beef Protein Pasta](https://map-to-success.ghost.io/meal-prep/)** — 14 servings, about **$3.55** each
- **[Chicken Marinara Pasta](https://map-to-success.ghost.io/meal-prep/)** — 14 servings, about **$2.85** each

Three batches is 42 servings — more than a week of dinners for two people, with lunches left over. Total cost lands around **$130 for all of it.**

### The number that matters

Say you were doing takeout five nights a week. That's roughly **$300/month.** Switch those dinners to meal prep at ~$3 a plate and you're spending closer to **$90/month** on the same meals.

That's **~$210 a month freed up.** Not by earning a dollar more. Not by feeling deprived. Just by cooking on Sunday.

Now point that $210 at your smallest debt. On a $1,000 balance, that's gone in about five months instead of dragging on for two years of minimum payments. Same life, same fridge, wildly different trajectory — because you moved money from a habit that gave you nothing to a goal that gives you everything.

### Why this works when budgets fail

Budgets fail because they rely on willpower at the worst possible moment — 6pm, tired, hungry, nothing thawed. Meal prep removes the decision. Dinner already exists. The expensive choice isn't even on the table.

That's the whole philosophy of this site in one plate: **the small, smart choice beats the expensive default.** You're not trying to be perfect. You're trying to make the good option the easy one, and then let it stack.

### Start this Sunday

Pick one recipe. Cook one batch. Notice what you *didn't* spend this week — then send that number to your debt before it can disappear somewhere else.

**→ Start with the free [Chicken Alfredo](https://map-to-success.ghost.io/free-chicken-alfredo/).** Fourteen dinners, about $2.40 each. Your first payment to Future You.
