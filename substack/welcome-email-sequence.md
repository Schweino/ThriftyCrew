# Simple Money Playbook — 5-Email Welcome Sequence

*Drafted for the Ghost migration. Replaces the single welcome email with a 5-step onboarding drip — the highest-leverage retention lever for a $1 volume play (the first days after signup decide whether someone stays).*

**How to use these:**
- **Email 1** is the instant welcome (Ghost → Settings → Emails → Welcome email, or the first step of an automation).
- **Emails 2–5** are a timed drip. Ghost's native automations (beta) can send these; otherwise a lightweight tool (MailerLite / Buttondown / ConvertKit) triggered on new-member signup works.
- Suggested cadence: **Day 0, Day 2, Day 4, Day 7, Day 12.** Adjust to taste.
- Links point to the live site at `www.simplemoneyplaybook.com` (every link verified to resolve, 2026-07-01).
- Voice: warm, plain, been-there. Audience-agnostic (works whether the reader is doing this for themselves or a young person). Keep them short and scannable.

## Email 3 — Day 4 — Your first money win

**Subject line options:**
- The one number that beats your income
- A money win you can get in 10 minutes
- Forget your salary — watch this instead

**Body:**

Quick one today — an idea that changed everything for me, and a small thing you can do with it in ten minutes.

Most people think the key to money is earning more. It isn't. The number that actually decides your future is **the gap** — what comes in, minus what goes out. You can earn a fortune and keep none of it, or earn modestly and quietly build real freedom. The gap is the score.

Here's the ten-minute version: open your bank or card app and find **one** recurring charge you forgot you had — a subscription, an app, a "free trial" that started billing. Cancel it, or move that same amount into savings automatically. That's it. You just widened your gap, on autopilot, forever.

Small choice, made once, stacked over years. That's the whole game.

Want to see how far a small, early habit can go? This one's free:

**→ [Time Is Your Superpower](https://www.simplemoneyplaybook.com/week-27-time-is-your-superpower/)** — why *when* you start beats how much you start with.

— Brad

*P.S. Nothing here is financial advice — it's a starting point for thinking things through. For real money decisions, check with a qualified professional.*

## Notes (for Brad, not the emails)

- **Sequence logic:** welcome → *who I am* (trust) → *a real money win* (value) → *a real savings win that pays for membership* (value + sets up the ask) → *soft upgrade*. Every email gives something before it asks for anything.
- **The upgrade email (5) leans on the meal-prep ROI**, not guilt or urgency — matches the site's value-forward positioning ("you get real value now, not later").
- **Founding-member angle:** if/when you run a founding-member window, add a line to Email 5 ("the first 500 lock in $10/year for life") — that's the one high-attention moment worth the urgency.
- **Trim freely.** Shorter almost always wins in email. If any feels long, cut the P.S. first.
- Keep the not-financial-advice note on any email that touches saving/investing (Emails 3 and 5).
