# Simple Money Playbook — Content Streams & Weekly Rhythm

Three recurring series under one brand: "practical, everyday steps to a money-smart family."

---

## The three streams
1. **The Weekly Lesson (backbone)** — the 52-week teen money/success program. *Done & formatted.*
2. **Money-Saving Meal Prep** — cheap, doable meal ideas/plans that shrink the grocery bill.
3. **Budgeting Tips** — short, practical money-management wins for the whole household.

All three serve the same goal: help families **widen "the gap"** (keep more of what they earn).
Meal prep + budgeting are the household money-savers; the teen program is the long-game.

## Recommended weekly rhythm (3 posts/week)
| Day | Post | Audience |
|-----|------|----------|
| **Monday** | Weekly Lesson (teen money/success) | Paid (Weeks 1 & 27 free) |
| **Wednesday** | Money-Saving Meal Prep | Mostly paid; ~1/month free |
| **Friday** | Budgeting Tip | Mostly paid; ~1/month free |

*Lighter option:* if 3/week feels like too much, run ONE mid-week post that alternates meal prep
and budgeting week to week (2 posts/week total). Start heavier, dial back if needed — easy to do.

## Free vs paid cadence ("mostly paid, some free")
- **Free hooks:** ~1 free meal-prep AND ~1 free budgeting post per month, released publicly to pull
  in new subscribers (shareable, low-commitment, high appeal). Plus the 2 free teen lessons (Wk 1, 27).
- **Everything else:** paid / member-only — the bulk of the value lives behind the $5 paywall.
- Free posts double as your social/share content (a free meal-prep idea is the most shareable thing
  you'll publish).

## Keeping it near-passive (the key to your goal)
Meal prep and budgeting are **even more evergreen and batchable** than the teen lessons:
- **Batch a library** of each up front (write 12–24 at a time), then **schedule them to drip**.
- They **recycle seasonally** — meal prep especially (summer grilling, back-to-school lunches,
  holiday-on-a-budget, slow-cooker winter, fresh-and-cheap spring produce). Write once, reuse yearly.
- Once the libraries are loaded and scheduled, the whole publication runs ~hands-off, with a yearly
  refresh week.

## Suggested first build (to seed the vault before launch)
- Teen lessons: **Weeks 1–13 already formatted** ✓
- Meal prep: a starter batch of **~12** (covers 3 months of Wednesdays), 1–2 released free
- Budgeting: a starter batch of **~12** (covers 3 months of Fridays), 1–2 released free

That gives a launch-day archive of ~25–35 posts across three streams — instant "this is worth way
more than $5" value, which is the #1 defense against early churn.
