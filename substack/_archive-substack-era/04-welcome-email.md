# Simple Money Playbook — Welcome Email

Substack → Settings → Emails → Welcome email (auto-sends to every new subscriber).
Warm, non-presumptuous, with a clear clickable call to action.

---

## Welcome email (primary — all new subscribers)

**Subject line options (pick one):**
- Welcome — start here (it's free)
- You're in. Here's where to begin.
- Welcome to Simple Money Playbook

---

Hi, and welcome — I'm really glad you're here.

Simple Money Playbook is here to make money and success feel simpler and more doable, a little at a time —
whether you're sharpening your own game or helping someone younger get a head start.

The whole idea is simple: skip the big lectures. Each week is one short, practical lesson — a clear
idea, a simple "try this," and a couple of questions worth sitting with. A few minutes, whenever it
suits you.

The best way to see what it's about is to read the very first lesson — it's free:

**→ [Read Week 1: Future You Is a Real Person](https://maptosuccess.substack.com/p/week-1-future-you-is-a-real-person)**

Want the full picture of everything inside? Have a look around here:

**→ [Explore Simple Money Playbook](https://maptosuccess.substack.com)**

It's all self-paced, so start whenever you like — there's no catching up and nothing to fall behind
on.

One small favor: just hit reply and tell me what brought you here — getting your own money on track,
helping a kid or someone younger, or both. I read every message, and it genuinely helps me make this
better for you.

Thanks for being here. Let's build something good.

— Brad
*Simple Money Playbook — The Family Money Playbook*

*P.S. When a lesson touches on saving or investing, it's a starting point for thinking things
through — not financial advice. For real money decisions, check with a qualified professional.*

---

## Notes (for you, not the email)
- **Two clear, clickable links** — the primary one points straight to free Week 1 so a brand-new
  subscriber knows exactly what to click. (Tip: in the Substack welcome-email editor you can also
  turn that line into a big orange Button via the toolbar if you want it even more obvious.)
- **Opening reframed** — leads by affirming the reader's intent ("sharpening your own game / helping
  someone younger"), not implying they don't know money. No more "nobody gets taught this."
- Keep it short and scannable. Resist adding more.
