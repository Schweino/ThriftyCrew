## The money lessons nobody taught us

Most of us walked into adult life never having been taught how money actually works. How to budget a
paycheck. Why a credit card can quietly cost you thousands. How starting to save at 20 instead of 40
can mean retiring years earlier. How to build a reputation that opens doors before you even walk into
the room.

These aren't advanced skills. They're the basics of a good life. And most of us had to learn them the
hard, expensive way — by making the mistakes ourselves.

**Simple Money Playbook is the guide I wish someone had handed me.** It takes the lessons about money, work,
and character that usually cost years and real money to learn, and breaks them into short, practical
weekly pieces anyone can use.

## It works on two levels

- **For you.** Every idea here applies at any age — keep more than you spend, let time do the heavy
  lifting, build a reputation that opens doors, want less and win more. It is never too late to get
  money-smart, and you can use every one of these lessons on your own life.
- **For the young people in your life.** Got kids, a niece or nephew, a younger sibling, a student,
  someone you mentor? Each lesson also hands you a simple way to pass it on — so they get the head
  start most of us never did.

## What you get

A growing library you can work through one week at a time. Each lesson includes:

- **A clear, plain-English idea** you can actually use (no finance background required)
- **A "try this" activity** — do it for yourself, or with the young person in your life
- **Questions worth sitting with** — to spark a real conversation, or some honest reflection

No lectures. No overwhelm. No 400-page book to grind through. Just a few minutes a week that quietly
add up — because the small stuff, repeated, is exactly how a money-smart life gets built.

There's also a **Meal Prep** section with money-saving ideas for any household — practical wins that
help you keep more of what you earn. (More there soon.)

## Try it free

Two full lessons are open to everyone — start with the pinned **Week 1: "Future You Is a Real

## Why it's so affordable

Because this should be a no-brainer. We made it a dollar a month on purpose — less than a single
coffee, once a month — so getting money-smart (and helping the people you care about do the same) is
the easiest "yes" you'll make all year.

## Where this comes from

Simple Money Playbook grew out of a book about climbing from the bottom to a life worth being proud of — and
the hard-won lessons about money, work, and character that made the climb possible. This is that
wisdom, broken into bite-sized weekly pieces you can actually put to use.

*An honest note: some lessons touch on saving and investing. They're starting points for thinking
things through — not financial advice. For real money decisions, check with a qualified
professional.*
